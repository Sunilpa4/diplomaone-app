PREREQUISITE
Function, Logarithm, Determinant, Trigonometry, Limit, Factorization, Polynomial,
Quadratic Equation, Coordinate Geometry, LCM, GCD, Concept of Set.

COURSE OUTCOMES
CO1 Demonstrate the ability to crack engineering related problems based on Matrices.          A
CO2 Demonstrate the ability to solve engineering related problems based on applications of
    differentiation.                                                                          A
CO3 Demonstrate the ability to solve engineering related problems based on applications of
    integration.                                                                              A
CO4 Develop the ability to apply differential equations to significant applied problems.      A
CO5 Represent complex numbers algebraically and geometrically for solving engineering
    related problems.                                                                         A

COURSE CONTENT
Unit 1  Matrices                                                          10 hours  23 %
  1.1 Concept of Matrix
  1.2 Types of Matrices
  1.3 Addition, Subtraction and multiplication by scalar of matrices
  1.4 Product of two matrices
  1.5 Adjoint and Inverse of a matrix of order 2X2 and 3X3
  1.6 Solution of Simultaneous linear equations of two variables

Unit 2  Differentiation and its Applications                                11 hours  23 %
  2.1 Concept and Definition of Differentiation
  2.2 Working rules: Sum, Product, Division
  2.3 Chain Rule
  2.4 Derivative of Implicit functions
  2.5 Derivative of Parametric functions
  2.6 Logarithmic Differentiation
  2.7 Successive Differentiation up to second order
  2.8 Applications: Velocity, Acceleration, Maxima & Minima of given simple functions

Unit 3  Integration and its Applications                                    10 hours  20 %
  3.1 Concept and Definition of Integration
  3.2 Working rules and Integral of standard functions
  3.3 Method of substitution
  3.4 Integration by parts
  3.5 Definite Integral and its properties
  3.6 Applications: Area and volume (simple problems)

Unit 4  Differential Equations                                              7 hours  17 %
  4.1 Concept and Definition, Order and Degree of differential equation
  4.2 Solution of DE of first degree and first order by Variable Separable method
  4.3 Solution of linear Differential equation

Unit 5  Complex Numbers                                                    7 hours  17 %
  5.1 Concept of Complex number
  5.2 Algebra of Complex numbers
  5.3 Conjugate, Modulus and inverse of Complex numbers
  5.4 Argument and Polar form of a Complex number
  5.5 De Moivre's Theorem and related simple examples
  5.6 Square root of a Complex number and cube root of unity

Total 45 hours, 100 %
