Rationale:
Engineering Drawing is language of engineers. It is the foundation block which strengthens the
engineering structure. Moreover, it is the transmitting link between ideas and realization.

Course Outcome:
After Completion of the Course, Student will able to:
01 Apply the conventions and methods of engineering drawing.                        Application
02 Construct engineering curves as per given dimensions.                            Remember
03 Draw the projection of points, lines and planes under different conditions.      Analyze
04 Draw orthographic projection from isometric views of simple objects.             Application
05 Draw isometric views from orthographic projection of simple objects.             Application

Course Content:
Unit 1  Introduction to Engineering Graphics                                   04 hours  10 %
  1. Introduction to drawing instruments and accessories.
  2. Types of lines, dimensioning and plane scale as per BIS-SP 46.
  3. Draw polygons with various methods.
     (In Exam, specific method not to be asked. Students can draw polygon by any method.)

Unit 2  Engineering Curves                                                     05 hours  20 %
  1. Applications of Engineering Curves, Construction of Conical curves.
  2. Drawing of cycloidal Curves, Involutes and Spirals.
     (In Exam, specific method not to be asked. Students can draw these curves by any method.)

Unit 3  Projections of Points and Lines                                        05 hours  20 %
  1. Introduction to principal planes of projections, Projections of points.
  2. Projections of line with its inclination to HP and VP, True length.

Unit 4  Projections of Planes                                                   04 hours  10 %
  1. Inclination of square, hexagonal and circular plane with reference planes.

Unit 5  Orthographic Projections                                               06 hours  20 %
  1. Projections from the pictorial view of the object.
  2. Draw front, top and side views of simple objects.
  3. Free hand sketches of simple geometrical shapes like square, triangle, circle,
     semi-circle, hexagon and pentagon.

Unit 6  Isometric view                                                         06 hours  20 %
  1. Isometric view of simple objects and Isometric scale.
  2. Free hand sketches of simple objects like prism, pyramid, cylinder and cone.

Total 30 hours | 100 %

Suggested Course Practical List:
  1. Use of drawing instruments (Types of lines, dimensioning, scale as per BIS, Angles, polygons)
  2. Drawing of Conical curves (Ellipse, parabola, hyperbola)
  3. Drawing of spiral, involute and cycloidal curves
  4. Projections of points and line
  5. Projections of plane
  6. Draw orthographic projections of given isometric view
  7. Draw Isometric view of given orthographic projection

All the problems must be drawn in sketch book and then in drawing sheet of A2 size. Draw both
sides, if needed.

List of Laboratory/Learning Resources Required:
Drawing paper, scale, 0.5 mm H and 2H lead pencil, angle protector, set square, etc.

Suggested Project List:

Suggested Activities for Students:
