Q1(b) Explain any two annealing processes briefly.કોઈપણ બે annealing પ્રક્રિયા ટૂંકમાં સમજાવો. 4 marks4 ગુણ ⌄

Model answer · 4 marksનમૂનાનો જવાબ · 4 ગુણ

1. Full annealing

  1. Heat steel into the correct austenitising range: usually about 30–50 °C above A₃ for hypoeutectoid steel; for hypereutectoid steel, just above A₁ but below Acm to avoid a continuous cementite network.
  2. Soak so the section reaches a uniform temperature, then cool very slowly inside the furnace.

This produces a relatively soft ferrite–pearlite structure, relieves stress and improves machinability and ductility.

2. Process (subcritical) annealing

Heat cold-worked low-carbon steel below A₁, hold, then cool. It does not fully form austenite; it allows recovery/recrystallisation, removes much of the work-hardening and restores ductility between drawing or rolling operations.

1. Full annealing

  1. Steel ને યોગ્ય austenitising range સુધી ગરમ કરો: hypoeutectoid steel માટે સામાન્ય રીતે A₃ થી આશરે 30–50 °C ઉપર; hypereutectoid steel માટે continuous cementite network ટાળવા A₁ થી થોડું ઉપર, પણ Acm થી નીચે.
  2. ભાગનું temperature સરખું થાય ત્યાં સુધી hold કરો અને પછી furnace ની અંદર ધીમે ઠંડો કરો.

આથી ferrite–pearlite structure નરમ બને છે, internal stress ઘટે છે અને machinability તથા ductility સુધરે છે.

2. Process (subcritical) annealing

Cold-worked low-carbon steel ને A₁ થી નીચે ગરમ કરી hold કરો, પછી ઠંડો કરો. તેમાં સંપૂર્ણ austenite બનતું નથી; recovery/recrystallisation થાય છે અને drawing કે rolling વચ્ચે work-hardening ઘટીને ductility પાછી મળે છે.

Q1(c) Classify metals and explain the flow diagram for production of iron and steel.ધાતુઓનું વર્ગીકરણ કરો અને iron તથા steel બનાવવાનો flow diagram સમજાવો. 7 marks7 ગુણ ⌄

Model answer · 7 marksનમૂનાનો જવાબ · 7 ગુણ

Classification

  • Ferrous metals have iron as the main base: steels and cast irons.
  • Non-ferrous metals are not iron-based: copper, aluminium, magnesium, zinc, nickel and their alloys; selected for conductivity, low mass or corrosion resistance.

Iron-to-steel production

  1. Prepare the charge: iron ore is sized/sintered; coke supplies heat and carbon monoxide; limestone is a flux.
  2. Blast furnace: carbon monoxide reduces iron oxides (e.g. Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂). Molten hot metal (pig iron) collects below; lime combines with gangue to form slag, which is tapped separately.
  3. Steelmaking: in the primary route, hot metal plus some scrap is refined in a basic oxygen furnace (BOF); oxygen removes excess carbon and impurities, and flux carries them into slag. Alternatively, an electric arc furnace (EAF) melts/refines mainly scrap and/or direct-reduced iron (DRI).
  4. Refine and cast: adjust carbon/alloy content, deoxidise, then continuously cast steel into slabs, blooms or billets.
  5. Shape: roll or form the cast semi-finished products into plate, sheet, bar, rail and sections.

વર્ગીકરણ

  • Ferrous metals માં મુખ્ય ધાતુ iron હોય છે: steel અને cast iron.
  • Non-ferrous metals iron આધારિત નથી: copper, aluminium, magnesium, zinc, nickel અને તેમના alloys; conductivity, ઓછું વજન અથવા corrosion resistance માટે પસંદ થાય.

Iron માંથી steel બનાવવાની પ્રક્રિયા

  1. Charge તૈયાર કરો: iron ore ને યોગ્ય કદનું/sinter કરો; coke ગરમી અને carbon monoxide આપે; limestone flux છે.
  2. Blast furnace: carbon monoxide, iron oxide માંથી iron છૂટું કરે (જેમ Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂). નીચે hot metal (pig iron) ભેગું થાય; lime અશુદ્ધિ સાથે મળી slag બનાવે, જેને અલગ કાઢે છે.
  3. Steelmaking: મુખ્ય route માં hot metal સાથે થોડો scrap, basic oxygen furnace (BOF) માં refine થાય; oxygen વધારાનું carbon અને impurities દૂર કરે, flux તેને slag માં લઈ જાય. બીજા route માં electric arc furnace (EAF) મુખ્યત્વે scrap અને/અથવા direct-reduced iron (DRI) ઓગાળી/refine કરે છે.
  4. Refine અને cast: carbon/alloy ની માત્રા ગોઠવી, deoxidise કરી, steel ને સતત casting થી slabs, blooms અથવા billets માં ઢાળો.
  5. આકાર આપો: ઢાળેલા અર્ધતૈયાર ભાગોને roll/form કરીને plate, sheet, bar, rail અને sections બનાવો.

A covalent bond forms when neighbouring atoms share one or more pairs of valence electrons to obtain a more stable outer shell. It is usually directional.

  • Strong covalent networks give high hardness and high melting temperature (diamond, SiC).

Q2(c) Discuss the importance of the iron–carbon diagram with a neat sketch. Also state the effect of carbon content on the hardness of iron and steel.Iron–carbon diagram નું મહત્વ neat sketch સાથે સમજાવો. Carbon content થી iron અને steel ની hardness પર થતી અસર પણ લખો. 7 marks7 ગુણ ⌄

Model answer · 7 marksનમૂનાનો જવાબ · 7 ગુણ

The Fe–Fe₃C (iron–cementite) equilibrium diagram maps phases against temperature and carbon content for slow heating/cooling. It identifies the steel range (up to about 2.14% C) and cast-iron range (to 6.67% C), phase fields such as ferrite (α), austenite (γ), liquid and cementite (Fe₃C), and the temperatures at which transformations occur. The diagram is schematic and its carbon axis is compressed.

  • Eutectoid: about 0.76–0.80% C at 727 °C; γ transforms to α + Fe₃C, a fine mixture called pearlite.
  • Eutectic: about 4.3% C at 1147 °C; liquid transforms to austenite + cementite (ledeburite on solidification).
  • Critical lines: A₁ is the eutectoid temperature; A₃ bounds the γ and α + γ fields in hypoeutectoid steel; Acm bounds γ and γ + Fe₃C in hypereutectoid steel.

Importance: use the diagram to predict equilibrium microstructures from composition and temperature, identify critical heating/cooling ranges, select annealing/normalizing/hardening temperatures, and understand steel/cast-iron transformations.

Effect of carbon on hardness

Within plain-carbon steel, increasing carbon generally increases pearlite/cementite, hardness and strength, but reduces ductility, toughness and weldability. Low-carbon, ferrite-rich steel is softer and more formable; higher-carbon steel is harder but less tough. This simple trend should not be extended uncritically through the cast-iron range.

Fe–Fe₃C (iron–cementite) equilibrium diagram ધીમે ગરમ/ઠંડા કરતાં composition અને temperature પ્રમાણે કયો phase બને તે બતાવે છે. તેમાં steel range (આશરે 2.14% C સુધી), cast-iron range (6.67% C સુધી), ferrite (α), austenite (γ), liquid અને cementite (Fe₃C) ના ક્ષેત્ર તથા transformation temperatures દેખાય છે. આકૃતિ schematic છે અને carbon axis સંકુચિત છે.

  • Eutectoid: આશરે 0.76–0.80% C અને 727 °C; γ બદલાઈ α + Fe₃C બને છે. આ બારીક મિશ્રણને pearlite કહે છે.
  • Eutectic: આશરે 4.3% C અને 1147 °C; liquid માંથી austenite + cementite બને છે (solidification પછીનું મિશ્રણ ledeburite કહેવાય).
  • Critical lines: A₁ eutectoid temperature છે; hypoeutectoid steel માં A₃, γ અને α + γ ક્ષેત્રની સીમા છે; hypereutectoid steel માં Acm, γ અને γ + Fe₃C ક્ષેત્રની સીમા છે.

મહત્વ: composition અને temperature થી equilibrium microstructure સમજવા, critical heating/cooling temperature નક્કી કરવા, annealing/normalizing/hardening માટે temperature પસંદ કરવા અને steel/cast-iron transformation સમજવા diagram ઉપયોગી છે.

Carbon થી hardness પર અસર

Plain-carbon steel માં carbon વધે ત્યારે સામાન્ય રીતે pearlite/cementite, hardness અને strength વધે; ductility, toughness અને weldability ઘટે. ઓછા carbon વાળું ferrite-rich steel નરમ અને વધુ formable હોય; વધુ carbon વાળું steel કઠિન પણ ઓછું tough બને. આ સરળ trend ને cast-iron range માં સીધો લાગુ ન કરવો.

Q2(a) Explain ionic (electrovalent) bond and state its properties.Ionic (electrovalent) bond સમજાવો અને તેના ગુણધર્મો લખો. 3 marks3 ગુણ ⌄

Model answer · 3 marksનમૂનાનો જવાબ · 3 ગુણ

An ionic (electrovalent) bond forms when an electropositive atom transfers electron(s) to an electronegative atom. The resulting positive and negative ions attract each other electrostatically (for example, Na⁺ and Cl⁻ in NaCl).

  • Ionic solids are hard, brittle and usually have high melting points.
  • They do not conduct electricity as solids, because the ions cannot move; they conduct when molten or dissolved in water.
  • Many dissolve in polar solvents such as water.

Ionic (electrovalent) bond માં electropositive atom electron આપે છે અને electronegative atom તે મેળવે છે. બનેલા positive અને negative ions વચ્ચે electrostatic આકર્ષણ રહે છે (જેમ NaCl માં Na⁺ અને Cl⁻).

  • Ionic solids સામાન્ય રીતે hard, brittle અને ઊંચા melting point વાળા હોય છે.
  • Solid અવસ્થામાં ions ખસી શકતા નથી એટલે વીજળી વહન કરતા નથી; molten અથવા પાણીમાં dissolved હોય ત્યારે conduct કરે છે.

  1. Use steel with enough carbon (commonly medium-carbon steel); clean the surface.

  3. Immediately spray water or another suitable quenchant. The heated surface forms hard martensite; the core remains tougher.

Pack carburizing

  1. Pack low-carbon steel parts in a sealed box with a carbon-rich solid pack (often charcoal plus an activator).
  2. Heat and soak, commonly around 900–950 °C, so carbon diffuses into the surface.
  3. Quench (then temper as required). The carbon-enriched case becomes hard; the low-carbon core remains tough.

Q3(a) Explain Hume–Rothery’s factors for formation of a solid solution.Solid solution બનવા માટેના Hume–Rothery factors સમજાવો. 3 marks3 ગુણ ⌄

Model answer · 3 marksનમૂનાનો જવાબ · 3 ગુણ

For extensive substitutional solid solubility, Hume–Rothery’s main rules are:

  1. Atomic size: solute and solvent radii should differ by less than about 15%.
  2. Crystal structure: both metals should have the same structure.
  3. Electronegativity: values should be similar; a large difference favours compound formation.
  4. Valence: equal valence favours solubility; a lower-valence metal generally dissolves more of a higher-valence metal than the reverse.

Example: Cu–Ni satisfies these rules closely and forms a continuous substitutional solid solution.

Substitutional solid solution માં વધુ પ્રમાણમાં solubility માટે Hume–Rothery ના મુખ્ય નિયમો:

  1. Atomic size: solute અને solvent ના radius નો ફરક આશરે 15% થી ઓછો હોવો જોઈએ.
  2. Crystal structure: બંને metals ની structure સરખી હોવી જોઈએ.
  3. Electronegativity: મૂલ્ય નજીક હોય; ઘણો ફરક હોય તો solid solution બદલે compound બની શકે.
  4. Valence: valence સરખું હોય તો solubility સારી રહે; અલગ હોય તો સામાન્ય રીતે નીચી valence વાળો metal ઊંચી valence વાળાને વધુ ઓગાળે છે.

ઉદાહરણ: Cu–Ni આ નિયમો લગભગ પૂરા કરે છે અને સતત substitutional solid solution બનાવે છે.

Corrosion can be reduced by breaking the electrochemical cell or protecting the metal:

  1. Material selection: choose a corrosion-resistant alloy for the environment.
  2. Barrier coatings: paint, polymer, galvanizing or plating keep water/oxygen away.

Q4(c) Discuss the working principle and purpose of electroplating with a neat sketch.Neat sketch સાથે electroplating નો સિદ્ધાંત અને હેતુ સમજાવો. 7 marks7 ગુણ ⌄

Model answer · 7 marksનમૂનાનો જવાબ · 7 ગુણ

Purpose

Electroplating deposits a thin metal layer to improve corrosion resistance, appearance, wear resistance, solderability or dimensional repair.

Principle and working

  1. Clean and activate the workpiece. Connect it as the cathode (−); connect the plating metal as the anode (+).
  2. Immerse both in an electrolyte containing ions of the coating metal and pass direct current.
  3. At the cathode, metal ions gain electrons and deposit: Mⁿ⁺ + ne⁻ → M. The soluble anode can dissolve: M → Mⁿ⁺ + ne⁻.
  4. Rinse, dry and inspect the coating thickness and adhesion.

Example: nickel plating on a steel fitting gives a decorative, corrosion-resistant surface. The workpiece must be the cathode.

હેતુ

Electroplating થી પાતળું metal coating ચઢે છે. તે corrosion resistance, દેખાવ, wear resistance, solderability અથવા ઘસાયેલા ભાગનું માપ સુધારે છે.

સિદ્ધાંત અને કાર્ય

  1. Workpiece સાફ કરીને activate કરો. તેને cathode (−) બનાવો; ચઢાવવાના metal ને anode (+) જોડો.
  2. બંનેને coating metal ના ions ધરાવતા electrolyte માં મૂકી direct current આપો.
  3. Cathode પર metal ion electron મેળવે અને જમા થાય: Mⁿ⁺ + ne⁻ → M. ઓગળતો anode ધાતુ આપે: M → Mⁿ⁺ + ne⁻.
  4. Rinse, dry કરીને coating ની જાડાઈ અને ચોંટ તપાસો.

ઉદાહરણ: steel fitting પર nickel plating કરવાથી દેખાવ સારો અને corrosion resistance વધે છે. Workpiece cathode હોવો જરૂરી છે.

Q4(c) Describe surface-coating techniques used in engineering. Briefly explain (i) Varnish (ii) Porcelain enamel.Engineering માં વપરાતી surface-coating રીતો સમજાવો. (i) Varnish (ii) Porcelain enamel ટૂંકમાં સમજાવો. 7 marks7 ગુણ ⌄

Model answer · 7 marksનમૂનાનો જવાબ · 7 ગુણ

Surface preparation

Remove oil, dust, rust and loose scale; dry the surface and provide a suitable clean/rough profile for adhesion.

Common coating techniques

  • Organic coatings: paint and varnish by brush, roller, spray or dip; powder coating is electrostatically applied and baked.
  • Metallic coatings: hot dipping, electroplating, electroless plating and thermal spraying.
  • Conversion coatings: anodising, phosphating and chromating create a protective surface layer.
  • Diffusion / thin films: carburizing or nitriding modifies the surface; PVD/CVD deposits a thin hard film.

Varnish

A varnish is a clear or lightly tinted resin/oil film without an opaque pigment. It protects wood or metal while showing the surface beneath; it is applied in thin coats and allowed to dry/cure.

Porcelain enamel

A glass-like silicate coating is fused onto prepared metal by firing. It is smooth, hygienic, chemical- and heat-resistant but can chip under impact. Uses include cookware, sanitary ware and chemical equipment.

Model answer · 7 marksનમૂનાનો જવાબ · 7 ગુણ

Scope of solar energy in India

  • India has many sunny regions; solar can supply electricity on rooftops and in utility-scale parks.
  • It can support irrigation pumps, street lighting, telecom, schools and remote/off-grid loads; solar water heating is another direct use.
  • Rooftop and distributed systems reduce the need to transport electricity over long distances and can complement wind/hydro.
  • Output is intermittent, so storage, grid balancing, suitable land/roofs, dust cleaning and end-of-life panel recycling must be planned.

Photovoltaic (PV) cell

  1. A silicon cell has p-type and n-type regions forming a p–n junction.
  2. Incoming photons create electron–hole pairs. The junction’s built-in electric field separates them.
  3. Metal contacts collect charges; when an external circuit is connected, electrons flow through the load and the cell supplies direct current (DC).
  4. Cells are connected into modules; an inverter converts DC to AC for ordinary electrical loads or the grid.

ભારતમાં solar energy નો વ્યાપ

  • ભારતના ઘણા વિસ્તારોમાં સૂર્યપ્રકાશ સારો મળે છે; rooftop અને મોટા solar park બંનેમાં વીજળી બનાવી શકાય.
  • Irrigation pump, street light, telecom, school અને દૂરના/off-grid load માટે ઉપયોગી; solar water heating પણ સીધો ઉપયોગ છે.
  • Rooftop અને distributed system લાંબા અંતરે વીજળી પહોંચાડવાની જરૂર ઘટાડે અને wind/hydro સાથે પૂરક બને.
  • સૂર્યપ્રકાશ સતત ન હોવાથી storage અને grid balancing જોઈએ; જમીન/છત, ધૂળની સફાઈ અને જૂના panel નું recycling પણ યોજવું પડે.

Photovoltaic (PV) cell

  1. Silicon cell માં p-type અને n-type ભાગ જોડાઈ p–n junction બનાવે છે.
  2. Photon પડતાં electron–hole pair બને. Junction નું built-in electric field તેમને અલગ કરે છે.
  3. Metal contact charge ભેગો કરે; external circuit જોડતાં electrons load માંથી વહે અને cell direct current (DC) આપે.
  4. Cells ને module માં જોડાય; inverter DC ને સામાન્ય load/grid માટે AC માં ફેરવે.

Q1(a) Define: (i) Ductility (ii) Electrical conductivity (iii) Melting point.વ્યાખ્યા આપો: (i) Ductility (ii) Electrical conductivity (iii) Melting point. 3 marks3 ગુણ ⌄

Model answer · 3 marksનમૂનાનો જવાબ · 3 ગુણ

  • Ductility is the ability of a material to undergo plastic deformation in tension and be drawn into wire before fracture. It is often reported as percentage elongation.
  • Electrical conductivity is the ability to carry electric current; it is the reciprocal of resistivity, σ = 1/ρₑ, measured in S/m.
  • Melting point is the temperature at which solid and liquid phases of a material coexist in equilibrium at a stated pressure.
  • Ductility (તન્યતા) એટલે ખેંચવાના load હેઠળ તૂટ્યા પહેલાં plastic deformation સહન કરીને wire રૂપે ખેંચાવાની ક્ષમતા. ઘણી વાર percentage elongation થી બતાવાય છે.
  • Electrical conductivity એટલે વીજપ્રવાહ વહન કરવાની ક્ષમતા; તે resistivity નો ઊલટો છે: σ = 1/ρₑ, unit S/m.

Q2(c) State the objectives of the heat-treatment process.Heat-treatment પ્રક્રિયાના હેતુઓ લખો. 7 marks7 ગુણ ⌄

Model answer · 7 marksનમૂનાનો જવાબ · 7 ગુણ

Heat treatment is controlled heating, holding and cooling of a solid metal to change its microstructure and properties without changing its shape or overall composition. Its objectives include:

  1. increase hardness, strength and wear resistance (hardening);
  2. improve toughness and reduce brittleness (tempering);
  3. soften metal and improve machinability/formability (annealing);
  4. relieve residual stresses from casting, welding or machining;
  5. refine grain size and obtain a more uniform structure (normalizing);
  6. improve dimensional stability and reduce risk of cracking/distortion;
  7. make a hard wear-resistant surface while retaining a tough core (case hardening).

Heat treatment માં solid metal ને નિયંત્રિત રીતે ગરમ, hold અને ઠંડો કરીને microstructure તથા ગુણધર્મ બદલાય છે; આથી shape અથવા કુલ composition બદલાતું નથી. હેતુઓ:

  1. hardness, strength અને wear resistance વધારવી (hardening);
  2. toughness વધારી brittleness ઘટાડવી (tempering);
  3. ધાતુ નરમ કરીને machinability/formability સુધારવી (annealing);
  4. casting, welding કે machining થી આવેલા residual stress દૂર કરવું;
  5. grain fine કરી વધુ uniform structure મેળવવું (normalizing);
  6. માપ સ્થિર રાખવું અને crack/distortion નો જોખમ ઘટાડવો;
  7. અંદર tough core રાખીને બહાર hard wear-resistant surface બનાવવી (case hardening).

Q2(c) Classify heat-treatment furnaces and explain any two.Heat-treatment furnace નું વર્ગીકરણ કરી કોઈપણ બે સમજાવો. 7 marks7 ગુણ ⌄

Model answer · 7 marksનમૂનાનો જવાબ · 7 ગુણ

Classification

Heat-treatment furnaces may be batch (one load at a time) or continuous (parts move through zones); they may use electric resistance, gas/oil firing or a controlled/protective atmosphere. Examples include box, muffle, pit, salt-bath, bogie-hearth, pusher and roller-hearth furnaces.

1. Box / muffle furnace

A refractory chamber with a front door and electric elements or burners heats a batch. The muffle separates the work from combustion products. It is useful for small/medium components needing annealing, normalizing or tempering.

2. Pit furnace

A vertical, insulated pit holds parts suspended or on a fixture. The lid closes the chamber; controlled heating and atmosphere can be used. Its depth suits long shafts, rods and gears, including stress relieving and case-hardening cycles.

Q5(b) Explain electric vehicle.Electric vehicle સમજાવો. 4 marks4 ગુણ ⌄

Model answer · 4 marksનમૂનાનો જવાબ · 4 ગુણ

An electric vehicle (EV) is propelled wholly or partly by an electric motor. A battery-electric vehicle stores electricity in a rechargeable battery rather than carrying petrol/diesel for propulsion.

  1. The battery pack stores DC energy and the BMS monitors cell voltage, temperature and safety.
  2. The inverter/controller supplies controlled current to the traction motor.
  3. The motor drives the wheels through a reduction gear; regenerative braking can return some energy to the battery.
  4. The on-board charger and charge port recharge the pack; a DC–DC converter supplies the 12-V system.

EVs are quiet and have no tailpipe emissions while driving; total environmental benefit depends on electricity generation, battery manufacture and end-of-life recycling.

Electric vehicle (EV) એવું વાહન છે જે પૂરેપૂરું અથવા થોડું electric motor થી ચાલે. Battery-electric vehicle માં propulsion માટે petrol/diesel બદલે rechargeable battery માં વીજળી સંગ્રહાય.

  3. Motor reduction gear મારફતે wheels ચલાવે; regenerative braking થી થોડી energy battery માં પાછી જાય.

1. Surface preparation

Remove oil, dust, rust and scale by degreasing, scraping/wire brushing, abrasive blasting or chemical cleaning. Dry the surface and apply compatible primer promptly.

2. Coating methods

  • Brush: simple and controlled; good for small areas, corners and maintenance, but slow.
  • Roller: fast for large, flat walls and plates; less suitable for intricate shapes.

3. Varnish and finishing

Q5(c) List types of corrosion and explain them.Corrosion ના પ્રકારો લખી સમજાવો. 7 marks7 ગુણ ⌄

Model answer · 7 marksનમૂનાનો જવાબ · 7 ગુણ

Important forms of corrosion are:

  1. Uniform: fairly even loss over the exposed surface; estimate with corrosion allowance.
  2. Galvanic: dissimilar metals electrically connected in an electrolyte; the less noble metal corrodes faster.
  3. Pitting: highly local attack after a passive film breaks, often in chloride solution.
  4. Crevice: attack in stagnant, oxygen-poor liquid under gaskets, deposits or lap joints.
  5. Intergranular: corrosion follows grain boundaries, for example sensitised stainless steel.
  6. Erosion-corrosion: fast fluid removes protective film and accelerates attack.
  7. Stress-corrosion cracking: tensile stress plus a specific corrosive environment causes cracks; related forms include corrosion fatigue.

Prevention depends on the type: select material, coat/passivate, control environment, improve joint/flow design, use inhibitors or cathodic protection, and inspect.

કાટના મહત્વના પ્રકાર:

  1. Uniform: ખુલ્લી સપાટી પર લગભગ સરખો metal loss; corrosion allowance થી ધ્યાનમાં લો.
  2. Galvanic: electrolyte માં જુદી ધાતુઓ electrically જોડાય; ઓછી noble ધાતુ ઝડપથી ખવાય.
  3. Pitting: ઘણી local જગ્યાએ passive film તૂટે પછી ઊંડો ખાડો બને, chloride હોય ત્યારે સામાન્ય.
  4. Crevice: gasket, deposit અથવા lap joint નીચે oxygen ઓછું અને પ્રવાહી સ્થિર રહે ત્યાં કાટ.
  5. Intergranular: કાટ grain boundary સાથે આગળ વધે; ઉદાહરણ sensitised stainless steel.
  6. Erosion-corrosion: ઝડપી fluid protective film દૂર કરે અને કાટ વધારે.
  7. Stress-corrosion cracking: tensile stress અને ચોક્કસ corrosive વાતાવરણ સાથે crack બને; corrosion fatigue પણ સંબંધિત પ્રકાર છે.

બચાવ પ્રકાર પ્રમાણે કરો: યોગ્ય material, coating/passivation, environment control, સારી joint/flow design, inhibitor અથવા cathodic protection અને તપાસ.
