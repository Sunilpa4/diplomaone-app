Question: Q1(a) Explain primary and secondary bonds.

Primary bonds hold atoms together within a material and are generally strong:

  • Ionic/electrovalent: electrons transfer from one atom to another; opposite ions attract (e.g. NaCl). Strong, often hard/brittle and high-melting.
  • Covalent: atoms share electron pairs (e.g. diamond, silicon). Directional; can be very strong and hard.
  • Metallic: positive metal-ion cores are held by delocalised mobile electrons. Explains electrical/thermal conductivity, ductility and metallic lustre.
  • Coordinate/dative: a shared pair is donated by one atom; it is a covalent-type primary bond (e.g. complex ions).

Secondary bonds are weaker attractions between molecules/regions: van der Waals (dipole, induced dipole and dispersion/London) and hydrogen bonding (H attracted to O, N or F). They strongly affect polymer melting/softening, boiling point and adhesion. Do not confuse secondary attractions with primary bonds within a crystal.

પ્રશ્ન: Q1(a) Primary અને secondary bond સમજાવો.

Primary bond સામગ્રીના atomsને અંદરથી જોડે અને સામાન્ય રીતે મજબૂત હોય:

  • Ionic/electrovalent: એક atom electron આપે, બીજો લે; વિપરીત ion આકર્ષે (જેમ કે NaCl). મજબૂત, ઘણીવાર કઠણ/બરડ અને ઊંચું melting point.
  • Covalent: atoms electron pair વહેંચે (જેમ કે diamond, silicon). દિશાવાળો; ઘણો મજબૂત અને કઠણ હોઈ શકે.
  • Metallic: ધન metal-ion coreને delocalised, ગતિશીલ electron જોડે. Electrical/thermal conductivity, ductility અને metallic lustre સમજાવે.
  • Coordinate/dative: shared pair એક atom આપે; covalent પ્રકારનો primary bond (જેમ કે complex ion).

Secondary bond અણુ/વિસ્તાર વચ્ચેનું નબળું આકર્ષણ છે: van der Waals (dipole, induced dipole, London dispersion) અને hydrogen bond (Hનું O, N અથવા F તરફ આકર્ષણ). Polymerનું softening/melting, boiling point અને adhesion પર અસર કરે. Crystalની અંદરના primary bond સાથે ગૂંચવશો નહીં.

Question: Q1(b) Explain BCC and FCC structure with figures.

A unit cell is the smallest repeating block of a crystal lattice. Show the atom positions and count the fractional atoms belonging to one cell.

  • BCC (body-centred cubic): atoms at 8 corners and 1 body centre. Count = 8×1/8 + 1 = 2 atoms/cell. Coordination number 8; atoms touch along the body diagonal; packing factor ≈0.68. Examples: α-iron at room temperature, chromium, tungsten.
  • FCC (face-centred cubic): atoms at 8 corners and at the centre of 6 faces. Count = 8×1/8 + 6×1/2 = 4 atoms/cell. Coordination number 12; atoms touch along a face diagonal; packing factor ≈0.74. Examples: aluminium, copper, nickel and γ-iron.

For the sketch draw a cube, place corner atoms in both; add one central atom for BCC or six face-centre atoms for FCC. Remember face-centre atoms are shared by two cells.

પ્રશ્ન: Q1(b) આકૃતિ સાથે BCC અને FCC structure સમજાવો.

Unit cell crystal latticeનો નાનો પુનરાવર્તિત block છે. Atomની જગ્યા દોરી cellમાં કેટલો અંશ આવે તે ગણી બતાવો.

  • BCC (body-centred cubic): 8 corner અને 1 body-centre atom. ગણતરી = 8×1/8 + 1 = દર cell 2 atom. Coordination number 8; atom body diagonalને અડે; packing factor આશરે 0.68. ઉદાહરણ: room-temperature α-iron, chromium, tungsten.
  • FCC (face-centred cubic): 8 corner અને 6 face-centre atom. ગણતરી = 8×1/8 + 6×1/2 = દર cell 4 atom. Coordination number 12; atom face diagonalને અડે; packing factor આશરે 0.74. ઉદાહરણ: aluminium, copper, nickel, γ-iron.

Sketchમાં cube દોરી બંનેમાં corner atom; BCCમાં વચ્ચે 1 atom અથવા FCCમાં છ face-centre atom બતાવો. Face atom બે cell વચ્ચે વહેંચાય છે.

Question: Q1(c) Explain concept of cooling curve with plotting method and write sequential steps to draw equilibrium diagram with the help of them.

A cooling curve is a temperature–time plot for a molten metal/alloy cooling at a controlled rate. A change in slope or a thermal arrest indicates a phase transformation; latent heat released during solidification can temporarily slow or stop the fall in temperature.

  • Pure metal: solidifies at one equilibrium temperature; the curve shows a near-horizontal arrest at its freezing point.
  • Alloy in a solid-solution range: usually starts freezing at the liquidus and finishes at the solidus, so the curve shows a slope change/freezing interval rather than one long plateau.
  • Invariant reactions (eutectic/eutectoid) may give a pronounced arrest; slow, near-equilibrium cooling gives clearer transition temperatures.

Plotting cooling curves: heat a known, weighed sample above its liquidus; place a calibrated thermocouple in it; record temperature at equal time intervals during slow, uniform cooling; plot temperature vertically against time horizontally; mark arrests/inflections.

Constructing a binary equilibrium diagram from them:

  1. Choose several known compositions from component A to B, including both pure metals and intermediate alloys.
  2. For each composition, measure and plot its cooling curve under comparable slow-cooling conditions.
  3. Read the arrest/inflection temperatures that indicate liquidus, solidus and invariant reactions.
  4. Transfer each transition temperature to a temperature-versus-composition graph at that alloy composition.
  5. Join corresponding points smoothly to form liquidus/solidus and reaction lines; add end-member melting points and invariant points.
  6. Label phase fields, axes, compositions, temperatures and reactions; check the diagram is consistent with the measured curves.

True equilibrium requires sufficient time for diffusion; rapid cooling can shift/blur observed arrests.

પ્રશ્ન: Q1(c) Cooling curveનો ખ્યાલ અને તેને plot કરવાની રીત સમજાવો; cooling curveની મદદથી equilibrium diagram દોરવાનાં ક્રમબદ્ધ પગલાં લખો.

Cooling curve એટલે નિયંત્રિત દરે ઠંડા થતા molten metal/alloyનું temperature–time graph. ઢાળ બદલાય અથવા thermal arrest આવે તે phase transformation બતાવે; solidificationમાં નીકળતી latent heat તાપમાન ઘટવાનું ધીમું/થોડો સમય અટકાવી શકે.

  • શુદ્ધ ધાતુ: એક equilibrium તાપમાને ઘનીકરણ; freezing point પર curveમાં લગભગ આડી arrest.
  • Solid-solution rangeવાળો alloy: liquidus પર ઘનીકરણ શરૂ અને solidus પર પૂરું; એક લાંબી plateau બદલે slope change/freezing interval.
  • Eutectic/eutectoid જેવી invariant reactionમાં સ્પષ્ટ arrest આવી શકે; ધીમું, equilibrium નજીકનું cooling transition temperature વધુ સ્પષ્ટ કરે.

Cooling curve દોરવી: જાણીતા વજનનો sample liquidusથી ઉપર ગરમ કરો; calibrated thermocouple મૂકો; ધીમે સમાન દરે ઠંડો કરતાં સમાન સમયાંતરે temperature નોંધો; ઊભી ધરીએ temperature અને આડી ધરીએ time plot કરો; arrest/inflection દર્શાવો.

તે પરથી binary equilibrium diagram:

  1. Aથી B સુધીની જાણીતી composition પસંદ કરો; શુદ્ધ ધાતુ અને વચ્ચેના alloy સામેલ કરો.
  2. દરેક composition માટે સમાન ધીમા cooling conditionમાં cooling curve માપો/દોરો.
  3. Liquidus, solidus અને invariant reaction બતાવતા arrest/inflection temperature વાંચો.
  4. દરેક transition temperatureને તે alloy composition પર temperature-versus-composition graphમાં મૂકો.
  5. સંબંધિત બિંદુઓ જોડીને liquidus/solidus અને reaction line બનાવો; end-member melting point અને invariant point ઉમેરો.
  6. Phase field, axes, composition, temperature અને reaction label કરો; માપેલી curve સાથે consistency તપાસો.

સાચા equilibrium માટે diffusionને પૂરતો સમય જોઈએ; ઝડપી coolingથી arrest બદલાઈ/અસ્પષ્ટ થઈ શકે.

Q2 Set A(a) Write short note on Heat treatment.Q2 Set A(a) Heat treatment પર ટૂંકી નોંધ લખો.3 marks3 ગુણ

Question: Q2 Set A(a) Write short note on Heat treatment.

Heat treatment is controlled heating, soaking and cooling of a metal/ alloy in the solid state to change its microstructure and properties without intentionally changing its shape or chemical composition.

  1. Heat to the specified temperature.
  2. Soak long enough for the required temperature/structure through the section.
  3. Cool at a selected rate in furnace, air, oil, water or another medium.

Common treatments: annealing (soften, improve machinability), normalising (refine grains/strengthen), hardening (quench for hardness), tempering (reduce brittleness after hardening) and case hardening (hard surface, tough core). Temperature, time, alloy and section size determine the result.

પ્રશ્ન: Q2 Set A(a) Heat treatment પર ટૂંકી નોંધ લખો.

Heat treatment એટલે ધાતુ/alloyને ઘન અવસ્થામાં નિયંત્રિત રીતે ગરમ, soak અને ઠંડું કરીને microstructure/ગુણ બદલવાની પ્રક્રિયા; આકાર કે chemical composition ઇરાદાપૂર્વક બદલાતાં નથી.

  1. નક્કી temperature સુધી ગરમ કરો.
  2. આખા sectionમાં જરૂરી temperature/structure થાય એટલો સમય soak કરો.
  3. Furnace, air, oil, water અથવા બીજા માધ્યમમાં પસંદ કરેલા દરે ઠંડું કરો.

સામાન્ય process: annealing (નરમ, machinability સારી), normalising (grain સુધારે/મજબૂત), hardening (quenchથી hardness), tempering (hardening પછી brittleness ઘટાડે), case hardening (hard surface, tough core). પરિણામ temperature, time, alloy અને section size પર આધારિત.

Q2 Set A(b) List Physical properties of material & Explain any Two properties.Q2 Set A(b) Materialના physical properties લખો અને કોઈપણ બે સમજાવો.4 marks4 ગુણ

Question: Q2 Set A(b) List Physical properties of material & Explain any Two properties.

Physical properties include: density/specific gravity, melting/freezing point, colour/appearance, structure/porosity, specific heat, thermal conductivity/expansion, electrical conductivity/resistivity, magnetic and optical properties.

1. Density: mass per unit volume, ρ=m/V (kg/m³). It matters in component weight, inertia and strength-to-weight selection; lower density is useful in transport, provided strength/stiffness still meet the design.

2. Thermal conductivity: ability to conduct heat, k (W/m·K). High k materials such as copper spread/remove heat in heat exchangers; low k materials such as ceramics/polymers are used for insulation. It is distinct from specific heat, which is energy needed to raise unit mass by one degree.

Other properties may be listed; explain any two with definition, unit/use and a relevant example for full marks.

પ્રશ્ન: Q2 Set A(b) Materialના physical properties લખો અને કોઈપણ બે સમજાવો.

Physical properties: density/specific gravity, melting/freezing point, રંગ/દેખાવ, structure/porosity, specific heat, thermal conductivity/expansion, electrical conductivity/resistivity, magnetic અને optical ગુણ.

1. Density: એકમ volume દીઠ mass, ρ=m/V (kg/m³). Componentનું વજન, inertia અને strength-to-weight પસંદગીમાં કામ લાગે; transportમાં ઓછી density લાભદાયી, જો strength/stiffness પૂરતી હોય.

2. Thermal conductivity: heat વહન કરવાની ક્ષમતા, k (W/m·K). Copper જેવી ઊંચા kની સામગ્રી heat exchangerમાં heat ફેલાવે/કાઢે; ceramic/polymer જેવી ઓછી kની સામગ્રી insulationમાં. તે specific heatથી જુદી છે; specific heat એટલે એકમ massનું તાપમાન એક degree વધારવા જરૂરી energy.

Question: Q2 Set B(a) Explain Nitriding process.

Nitriding is a case-hardening process in which nitrogen diffuses into the surface of a suitable alloy steel and forms hard nitrides, without heating into the austenite range.

  1. Use a clean, usually quenched-and-tempered alloy-steel part containing nitride-forming elements such as Al, Cr or Mo.
  2. Heat in dissociated ammonia gas (gas nitriding), plasma, or a controlled salt medium, typically around 500–550°C; hold for the required case depth.
  3. Cool in a controlled manner, usually without a quench; finish-grind only if specified.

Advantages: very hard wear-resistant case, improved fatigue resistance, low distortion because temperature is relatively low and no severe quench is required. Limitations: long cycle/cost; best on suitable alloy steels; brittle compound (“white”) layer may need control/removal.

Question: Q3 Set A(b) Explain Carburizing process.

Carburizing is a case-hardening process for low-carbon steel. Carbon diffuses into the surface while the part is held in a carbon-rich environment at austenitising temperature (commonly about 900–950°C, chosen for the steel/process).

  1. Clean and machine a low-carbon steel component; protect surfaces that must not be carburized if needed.
  2. Heat in a carbon-rich medium: pack/solid carburizing, gas carburizing or liquid carburizing.
  3. Hold at temperature so carbon diffuses to the required case depth; time and carbon potential control the case.
  4. Quench to transform the high-carbon surface to hard martensite; temper as specified. The low-carbon core remains tougher.
  5. Finish-grind and inspect case depth, hardness, distortion and dimensions.

Result/uses: hard, wear-resistant surface with a tough core for gears, shafts, cams and pinions. Control distortion, cracking and excessive/brittle surface carbon.

Question: Q3 Set B(b) List types of furnaces and explain any one furnace.

Types: cupola, crucible, reverberatory, rotary, blast, muffle, resistance/electric, induction and electric-arc furnace. Choose by metal, quantity, temperature, melt quality and fuel/power.

Cupola furnace (cast iron melting):

  • Construction: vertical steel shell lined with refractory; charging door, stack, tuyeres for air blast, wind belt, coke bed, slag hole, tap hole and drop-bottom/clean-out.
  • Charge: alternate layers of pig iron/return scrap, coke and limestone flux are charged from the top.

Question: Q4 Set A(b) What is refractory material? State its basic properties.

A refractory material is a heat-resistant material used to line furnaces, kilns, boilers and vessels that face high temperature, chemical attack and mechanical/thermal stress.

  • Refractoriness: withstands high temperature without softening/melting; refractoriness under load is also important.
  • Thermal stability: resists thermal shock, spalling and repeated temperature cycling; controlled thermal expansion.
  • Chemical resistance: compatible with furnace atmosphere, slag, molten metal and flux (acidic/basic/neutral character).
  • Mechanical properties: adequate hot strength, abrasion/erosion resistance, dimensional stability and low creep.
  • Heat-transfer properties: conductivity/porosity selected for the duty—insulating brick has low conductivity, dense working lining may need erosion resistance.

Examples: silica (acid), magnesite (basic), fireclay/high-alumina (often neutral to mildly acidic depending on service), silicon carbide.

Principle/setup: electroplating deposits a thin metal layer on a conductive article by electrolysis. Connect the article to the negative terminal as the cathode; use a plating-metal anode (or inert anode for a supplied-metal-ion bath), suitable electrolyte and DC supply.

  1. Degrease, clean, pickle/rinse and activate the surface; poor preparation causes peeling/poor adhesion.
  2. Immerse the work and anode in the electrolyte; keep proper spacing and electrical contact.
  3. Pass controlled DC. Metal cations move to the cathode and gain electrons to form a coating; the anode replenishes ions when it is soluble.
  4. Control bath chemistry, temperature, current density, agitation, time and anode/cathode area.
  5. Rinse, dry and inspect coating thickness, adhesion, porosity, appearance and required properties.

Purposes: corrosion protection (zinc/nickel), decorative finish (chromium/nickel/gold), wear/hardness, improved electrical contact and restoration of dimensions.

Q5 Set A(a) Explain properties of cast iron (any Three).Q5 Set A(a) Cast ironના કોઈપણ ત્રણ ગુણધર્મ સમજાવો.3 marks3 ગુણ

Question: Q5 Set A(a) Explain properties of cast iron (any Three).

  • High carbon content: typically about 2.1–4 wt% C plus silicon; composition depends on grade.
  • Excellent castability: relatively low melting range and good fluidity allow complex shapes.
  • High compressive strength and wear resistance, especially in suitable grades.
  • Brittle/low tensile ductility: graphite flakes in grey iron act as stress raisers; avoid impact/tension-heavy design unless grade permits.
  • Good vibration damping and machinability for grey iron; graphite can retain lubricant.
  • Other grades vary: ductile iron has nodular graphite and much better tensile ductility; white iron is very hard and wear resistant.

પ્રશ્ન: Q5 Set A(a) Cast ironના કોઈપણ ત્રણ ગુણધર્મ સમજાવો.

  • Carbon વધુ: સામાન્ય આશરે 2.1–4 wt% C અને silicon; grade પ્રમાણે રચના બદલાય.
  • Castability ઉત્તમ: ગલન તાપમાનનો વિસ્તાર ઓછો/પ્રવાહ સારું હોવાથી જટિલ આકાર સરળતાથી ઢળે.
  • Compression strength અને wear resistance ઊંચી, યોગ્ય gradeમાં.
  • Brittle અને tensile ductility ઓછી: grey ironના graphite flake stress વધારતા crack જેવી અસર કરે; grade યોગ્ય ન હોય તો impact/tensionથી બચો.
  • Vibration damping અને machinability સારી (grey iron); graphite lubricant પકડી શકે.
  • Grade જુદા: ductile ironમાં nodular graphiteથી tensile ductility સારી; white iron ખૂબ hard/wear-resistant.
Q5 Set A(b) Explain surface coating through electrolysis set up.Q5 Set A(b) Electrolysis setupથી surface coating process સમજાવો.4 marks4 ગુણ

Question: Q5 Set A(b) Explain surface coating through electrolysis set up.

Electrolytic coating/electroplating: a clean conductive article is connected as cathode (−); coating metal is commonly the soluble anode (+); both are immersed in an electrolyte containing metal ions and connected to a controlled DC supply.

  1. Degrease, mechanically/chemically clean, rinse and activate the article; masking protects areas not to be coated.
  2. Prepare compatible electrolyte and anode; arrange electrical contacts and bath agitation/temperature control.
  3. Pass DC. Metal ions move to the cathode, gain electrons and form an adherent metal layer. The soluble anode dissolves to replenish ions; an inert anode requires bath chemistry control.
  4. Coating thickness depends on current, time, current efficiency and distribution; rinse, dry and inspect adhesion, thickness and appearance.

Examples: zinc on steel for corrosion protection, nickel/chromium for appearance/wear, copper/tin for electrical or soldering duties. Good cleaning, current distribution, bath control and effluent treatment are essential.

પ્રશ્ન: Q5 Set A(b) Electrolysis setupથી surface coating process સમજાવો.

Electrolytic coating/electroplating: સાફ conductive વસ્તુને cathode (−) જોડો; coating metal સામાન્ય રીતે soluble anode (+); બંનેને metal-ionવાળા electrolyteમાં મૂકી નિયંત્રિત DC આપો.

  1. વસ્તુ degrease, mechanical/chemical clean, rinse અને activate કરો; coating ન જોઈએ તે વિસ્તાર mask કરો.
  2. સુસંગત electrolyte/anode તૈયાર કરો; electrical contact, bath agitation અને temperature ગોઠવો.
  3. DC વહાવો. Metal ion cathode તરફ જઈ electron લઈને ચોંટેલો metal layer બનાવે. Soluble anode ઓગળી ion પૂરા કરે; inert anode હોય તો bath chemistry નિયંત્રિત કરો.
  4. Thickness current, time, current efficiency અને distribution પર આધારિત; rinse/dry કરી adhesion, thickness અને દેખાવ તપાસો.

ઉદાહરણ: corrosion સામે steel પર zinc; દેખાવ/wear માટે nickel/chromium; electrical/soldering માટે copper/tin. સારી સફાઈ, current distribution, bath control અને effluent treatment જરૂરી.

Examples: alumina (Al₂O₃), silica/glass, zirconia (ZrO₂), silicon carbide (SiC), silicon nitride (Si₃N₄), boron carbide (B₄C), magnesia (MgO), clay/refractories and cementitious ceramics.

Useful engineering characteristics:

  • High hardness, compressive strength, wear/abrasion resistance and dimensional stability.
  • High melting point and oxidation/chemical/corrosion resistance; good for hot, corrosive or abrasive service.

Q5 Set B(a) Explain Cloud point and pour point.Q5 Set B(a) Cloud point અને pour point સમજાવો.3 marks3 ગુણ

Question: Q5 Set B(a) Explain Cloud point and pour point.

  • Cloud point: when an oil is cooled under a specified test, the temperature at which wax crystals first appear and the sample becomes visibly cloudy. It warns of wax formation and possible filter blockage at low temperature.
  • Pour point: the lowest temperature under a specified test at which the oil still flows/pours. Below it, wax structure/viscosity prevents observable flow.

Cloud point is generally higher than pour point. Both are low-temperature flow indicators, not the same as flash point or freezing point; report the test method because results depend on procedure.

પ્રશ્ન: Q5 Set B(a) Cloud point અને pour point સમજાવો.

  • Cloud point: નિર્ધારિત testમાં oil ઠંડું કરતાં wax crystal પહેલી વાર દેખાય અને sample ધૂંધળું બને તે તાપમાન. નીચા તાપમાને wax/filter blockageની ચેતવણી.
  • Pour point: નિર્ધારિત testમાં oil હજી વહે/ઢોળાય તે સૌથી નીચું તાપમાન. તેનાથી નીચે wax રચના/viscosityના કારણે પ્રવાહ દેખાતો નથી.

Cloud point સામાન્ય રીતે pour pointથી ઊંચો. બંને નીચા તાપમાનના flow દર્શાવે; flash point/freezing pointથી જુદા. પરિણામ test procedure પર આધારિત હોવાથી પદ્ધતિ જણાવો.

Question: Q5 Set B(b) Explain powder metallurgy in brief and where it is applicable.

Powder metallurgy (PM) makes components from metal/ceramic powders by pressing and sintering rather than melting and casting the whole part.

  1. Powder production: atomisation, reduction, electrolysis or other suitable route.
  2. Mix/blend: combine powders, alloying additions and lubricant/binder for uniform composition.
  3. Compact: press powder in a die to the required “green” shape/density.
  4. Sinter: heat below the main melting point in a controlled atmosphere so particles bond and strength develops.
  5. Finish: size/coining, impregnation, infiltration, heat treatment or machining if needed.

Advantages: near-net shape, good material utilisation, repeatability, controlled porosity and ability to combine high-melting materials. Applications: self-lubricating porous bearings, small gears, filters, electrical contacts, hard-metal cutting inserts and magnetic components. Limits: powder/die cost, part-size/shape limits, residual porosity and less economical for very small runs.

પ્રશ્ન: Q5 Set B(b) Powder metallurgy ટૂંકમાં સમજાવો અને તેનો ઉપયોગ ક્યાં થાય તે જણાવો.

Powder metallurgy (PM)માં આખો ભાગ ઓગાળી ઢાળવાને બદલે metal/ceramic powderને દબાવી sinter કરીને component બનાવે.

  1. Powder બનાવો: atomisation, reduction, electrolysis અથવા યોગ્ય પદ્ધતિ.
  2. Mix/blend: સમાન રચના માટે powder, alloying addition અને lubricant/binder ભેળવો.
  3. Compact: dieમાં દબાવી જરૂરી “green” shape/density બનાવો.
  4. Sinter: મુખ્ય melting pointથી નીચે નિયંત્રિત વાતાવરણમાં ગરમ કરો; કણ જોડાઈ strength મેળવે.
  5. Finish: sizing/coining, impregnation, infiltration, heat treatment અથવા જરૂર મુજબ machining.

લાભ: near-net shape, materialનો સારો ઉપયોગ, repeatability, porosity control, ઊંચા meltingના પદાર્થ જોડાય. ઉપયોગ: self-lubricating porous bearing, નાના gear, filter, electrical contact, hard-metal cutting insert, magnetic ભાગ. મર્યાદા: powder/die ખર્ચ, આકાર/કદ મર્યાદા, બાકી porosity, બહુ નાના જથ્થામાં ખર્ચાળ.

Q5 Set B(c) Write a short note on types of corrosion.Q5 Set B(c) Corrosionના પ્રકારો પર ટૂંકી નોંધ લખો.7 marks7 ગુણ

Question: Q5 Set B(c) Write a short note on types of corrosion.

Corrosion is deterioration of a material, usually a metal, by chemical/electrochemical interaction with its environment. Important forms include:

  • Uniform/general: roughly even metal loss over a surface; manage by coating/material selection/corrosion allowance.
  • Galvanic: dissimilar metals electrically connected in an electrolyte; the more active metal corrodes faster. Isolate metals or select compatible couples.
  • Pitting: small local passive-film breakdown produces deep pits; dangerous because little surface loss can hide penetration.
  • Crevice: local attack in stagnant shielded gaps under gaskets/deposits due to differential aeration/chemistry.
  • Intergranular: attack along grain boundaries, e.g. sensitised stainless steel; control alloy/heat treatment.
  • Erosion-corrosion: fast flow/particles remove protective film; common at bends, valves and pumps.
  • Stress-corrosion cracking: tensile stress plus a specific corrosive environment produces cracks.
  • Selective leaching/dealloying: one alloy element is preferentially removed (e.g. dezincification of brass).
  • Fretting corrosion: small repeated relative motion damages oxide at a contact and creates debris.

Identify by damage shape, environment and mechanism; prevention may combine material choice, coating, design, inhibitor, drainage, cathodic protection and inspection.

પ્રશ્ન: Q5 Set B(c) Corrosionના પ્રકારો પર ટૂંકી નોંધ લખો.

Corrosion એટલે પર્યાવરણ સાથે chemical/electrochemical ક્રિયાથી સામગ્રી, ખાસ કરીને ધાતુ, બગડવી. મહત્વના પ્રકાર:

  • Uniform/general: સપાટી પર લગભગ સરખું metal loss; coating/material પસંદગી/corrosion allowanceથી નિયંત્રણ.
  • Galvanic: electrolyteમાં જોડાયેલી જુદી ધાતુમાં વધુ active ધાતુ ઝડપથી કાટ ખાય; અલગ કરો અથવા compatible couple લો.
  • Pitting: passive filmનું નાનું સ્થાનિક તૂટવું ઊંડો pit બનાવે; બહારનું નુકસાન ઓછું છતાં છિદ્ર થઈ શકે.
  • Crevice: gasket/deposit નીચે અટકેલા પાણીમાં oxygen/chemistryના તફાવતથી સ્થાનિક કાટ.
  • Intergranular: grain boundary પર હુમલો, જેમ sensitised stainless steelમાં; alloy/heat treatment નિયંત્રો.
  • Erosion-corrosion: ઝડપી પ્રવાહ/કણ રક્ષણાત્મક film કાઢે; bend, valve, pump પાસે સામાન્ય.
  • Stress-corrosion cracking: tensile stress અને ખાસ corrosive environment સાથે crack થાય.
  • Selective leaching/dealloying: alloyનું એક element પસંદગીથી નીકળે (જેમ brassમાંથી zinc—dezincification).
  • Fretting corrosion: સંપર્કમાં નાની વારંવારની ગતિ oxide તોડે અને debris બનાવે.

નુકસાનનો આકાર, વાતાવરણ અને mechanismથી ઓળખો; preventionમાં યોગ્ય material, coating, design, inhibitor, drainage, cathodic protection અને inspection જોડો.
