Q1(a) Explain the classification of thread.Q1(a) Threadનું વર્ગીકરણ સમજાવો.3 marks3 ગુણ

Question: Q1(a) Explain the classification of thread.

A thread is a continuous helical ridge or groove on a cylindrical or conical surface. Classify it on different independent bases:

  • By location: external (bolt/screw) and internal (nut).
  • By helix direction: right-hand and left-hand.
  • By number of starts: single-start and multi-start. Lead = number of starts × pitch.
  • By profile/form: V/metric or Unified, Whitworth, square, Acme/trapezoidal, buttress and knuckle/round.
  • By use: fastening threads (e.g. metric V) and power/motion-transmitting threads (e.g. square, Acme, buttress).
  • By standard/series: metric, Unified, Whitworth, pipe and other national/product standards.

For a 3-mark answer, name at least three bases and give a correct example for each.

પ્રશ્ન: Q1(a) Threadનું વર્ગીકરણ સમજાવો.

Thread એટલે cylindrical અથવા conical સપાટી પરનો સતત helical ridge/groove. જુદા સ્વતંત્ર આધાર પ્રમાણે વર્ગીકરણ:

  • સ્થાન પ્રમાણે: external (bolt/screw) અને internal (nut).
  • Helix દિશા પ્રમાણે: right-hand અને left-hand.
  • Startsની સંખ્યા પ્રમાણે: single-start અને multi-start. Lead = startsની સંખ્યા × pitch.
  • આકાર પ્રમાણે: V/metric અથવા Unified, Whitworth, square, Acme/trapezoidal, buttress અને knuckle/round.
  • ઉપયોગ પ્રમાણે: fastening (જેમ કે metric V) અને power/motion transmission (જેમ કે square, Acme, buttress).
  • Standard/series પ્રમાણે: metric, Unified, Whitworth, pipe તથા અન્ય product standards.

Q1(b) Discuss any three factors affecting tool life and productivity.Q1(b) Tool life અને productivityને અસર કરતા કોઈ પણ ત્રણ પરિબળોની ચર્ચા કરો.4 marks4 ગુણ

Question: Q1(b) Discuss any three factors affecting tool life and productivity.

1. Cutting speed: the strongest common influence. Increasing speed raises cutting temperature and usually reduces tool life sharply; however, it can shorten machining time and raise output. Choose a speed within the tool/work-material recommendation.

2. Feed and depth of cut: higher feed/depth removes more material per pass and may improve output, but raises cutting force, heat, deflection and wear. Excessive values cause poor finish, chatter or tool failure.

3. Tool and work materials / tool geometry: a suitable hard, hot-hard and wear-resistant tool material, correct rake/clearance, nose radius and edge preparation improve life and finish. Geometry must suit material and operation.

4. Cutting fluid and temperature: suitable coolant/lubricant can reduce heat, friction and built-up edge and help chip removal; wrong fluid or poor delivery may not help and can cause thermal shock.

5. Rigidity, alignment and vibration: secure work/tool holding and a rigid machine reduce chatter and edge chipping, protecting life and dimensional accuracy.

6. Tool-change policy: replace or regrind at a planned wear limit. Excessively early changes waste time; a broken tool or rejected part costs more. Thus productivity depends on both cutting rate and acceptable tool life/quality.

પ્રશ્ન: Q1(b) Tool life અને productivityને અસર કરતા કોઈ પણ ત્રણ પરિબળોની ચર્ચા કરો.

1. Cutting speed: સામાન્ય રીતે સૌથી વધુ અસર કરે. Speed વધે તો cutting temperature વધે અને tool life ઝડપથી ઘટે; પણ machining time ઘટી output વધી શકે. Tool/work materialની ભલામણમાં speed પસંદ કરો.

2. Feed અને depth of cut: વધારે feed/depthથી એક passમાં વધુ material દૂર થાય અને output વધે; પરંતુ cutting force, heat, deflection અને wear વધે. અતિશય valueથી poor finish, chatter અથવા tool failure થાય.

3. Tool/work material અને geometry: યોગ્ય hard, hot-hard, wear-resistant tool material તથા rake/clearance, nose radius અને edge preparation tool life અને finish સુધારે. Geometry material/operationને અનુરૂપ હોવી જોઈએ.

4. Cutting fluid અને temperature: યોગ્ય coolant/lubricant heat, friction, built-up edge ઘટાડે અને chip removalમાં મદદ કરે; ખોટો fluid અથવા ખોટી deliveryથી લાભ ન મળે, thermal shock પણ થઈ શકે.

5. Rigidity, alignment અને vibration: મજબૂત work/tool holding અને rigid machine chatter, edge chipping ઘટાડે; tool life અને dimensional accuracy સુધરે.

6. Tool-change policy: નક્કી wear limitે tool બદલો/ફરી grind કરો. બહુ વહેલો બદલવાથી સમય બગડે; તૂટેલો tool અથવા rejected part વધુ ખર્ચ કરે. Productivityમાં cutting rate સાથે tool life અને quality બંને મહત્વના.

Question: Q1(c) Explain: Thread milling process.

Principle: a rotating multi-edge thread mill and controlled relative motion generate the helical thread. The cutter rotates about its own axis while the machine follows a circular path around the hole/shaft and advances axially by the thread lead for each revolution.

  1. Select a single-form or multi-form cutter matching thread form, pitch/lead, diameter, hand and material; select internal or external thread tool.
  2. Clamp and locate the work; establish the programmed centre, pitch diameter and safe approach/exit.
  3. Rotate the cutter at the specified cutting speed. CNC circular interpolation moves it around the work while the Z-axis advances one lead per helical revolution.
  4. For internal threads the tool orbits inside the bore; for external threads it orbits around the outside. One or more passes may be used for size/finish.
  5. Retract safely, remove chips, deburr and inspect pitch diameter, lead, flank form and fit with gauges or measurement.

Advantages: one cutter can cover several diameters of the same pitch; lower cutting force than a tap, good chip evacuation, controllable size, suitable for blind holes and interrupted threads, and a broken tool is easier to remove.

Limitations: CNC interpolation/programming and setup are needed; tool path takes time for small simple threads; cutter cost and tool deflection matter. It is cutting (chip-making), not thread rolling.

પ્રશ્ન: Q1(c) Thread milling process સમજાવો.

સિદ્ધાંત: ફરતો multi-edge thread mill અને નિયંત્રિત relative motionથી helical thread બને. Cutter પોતાની ધરી પર ફરે; machine hole/shaftની આસપાસ circular path કરે અને દરેક revolution દીઠ thread lead જેટલું axial આગળ વધે.

  1. Thread form, pitch/lead, diameter, hand અને materialને યોગ્ય single-form અથવા multi-form cutter પસંદ કરો; internal કે external tool નક્કી કરો.
  2. Workને પકડો/સ્થિર કરો; programmed centre, pitch diameter અને સુરક્ષિત approach/exit નક્કી કરો.
  3. નક્કી cutting speed પર cutter ફેરવો. CNC circular interpolation workની આસપાસ ફેરવે અને Z-axis દરેક helical revolution દીઠ એક lead આગળ વધે.
  4. Internal threadમાં tool boreની અંદર orbit કરે; external threadમાં બહાર orbit કરે. Size/finish માટે એક કે વધુ passes થઈ શકે.
  5. સુરક્ષિત retract કરો, chips દૂર કરો, deburr કરો અને gauge/measurementથી pitch diameter, lead, flank form, fit તપાસો.

લાભ: એક cutterથી સમાન pitchના અનેક diameter થઈ શકે; tap કરતાં cutting force ઓછી, chip કાઢવી સારી, size control, blind/interrupted thread માટે યોગ્ય; તૂટેલો tool કાઢવો સરળ.

મર્યાદા: CNC interpolation/programming અને setup જોઈએ; નાના સાદા threadમાં tool path સમય લે; cutter cost અને deflection મહત્વના. આ chip બનાવતી cutting છે, thread rolling નથી.

Q1(c) OR · Explain: Thread rolling process.Q1(c) OR · Thread rolling process સમજાવો.7 marks7 ગુણ

Question: Q1(c) OR · Explain: Thread rolling process.

Principle: thread rolling forms a thread by plastic deformation, not by cutting. Hardened dies press and roll a ductile cylindrical blank so material flows into the die grooves and forms the thread ridges.

  1. Prepare a ductile blank of suitable material and controlled diameter/length; blank diameter is chosen for the required thread and material flow, not simply assumed equal to major diameter.
  2. Set two flat dies (one fixed, one reciprocating) or cylindrical/rotary dies to the specified pitch, form and alignment.
  3. Place the blank between dies. Die motion rotates/feeds the blank while pressure progressively displaces surface metal into the thread profile.
  4. At completion, withdraw the dies and inspect major/minor diameter, pitch, lead, crest/root fill and gauge fit.

Methods: flat-die rolling (high-volume external threads; reciprocating die) and cylindrical-die rolling (infeed or through-feed, depending on die/work arrangement). Internal thread rolling is possible with special tools.

Advantages: no chips; high production rate; good surface finish; work-hardened surface and continuous grain flow can improve fatigue strength; low material waste.

Limitations: needs ductile material and accurate blank size; high die/setup cost; not suitable for every large, brittle or low-volume job; die wear and springback must be controlled. Include a labelled die-and-blank sketch.

પ્રશ્ન: Q1(c) OR · Thread rolling process સમજાવો.

સિદ્ધાંત: Thread rollingમાં cutting નહીં, plastic deformationથી thread બને. Hardened dies નરમ/ductile cylindrical blankને દબાવી ફેરવે; material die groovesમાં વહીને thread ridge બનાવે.

  1. યોગ્ય ductile materialનો નિયંત્રિત diameter/length blank તૈયાર કરો; blank diameter જરૂરી thread અને material flow પ્રમાણે પસંદ થાય, major diameter જેટલો જ માની ન લો.
  2. નક્કી pitch, form અને alignmentવાળા બે flat dies (એક સ્થિર, એક આગળ-પાછળ) અથવા cylindrical/rotary dies ગોઠવો.
  3. Blankને dies વચ્ચે મૂકો. Dieની ગતિ blankને ફેરવે/આગળ ધપાવે; દબાણથી સપાટીનું metal thread profileમાં ખસે.
  4. થ્રેડ બન્યા પછી dies દૂર કરો; major/minor diameter, pitch, lead, crest/root ભરાવ અને gauge fit તપાસો.

પદ્ધતિ: flat-die rolling (વધુ જથ્થાના external threads; reciprocating die) અને cylindrical-die rolling (die/work arrangement મુજબ infeed અથવા through-feed). ખાસ toolથી internal thread rolling પણ શક્ય.

લાભ: chips નહીં; production ઝડપી; finish સારી; work-hardened surface અને સતત grain flowથી fatigue strength સુધરી શકે; material waste ઓછો.

મર્યાદા: ductile material અને ચોક્કસ blank size જોઈએ; die/setup ખર્ચાળ; દરેક મોટા, brittle અથવા ઓછા જથ્થાના કામ માટે યોગ્ય નહીં; die wear/springback નિયંત્રિત કરો. Labelવાળી die-and-blank આકૃતિ દોરો.

Q2 Set A(a) Briefly explain gear forming process.Q2 Set A(a) Gear forming process ટૂંકમાં સમજાવો.3 marks3 ગુણ

Question: Q2 Set A(a) Briefly explain gear forming process.

In this paper's gear-manufacturing terminology, form cutting/form milling is the expected gear-forming example: a cutter whose profile corresponds to a tooth space cuts one space at a time. (In the broader process taxonomy, casting, forging, powder metallurgy and moulding are near-net-shape preforming; do not confuse these with chip-cutting form milling.)

  1. Mount a compatible form-relieved gear cutter on the milling machine and mount the blank on a dividing head.
  2. Set module/profile, cutter position and depth from the drawing/reference system.
  3. Mill one tooth space across the face width.
  4. Withdraw the cutter and index the blank by 1/Z of a revolution, where Z is the required tooth count.
  5. Repeat until all spaces are cut; inspect tooth thickness, runout and profile.

Advantages: simple, economical for small batches and general-purpose milling. Limitations: intermittent indexing is slower and cutter profile only approximates the involute over its designated tooth-number range; accuracy/productivity are below good generating methods.

પ્રશ્ન: Q2 Set A(a) Gear forming process ટૂંકમાં સમજાવો.

આ paperની gear-manufacturing terminologyમાં form cutting/form milling અપેક્ષિત gear-forming ઉદાહરણ છે: tooth-spaceને અનુરૂપ profileવાળો cutter એક સમયે એક space કાપે. (મોટી process taxonomyમાં casting, forging, powder metallurgy અને moulding near-net-shape preforming છે; તેને chip-cutting form milling સાથે ગૂંચવો નહીં.)

  1. Milling machineમાં યોગ્ય form-relieved gear cutter અને dividing head પર gear blank બેસાડો.
  2. Drawing/reference system પ્રમાણે module/profile, cutter position અને depth ગોઠવો.
  3. Face widthમાં એક tooth space mill કરો.
  4. Cutter દૂર કરીને blankને 1/Z revolution index કરો; Z જરૂરી teethની સંખ્યા.
  5. બધી spaces સુધી પુનરાવર્તન કરો; tooth thickness, runout અને profile તપાસો.

લાભ: સરળ, નાના જથ્થા અને general-purpose milling માટે સસ્તું. મર્યાદા: વારંવાર indexingથી ધીમું; cutter profile નિર્ધારિત tooth-number rangeમાં involuteનો અંદાજ આપે; સારી generating method કરતાં accuracy/productivity ઓછી.

Q2 Set A(b) Explain gear tooth terminology for spur gear.Q2 Set A(b) Spur gear માટે gear-tooth terminology સમજાવો.4 marks4 ગુણ

Question: Q2 Set A(b) Explain gear tooth terminology for spur gear.

Use the following terms for a standard external spur gear; dimensions depend on the specified module, pressure angle and tooth system.

  • Pitch circle / pitch diameter (d): imaginary rolling circle; d=mZ, where module m=d/Z and Z is tooth count.
  • Circular pitch (p): distance along pitch circle between corresponding points of adjacent teeth; p=πd/Z=πm.
  • Tooth thickness: circumferential tooth thickness measured on pitch circle; nominally p/2 before backlash allowance for a standard tooth.
  • Addendum circle: circle through tooth tips. Addendum (a): radial distance from pitch circle to addendum circle.
  • Root/dedendum circle: circle through tooth roots. Dedendum (b): radial distance from pitch circle to root circle.
  • Whole depth: addendum + dedendum; working depth: the depth of engagement of mating teeth; clearance: radial gap between one tooth tip and mating root.
  • Backlash: circumferential clearance between mating tooth flanks; face: tooth surface above pitch circle; flank: below pitch circle; face width: axial tooth width.
  • Base circle: circle from which the involute tooth profile is generated. Pressure angle is defined at the pitch point between the common normal and tangent to the pitch circle.

For a standard 20° full-depth, unshifted system only: a≈m, b≈1.25m, whole depth≈2.25m, working depth≈2m and clearance≈0.25m. State this assumption if quoting these values.

પ્રશ્ન: Q2 Set A(b) Spur gear માટે gear-tooth terminology સમજાવો.

નીચેના શબ્દો standard external spur gear માટે છે; માપો આપેલા module, pressure angle અને tooth system પર આધારિત છે.

  • Pitch circle / pitch diameter (d): કલ્પિત rolling circle; d=mZ, જ્યાં module m=d/Z અને Z toothની સંખ્યા.
  • Circular pitch (p): pitch circle પર બાજુના teethના સમાન બિંદુઓ વચ્ચેનું અંતર; p=πd/Z=πm.
  • Tooth thickness: pitch circle પર circumferential thickness; standard tooth માટે backlash પહેલાં આશરે p/2.
  • Addendum circle: tooth tipમાંથી જતો circle. Addendum (a): pitch circleથી addendum circleનું radial અંતર.
  • Root/dedendum circle: tooth rootમાંથી જતો circle. Dedendum (b): pitch circleથી root circleનું radial અંતર.
  • Whole depth: addendum + dedendum; working depth: mating teeth જોડાય ત્યારે કામ કરતો depth; clearance: એક tooth tip અને સામેના root વચ્ચે radial gap.
  • Backlash: mating flanks વચ્ચે circumferential clearance; face: pitch circle ઉપરનો tooth ભાગ; flank: નીચેનો ભાગ; face width: axial tooth width.
  • Base circle: involute profile જે circleમાંથી બને. Pitch pointએ common normal અને pitch circleની tangent વચ્ચેનો ખૂણો pressure angle.

ફક્ત standard 20° full-depth, unshifted system માટે: a≈m, b≈1.25m, whole depth≈2.25m, working depth≈2m અને clearance≈0.25m. આ values આપો તો ધારણા લખો.

Q2 Set A(c) Explain gear finishing process used in industries.Q2 Set A(c) Industriesમાં વપરાતી gear finishing processes સમજાવો.7 marks7 ગુણ

Question: Q2 Set A(c) Explain gear finishing process used in industries.

Need: finishing corrects tooth profile/lead errors and improves surface finish, noise, contact pattern, load sharing and service life. Choose the method, stock allowance and sequence according to accuracy requirement, hardness/heat treatment and production volume.

  • Gear shaving: a serrated shaving cutter meshes with the gear and removes very fine chips by sliding action. Commonly used on relatively soft gears before hardening to correct small errors quickly; not a substitute for grinding a fully hardened gear.
  • Gear grinding: an abrasive wheel is form-ground or generating-ground against the teeth. It removes small stock after heat treatment, corrects distortion and achieves high accuracy/finish; it is slower and requires controlled dressing, heat and inspection.
  • Honing: an abrasive gear-like tool meshes with the gear at light load, smoothing tooth surfaces and reducing noise; it corrects only limited geometric error.
  • Lapping: a gear is run with a mating lap and fine abrasive compound to improve contact and surface finish; it removes very little material and does not correct large errors.
  • Burnishing/roll finishing: hardened or shaped rolling tools plastically smooth the flanks for suitable softer gears; process/material limits apply.

Principle: a 3D CAD model is converted into a physical part by building successive thin cross-sectional layers, usually by an additive process.

Question: Q3 Set A(c) Explain 3D printing process.

3D printing is an additive manufacturing process in which material is joined in successive layers from digital 3D-model data to make a part.

  1. Design: make or obtain a watertight CAD model; check units, wall thickness and features.
  2. File preparation: export STL/3MF, orient the part and select layer height, supports, infill/scan strategy and material/process settings.
  3. Slicing: software divides the model into 2D layers and generates tool paths, supports and machine instructions.
  4. Set up: load suitable material, prepare platform and verify machine, safety and process-specific parameters.
  5. Build: print the first layer; then deposit/fuse/bond each following layer in sequence. Layer adhesion and process control govern quality.
  6. Finish: allow safe cooling; remove part/supports; wash/cure/sinter or machine/finish if required.
  7. Inspect: check dimensions, defects, surface, material properties and intended function; compare with CAD.

Benefits: complex geometry, customisation, low tooling and fast iteration. Limits: anisotropy/layer defects, surface finish, build size/speed, support and material/process constraints.

Question: Q3 Set B(c) Explain the fused deposition modeling (FDM) process.

Principle: a thermoplastic filament is fed through a heated liquefier/nozzle and extruded as a controlled bead onto a platform. The bead cools and bonds to neighbouring roads and the previous layer.

  1. Prepare CAD model, orient it, slice into layers and generate perimeter, infill and support paths.
  2. Load compatible filament spool and guide it through drive rollers into the heated nozzle.
  3. Move nozzle/platform in X–Y to deposit the first layer on the build plate; Z changes by the layer height.
  4. Continue depositing each 2D cross-section. Heat and pressure allow roads/layers to bond; cooling solidifies the shape.
  5. Use support material/structures for overhangs if required; remove them after printing.
  6. Cool, detach, trim/support-remove, finish and inspect the part.

Advantages: simple equipment, common thermoplastics, low tooling and low cost, useful for prototypes and fixtures. Limitations: visible layer lines, anisotropic strength, warping/stringing, support waste and limited fine detail/material choices.

Question: Q5 Set A(b) Explain : MEMS process

MEMS means Micro-Electro-Mechanical Systems: microscale mechanical structures integrated with sensing/actuation and often electronics. A MEMS “process” is a sequence of microfabrication steps, not one single universal recipe.

  1. Select substrate/material (commonly silicon; also glass, polymers, metals or piezoelectric materials as needed) and clean it.
  2. Grow/lay down films by oxidation, deposition or other suitable process.
  3. Apply photoresist; use lithography/mask exposure and development to define a micro-pattern.
  4. Etch exposed material (wet or dry) or deposit/pattern a layer; repeat layer-by-layer as the design requires.
  5. Form structural/sacrificial layers, release moving parts by selective etching, and add electrodes/interconnects.
  6. Bond/package the device, connect electronics and calibrate/test the sensor or actuator.

Examples: pressure/acceleration sensors, inkjet heads, microphones and microfluidic devices. Not every MEMS uses every step; packaging and contamination control are critical.

Question: Q5 Set A(c) Explain Electrochemical machining process with neat sketch.

Principle: ECM removes electrically conductive work material by controlled anodic dissolution, following Faraday's laws. It is the reverse of electroplating: work is the anode (+), shaped tool is the cathode (−).

Construction: low-voltage, high-current DC supply; shaped tool and work with a small controlled gap; pump/filter/tank for conductive electrolyte; feed/servo system; insulation, enclosure and sludge/gas handling.

  1. Clamp conductive work as the positive anode and connect the shaped tool as the negative cathode.
  2. Pump filtered electrolyte through the narrow inter-electrode gap; feed the tool toward the work while maintaining the specified gap.
  3. Apply DC. Metal atoms at the anode ionize and dissolve into electrolyte; tool shape is reproduced in the work with no direct cutting contact.
  4. Flush away heat, reaction products, gas and sludge; filter/condition electrolyte and monitor gap/current.
  5. Stop at the required size; rinse/neutralise as specified and inspect dimensions and surface.

Advantages: machines hard conductive materials, complex cavities, burr-free edges, low mechanical force and negligible conventional tool wear.

Limitations: only conductive work; electrolyte corrosion/disposal and safety; high current; stray machining, overcut and gap/electrolyte control affect accuracy. Tool design and electrolyte/material compatibility are essential.

Label DC supply, tool cathode (−), work anode (+), electrolyte inlet/outlet, gap and direction of tool feed.

પ્રશ્ન: Q5 Set A(c) સ્વચ્છ આકૃતિ સાથે Electrochemical Machining (ECM) process સમજાવો.

સિદ્ધાંત: ECM Faradayના નિયમ પ્રમાણે નિયંત્રિત anodic dissolutionથી electrically conductive work material દૂર કરે. Electroplatingની ઊલટી: work anode (+), આકારવાળું tool cathode (−).

રચના: low-voltage/high-current DC supply; નાનો નિયંત્રિત gapવાળું shaped tool અને work; conductive electrolyte માટે pump/filter/tank; feed/servo; insulation, enclosure અને sludge/gas handling.

  1. Conductive workને positive anode અને shaped toolને negative cathode તરીકે જોડો.
  2. Filter કરેલું electrolyte સાંકડા gapમાંથી pump કરો; નક્કી gap જાળવી tool work તરફ feed કરો.
  3. DC આપો. Anode પર metal atom ion બની electrolyteમાં ઓગળે; સીધો cutting contact વગર toolનો આકાર workમાં બને.
  4. Heat, reaction product, gas અને sludge flush કરો; electrolyte filter/condition કરો અને gap/current જુઓ.
  5. જરૂરી sizeએ રોકો; નિર્દેશ પ્રમાણે rinse/neutralise કરી dimension અને surface તપાસો.

લાભ: કઠણ conductive material, જટિલ cavity, burr વગરની edge, ઓછું mechanical force અને સામાન્ય tool wear નગણ્ય.

મર્યાદા: ફક્ત conductive work; electrolyte corrosion/disposal અને safety; ઊંચો current; stray machining, overcut, gap/electrolyte controlથી accuracy બદલાય. Tool design અને electrolyte/material compatibility જરૂરી.

DC supply, tool cathode (−), work anode (+), electrolyte inlet/outlet, gap અને tool feed direction label કરો.

Question: Q5 Set B(c) Explain Electrical Discharge machining process with neat sketch.

Principle: EDM removes material from an electrically conductive workpiece by a sequence of controlled electrical sparks across a small gap filled with dielectric fluid. Each pulse locally heats, melts and partly vaporises material.

Construction: shaped tool electrode and conductive workpiece; pulsed DC supply; dielectric tank/pump/filter; servo feed maintaining a small spark gap; work table and flushing arrangement.

  1. Immerse/flush tool and work with suitable dielectric (commonly hydrocarbon oil for die-sinking; deionised water for wire EDM).
  2. Connect tool and work with process-specific polarity; energise pulsed supply and bring tool close while servo maintains the gap.
  3. Dielectric breaks down locally at the electric field; a spark forms, creating a hot plasma channel.
  4. Small volumes of tool/work material melt or vaporise. Pulse-off time lets the channel collapse; dielectric cools and flushes debris.
  5. Repeated sparks reproduce the electrode shape in the work (die-sinking) or cut along a programmed path with a moving wire (wire EDM).
  6. Inspect size, overcut, surface/recast layer and electrode wear; finish/flush as required.

Advantages: machines hard conductive materials and intricate cavities with little mechanical force; no conventional sharp cutting edge.

Limitations: only electrically conductive work; electrode wear, slow removal, dielectric/fire/fume and waste handling, recast/heat-affected layer and overcut must be controlled.

Label pulse supply, servo, tool electrode, spark gap, dielectric and workpiece.

પ્રશ્ન: Q5 Set B(c) સ્વચ્છ આકૃતિ સાથે Electrical Discharge Machining (EDM) process સમજાવો.

સિદ્ધાંત: EDM dielectric fluidથી ભરેલા નાના gapમાં controlled electrical sparkની શ્રેણીથી electrically conductive workpieceમાંથી material દૂર કરે. દરેક pulse સ્થાનિક રીતે ગરમ કરીને થોડું material ઓગાળે/વાષ્પ બનાવે.

રચના: shaped tool electrode અને conductive workpiece; pulsed DC supply; dielectric tank/pump/filter; નાનો spark gap જાળવતો servo feed; work table અને flushing.

  1. Tool/workને યોગ્ય dielectricમાં ડૂબાડો/flush કરો (die-sinkingમાં સામાન્ય hydrocarbon oil; wire EDMમાં deionised water).
  2. Process મુજબ polarity જોડો; pulsed supply ચાલુ કરી toolને નજીક લાવો, servo gap જાળવે.
  3. Electric fieldથી dielectric સ્થાનિક રીતે breakdown થાય; spark અને ગરમ plasma channel બને.
  4. થોડું tool/work material ઓગળે/વાષ્પ બને. Pulse-off સમયે channel બંધ થાય; dielectric ઠંડું કરી debris બહાર કાઢે.
  5. વારંવાર sparkથી workમાં electrodeનો આકાર બને (die-sinking) અથવા moving wire programmed path કાપે (wire EDM).
  6. Size, overcut, surface/recast layer અને electrode wear તપાસો; જરૂર મુજબ finish/flush કરો.

લાભ: hard conductive material અને જટિલ cavity ઓછા mechanical forceથી બને; સામાન્ય sharp cutting edge નહીં.

મર્યાદા: ફક્ત electrically conductive work; electrode wear, removal ધીમું, dielectric/fire/fume અને waste, recast/heat-affected layer તથા overcut નિયંત્રણમાં રાખવા.

Pulse supply, servo, tool electrode, spark gap, dielectric અને workpiece label કરો.
