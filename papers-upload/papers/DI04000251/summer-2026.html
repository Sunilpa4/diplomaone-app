Q1(a) Explain the applications of preferred numbers.Q1(a) Preferred numbersના ઉપયોગો સમજાવો.3 marks3 ગુણ

Preferred numbers are standard rounded values arranged in a geometric series. They reduce unnecessary variety in sizes and ratings and make products easier to design, manufacture, stock and replace.

  • Select standard shaft, pipe, power, speed, capacity and product sizes instead of inventing many arbitrary values.
  • Help manufacturers use common tooling, gauges, components and production batches, reducing cost and inventory.
  • Make parts from different makers more interchangeable and simplify maintenance/spares.
  • Provide evenly spaced choices over a wide range; Renard R5, R10, R20 and R40 have 5, 10, 20 and 40 steps per decade.

Preferred values are a selection guide, not proof that a particular component standard or catalogue offers that size. Check the relevant product standard.

Preferred numbers એટલે standard પ્રમાણે ગોઠવેલી rounded sizesની શ્રેણી. તે sizesમાં બિનજરૂરી variety ઘટાડે અને design, manufacture, stock તથા replacement સરળ કરે છે.

  • Shaft, pipe, power, speed અને capacity માટે મનસ્વી value બદલે standard value પસંદ થાય.
  • એકસરખાં tools, gauges અને components વપરાય એટલે production cost અને inventory ઘટે.
  • જુદા makerના parts બદલવા અને maintenance/spares સરળ બને.
  • મોટી rangeમાં સમાન ratioથી choices મળે; Renard R5, R10, R20, R40માં દર decadeમાં અનુક્રમે 5, 10, 20, 40 પગથિયાં હોય.

Preferred number હોવું એટલે દરેક product standardમાં તે size ઉપલબ્ધ જ હશે એવું નથી; સંબંધિત standard/catalogue તપાસો.

Q1(b) Determine the standard sizes of six round bars from 20 mm to 64 mm.Q1(b) 20 mmથી 64 mm સુધીના છ ગોળ સળિયાના standard sizes શોધો.4 marks4 ગુણ

There are six sizes including both end values, so there are five equal geometric intervals. Let the common ratio be q.

q = (largest / smallest)^(1/(n−1)) = (64/20)^(1/5) = 1.2619

Generate the geometric progression:

20,  20q = 25.24,  20q² = 31.85,  20q³ = 40.19,  20q⁴ = 50.72,  64 mm

Round to the nearest usual preferred-number sizes in this range:

20, 25, 31.5, 40, 50, 63 mm

These are the standard rounded R10 choices (approximately a 1.26 ratio). Since 64 mm is the stated upper limit, 63 mm is the largest preferred size not exceeding it. If 64 mm is a strict required finished diameter rather than a limit, do not round it down—confirm the applicable bar standard or choose the next suitable size and recheck the constraint.

Q1(c) Define factor of safety and explain factors affecting it.Q1(c) Factor of safetyની વ્યાખ્યા આપો અને તેને અસર કરતા પરિબળો સમજાવો.7 marks7 ગુણ

Factor of safety (FOS), n is the ratio of an appropriate limiting strength to the allowable/design stress for a stated failure criterion.

n = limiting strength / allowable stress
allowable stress = limiting strength / n

For a ductile material under a stated static tensile-yield check, use yield strength as the limiting strength. For brittle materials, fatigue, shear or buckling, use the corresponding strength and criterion—not automatically the same tensile ultimate strength.

Factors affecting the selected FOS:

  1. Uncertainty in load magnitude, direction, impact, vibration and load cycles.
  2. Variation in material properties, defects, heat treatment and supplier quality.
  3. Accuracy of stress calculations, geometry, stress concentration and design assumptions.
  4. Failure mode: brittle fracture, yielding, fatigue, wear, buckling, corrosion or temperature.
  5. Consequences of failure, risk to people/equipment and required reliability.
  6. Manufacturing quality, inspection/maintenance access and service environment.
  7. Applicable codes, standards, experience and economic limits.

Use a justified value and apply it once in a consistent design method. A large FOS cannot correct a wrong load path or omitted bending moment.

Factor of safety (FOS), n એટલે નક્કી કરેલા failure criterion માટેનું limiting strength અને allowable/design stressનો ગુણોત્તર.

n = limiting strength / allowable stress
allowable stress = limiting strength / n

Ductile materialના static tensile-yield checkમાં yield strength લો. Brittle fracture, fatigue, shear કે buckling માટે તે failureને અનુરૂપ strength અને criterion લો; દરેક વખતે tensile ultimate strength જ ન લો.

FOSની પસંદગી પર અસર કરતા મુદ્દા:

  1. Loadનું પ્રમાણ/દિશા કેટલી ચોક્કસ છે; impact, vibration અને load cycles.
  2. Material properties, defects, heat treatment અને supplier qualityમાં ફેરફાર.
  3. Stress calculationની ચોકસાઈ, geometry, stress concentration અને ધારણાઓ.
  4. Failureનો પ્રકાર: brittle fracture, yielding, fatigue, wear, buckling, corrosion કે temperature.
  5. Failureના પરિણામો અને જરૂરી reliability.
  6. Manufacturing quality, inspection/maintenance અને service environment.
  7. લાગુ code/standard, અનુભવ અને ખર્ચની મર્યાદા.

કારણ સાથે યોગ્ય FOS લો અને એક જ consistent methodથી લાગુ કરો. બહુ મોટો FOS ખોટા load path કે ભૂલાયેલા bending momentને સુધારી શકતો નથી.

Q1(c) OR · Define machine design and explain factors affecting design of a machine element.Q1(c) OR · Machine designની વ્યાખ્યા આપો અને machine elementના designને અસર કરતા પરિબળો સમજાવો.7 marks7 ગુણ · વિકલ્પ

Machine design is the systematic process of deciding the form, material, dimensions and arrangement of machine parts so that the machine performs its intended function safely, reliably and economically throughout its required life.

Factors affecting a machine-element design:

  • Function, motion, interfaces and required performance.
  • Magnitude, direction and type of loads; stress, fatigue, deflection, vibration, wear and factor of safety.
  • Material strength, stiffness, toughness, corrosion/temperature resistance and availability.
  • Manufacturing method, standard sizes, tolerances, surface finish and assembly/interchangeability.
  • Operating environment, lubrication, maintenance, inspection and expected service life.
  • Safety, ergonomics, reliability, weight/space limits and applicable standards.
  • Cost of material, production, operation and repair.

Design is iterative: analyse the load path, select material, size the part, check failure and stiffness, then adjust to a practical standard size and verify again.

Machine design એટલે machineના ભાગોનું shape, material, dimensions અને arrangement એવી રીતે નક્કી કરવાની પદ્ધતિ કે machine જરૂરી function સુરક્ષિત રીતે, વિશ્વસનીય રીતે અને યોગ્ય ખર્ચે, નિર્ધારિત life સુધી કરે.

Machine elementના designને અસર કરતા મુદ્દા:

  • ભાગનું function, motion, જોડાણ અને જરૂરી performance.
  • Loadનું પ્રમાણ, દિશા અને પ્રકાર; stress, fatigue, deflection, vibration, wear અને FOS.
  • Materialની strength, stiffness, toughness, corrosion/temperature સામે પ્રતિકાર અને ઉપલબ્ધતા.
  • Manufacturing method, standard sizes, tolerances, surface finish, assembly અને interchangeability.
  • વાતાવરણ, lubrication, maintenance, inspection અને service life.
  • Safety, ergonomics, reliability, weight/space અને લાગુ standards.
  • Material, production, operation અને repairનો ખર્ચ.

Design વારંવાર ચકાસવાનો process છે: load path સમજો, material પસંદ કરો, dimensions નક્કી કરો, failure/stiffness તપાસો, practical standard size લો અને ફરીથી verify કરો.

Q2 Set A(a) List applications of a knuckle joint.Q2 Set A(a) Knuckle jointના ઉપયોગો લખો.3 marks3 ગુણ

A knuckle joint connects two coaxial members carrying mainly axial tension while allowing a small angular movement. Typical applications include:

  • Tie rods and tension links in structures or mechanisms.
  • Links in suspension/chain and valve or pump-rod mechanisms where slight articulation is needed.
  • Connecting a piston rod to a crosshead or joining two rod ends in a linkage.

It is not a torque-transmitting shaft coupling. Compressive use needs a separate buckling/alignment check.

Knuckle joint બે એક-અક્ષીય membersને મુખ્યત્વે axial tensionમાં જોડે છે અને થોડું angular movement થવા દે છે. ઉપયોગો:

  • Structure અથવા mechanismના tie rods અને tension links.
  • થોડું articulation જરૂરી હોય તેવી suspension/chain link અથવા valve/pump-rod mechanism.
  • કેટલીક રચનામાં piston rodને crosshead સાથે અથવા linkageમાં બે rod ends જોડવા.

આ torque આપતો shaft coupling નથી. Compressionમાં વાપરો તો buckling અને alignment અલગથી તપાસો.

Q2 Set A(b) Explain the failures in a riveted joint.Q2 Set A(b) Riveted jointમાં થતા failures સમજાવો.4 marks4 ગુણ

The joint can fail through the plate or the rivets. Check each mode on the same pitch basis:

  1. Plate tearing in tension: plate tears across the net section through rivet holes. For one row, Pt = (p − dh) t σt.
  2. Rivet shearing: the rivet shank shears across one plane in a lap/single-cover joint, or usually two planes in a double-cover butt joint. Use actual number of rivets and shear planes.
  3. Crushing / bearing: excessive contact pressure crushes the rivet or plate around the hole; use projected area drt, and check main plate and covers.
  4. Edge tear-out / diagonal tearing: a short edge ligament or staggered-hole path can tear out. Check the actual edge distance and transverse pitch.

The joint's elementary strength is the least relevant capacity; efficiency is joint strength divided by the solid plate strength for the same pitch.

Riveted joint plate અથવા rivetsમાંથી નિષ્ફળ થઈ શકે. દરેક failureને સમાન pitch આધાર પર તપાસો:

  1. Plate tearing in tension: rivet holesમાંથી પસાર થતા net section પર plate ફાટે. એક row માટે Pt = (p − dh) t σt.
  2. Rivet shearing: lap/single-cover jointમાં rivet સામાન્ય રીતે એક shear plane પર અને double-cover butt jointમાં સામાન્ય રીતે બે plane પર કપાય. Rivetની સંખ્યા અને planes સાચી ગણો.
  3. Crushing / bearing: holeની આસપાસ વધુ દબાણથી rivet અથવા plate દબાઈ/ચપટી થાય. Projected area drt લો; main plate અને covers બંને તપાસો.
  4. Edge tear-out / diagonal tearing: edge સુધીનો ligament ઓછો હોય અથવા zigzag holesથી ત્રાંસી line બને તો plate ફાટી શકે. Actual edge distance અને transverse pitch તપાસો.

જોઈન્ટની સરળ strength એટલે લાગુ પડતી capacitiesમાં સૌથી નાની. Efficiency = તે strength / સમાન pitchની આખી solid plateની strength.

Q2 Set A(c) Design the cotter joint dimensions for a 20 kN axial load.Q2 Set A(c) 20 kN axial load માટે cotter jointના માપો નક્કી કરો.7 marks7 ગુણ

Given: P = 20 kN = 20,000 N; σt = 70 MPa; τ = 40 MPa; σcr = 150 MPa. Use N and mm, so MPa = N/mm².

1. Rod diameter from tension

P = (π/4)d²σt ⇒ d = √[4P/(πσt)] = √[80,000/(π×70)] = 19.07 mm

Choose a practical standard rod diameter d = 20 mm.

2. Spigot diameter and cotter thickness Use the spigot's net tension area across the cotter slot and its bearing area against the cotter:

P = σt(πds²/4 − ds t)   ;   P = σcr ds t

From the bearing equation, ds t = P/σcr = 133.33 mm². Hence πds²/4 = P/σt + P/σcr = 285.71 + 133.33 = 419.05 mm².

ds = 23.10 mm;   t = 133.33/23.10 = 5.77 mm

Adopt spigot ds = 24 mm and cotter t = 6 mm. Recheck: spigot net tensile capacity = 70[π(24²)/4 − 24×6] = 21.59 kN; crushing capacity = 150×24×6 = 21.60 kN. Both exceed 20 kN.

Shear check / cotter breadth: cotter is in double shear, P = 2btτ. Required b = 20,000/(2×6×40) = 41.67 mm; adopt at least b = 45 mm, giving τ = 37.04 MPa. Socket diameter, collars and end ligaments were not requested and need separate checks before treating the complete joint as final.

Q2 Set B(a) List applications of a cotter joint.Q2 Set B(a) Cotter jointના ઉપયોગો લખો.3 marks · alternative set3 ગુણ · વિકલ્પ

A cotter joint connects two co-axial rods carrying axial tensile or compressive force when torque transmission is not required. Applications include:

  • Piston rod to crosshead connection in a reciprocating engine/pump.
  • Connecting a strap end to a connecting rod in suitable mechanisms.
  • Joining valve rods, pump rods, tie rods or foundation/structural rods that need detachable axial connection.

The cotter is a tapered flat wedge; it can be driven out for dismantling. The joint is not intended to transmit shaft torque or allow free angular articulation.

Cotter joint બે એક-અક્ષીય rodsને axial tension અથવા compressionમાં જોડે છે; torque આપવાનું કામ નથી.

  • Reciprocating engine/pumpમાં piston rod અને crossheadનું જોડાણ.
  • યોગ્ય mechanismમાં strap end અને connecting rod જોડવા.
  • Valve rods, pump rods, tie rods અથવા foundation/structural rodsનું detachable axial joint.

Cotter ઢાળવાળો flat wedge છે; તેને બહાર કાઢીને joint ખોલી શકાય. આ joint shaft torque આપવા કે freely angular movement માટે નથી.

Q2 Set B(c) Find pitch and efficiency of a double-riveted double-cover butt joint.Q2 Set B(c) Double-riveted double-cover butt jointની pitch અને efficiency શોધો.7 marks7 ગુણ

Ps = 4(πd²/4)τ = 4(π×20²/4)×80 = 100.53 kN

Crushing capacity of main plate:

Pc = 2dtσcr = 2×20×12×160 = 76.80 kN

Section modulus is Z = I/c, where I is the second moment of area about the centroidal bending axis and c is the distance from that axis to the extreme fibre. Units are mm³.

Q5 Set A(a) List advantages of anti-friction bearings.Q5 Set A(a) Anti-friction bearingsના ફાયદા લખો.3 marks3 ગુણ
  • Low starting friction and generally low friction during normal rolling operation.
  • Standardised, replaceable sizes make selection, installation and maintenance convenient.
  • Need relatively little lubricant compared with many sliding-bearing arrangements.
  • Can support radial, thrust or combined loads using suitable ball/roller types.
  • Compact, predictable running and low heat/power loss under appropriate load and speed.
  • Can be selected with seals/shields for contamination control.

They are not frictionless: fatigue life, shock, contamination, noise, speed and temperature limits still matter.

Q5 Set A(c) State Lamé theory and design the hydraulic-press cylinder.Q5 Set A(c) Lamé theory જણાવો અને hydraulic-press cylinder design કરો.7 marks7 ગુણ

Lamé theory: for a thick, long, elastic cylinder under axisymmetric pressure, radial and circumferential (hoop) stresses vary with radius r:

σr = A − B/r²;   σθ = A + B/r²

For internal pressure pi and zero external pressure, with internal radius ri and outer radius ro:

A = pi ri²/(ro² − ri²);   B = pi ri²ro²/(ro² − ri²)
Maximum hoop stress occurs at the inner wall: σθi = pi(ro² + ri²)/(ro² − ri²)

Given: internal diameter = 300 mm ⇒ ri = 150 mm; pi = 20 MPa; allowable hoop stress σθi = 40 MPa.

40 = 20(ro² + 150²)/(ro² − 150²)
2(ro² − 150²) = ro² + 150² ⇒ ro² = 3(150²)
ro = 259.81 mm;   wall thickness t = ro − ri = 109.81 mm
outside diameter Do = 2ro = 519.62 mm

Theoretical thickness ≈ 110 mm; outside diameter ≈ 520 mm.

This uses the inner-wall hoop-stress limit, zero external pressure and Lamé elastic model. A real pressure cylinder also needs code allowances, end/head design, material, weld/joint efficiency, fatigue and corrosion checks.
