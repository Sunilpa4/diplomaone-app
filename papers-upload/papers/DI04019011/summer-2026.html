Q1(a) List industrial applications of heat exchangers.Q1(a) ઉદ્યોગોમાં heat exchangersના ઉપયોગો લખો.3 marks3 ગુણ

Definition: A heat exchanger transfers heat between two fluids at different temperatures, normally without allowing them to mix.

  • Power plants/boilers: economiser heats feedwater; superheater raises steam temperature; condenser removes exhaust-steam heat.
  • Refrigeration and air-conditioning: evaporator and condenser transfer heat to/from the refrigerant.
  • Automotive: radiator, oil cooler and charge-air cooler.
  • Process industries: heaters, coolers, condensers and waste-heat recovery in chemical, petroleum and textile plants.
  • Food/dairy: pasteurisation and product cooling; also domestic water heaters.

For marks, name the equipment and state which stream is heated or cooled.

વ્યાખ્યા: જુદા તાપમાનવાળા બે પ્રવાહી વચ્ચે ગરમી આપ-લે કરતું સાધન heat exchanger છે; સામાન્ય રીતે બંને પ્રવાહી મિક્સ થતા નથી.

  • Power plant/boiler: economiser feedwater ગરમ કરે; superheater steamનું તાપમાન વધારે; condenser exhaust steamની ગરમી કાઢે.
  • Refrigeration/AC: evaporator અને condenser refrigerant સાથે heat exchange કરે.
  • Automobile: radiator, oil cooler અને charge-air cooler.
  • Process industries: chemical, petroleum અને textileમાં heater, cooler, condenser અને waste-heat recovery.
  • Food/dairy: pasteurisation અને product cooling; domestic water heater પણ.

Q1(b) Explain construction and working of a single-stage reciprocating air compressor.Q1(b) Single-stage reciprocating air compressorની રચના અને કાર્ય સમજાવો.4 marks4 ગુણ

Main parts: cylinder, piston, suction valve, delivery valve, connecting rod, crankshaft, flywheel and cooling arrangement. It is a positive-displacement machine.

  1. Suction stroke: piston moves away from the cylinder head; cylinder pressure falls slightly below inlet pressure, so the suction valve opens and air enters.
  2. Compression stroke: piston returns; both valves are closed and trapped air is compressed, raising its pressure and temperature.
  3. Delivery: when cylinder pressure becomes slightly greater than receiver pressure, the delivery valve opens and compressed air leaves.
  4. The cycle repeats. A small clearance is required mechanically; the ideal calculations in this paper neglect its effect unless stated.

Label the two valves and show piston motion on a neat sketch.

મુખ્ય ભાગો: cylinder, piston, suction valve, delivery valve, connecting rod, crankshaft, flywheel અને cooling arrangement. આ positive-displacement machine છે.

  1. Suction stroke: piston cylinder headથી દૂર જાય; cylinder pressure inlet કરતાં થોડું ઓછું થાય, suction valve ખૂલે અને હવા અંદર આવે.
  2. Compression stroke: piston પાછો ફરે; બંને valve બંધ હોય, trapped હવાનો pressure અને temperature વધે.
  3. Delivery: cylinder pressure receiver pressure કરતાં થોડો વધારે થાય ત્યારે delivery valve ખૂલે અને compressed air બહાર જાય.
  4. Cycle ફરી ચાલે. યાંત્રિક રીતે નાનું clearance રહે છે; પ્રશ્નમાં ન આપ્યું હોય તો ideal numericalમાં તેની અસર અવગણીએ.

Q1(c) State why steam dryness fraction is measured and explain one method.Q1(c) Steam dryness fraction શા માટે માપે છે? એક પદ્ધતિ સમજાવો.7 marks7 ગુણ

Meaning: Dryness fraction x = mass of dry saturated vapour / total mass of wet steam. It lies between 0 and 1 for wet steam.

Why measure it? It tells how much liquid water is carried with steam; it is needed to find steam enthalpy/quality, check boiler performance, estimate turbine moisture/erosion risk and calculate heat supplied.

Throttling calorimeter: Take a representative steam sample through a small throttle into a lower-pressure chamber. Throttling is approximately adiabatic with no shaft work, so h1 = h2. Measure downstream pressure and temperature. If the outlet is superheated, obtain h2 from superheated-steam data.

At inlet: h1 = hf1 + x hfg1
At outlet: h2 = hg2 + cp(T2 − Tsat2)
x = (h2 − hf1) / hfg1

Limitation: outlet must be superheated for a direct throttling test. Very wet steam may need a separating calorimeter followed by throttling; state the limitation for full credit.

અર્થ: Dryness fraction x = wet steamમાં dry saturated vapourનું mass / કુલ wet-steam mass. Wet steam માટે x 0 અને 1 વચ્ચે છે.

શા માટે માપીએ? Steam સાથે કેટલું પાણી જાય છે તે જાણવા; steamની enthalpy/quality, boiler performance અને turbineમાં ભેજથી થતું erosion તપાસવા; heat ગણવા.

Throttling calorimeter: Steamનો નમૂનો નાના throttleમાંથી ઓછા દબાણવાળા chamberમાં મોકલો. Throttling લગભગ adiabatic છે, shaft work નથી, તેથી h1 = h2. બહારનું pressure અને temperature માપો. Outlet superheated હોય તો તેની h2 tableમાંથી મેળવો.

Inlet: h1 = hf1 + x hfg1
Outlet: h2 = hg2 + cp(T2 − Tsat2)
x = (h2 − hf1) / hfg1

મર્યાદા: સીધી throttling test માટે outlet superheated હોવું જરૂરી છે. બહુ ભીની steam માટે પહેલા separating calorimeter પછી throttling વાપરો; આ limitation લખશો.

Given: P = 6 bar; Tsat = 158.8°C; hf = 670.4; hfg = 2085.1; hg = 2755.5 kJ/kg; cp,superheat = 2.3 kJ/(kg·K). Initial water is at 25°C. Since h25 is not supplied, take liquid-water cp = 4.187 kJ/(kg·K).

h25 ≈ cp,wT = 4.187 × 25 = 104.675 kJ/kg

Step 2 — wet steam, x = 0.9: first find the final wet-steam enthalpy, then subtract the starting-water enthalpy.

hwet = hf + xhfg
= 670.4 + 0.9(2085.1) = 2546.99 kJ/kg
Q = m(hwet − h25) = 1(2546.99 − 104.675) = 2442.32 kJ

Step 3 — dry saturated steam:

Q = 1(hg − h25) = 2755.5 − 104.675 = 2650.83 kJ

Step 4 — superheated steam at 250°C: first heat from saturation temperature to 250°C.

Q2 Set A(a) Find compressor and turbine efficiencies of the given Brayton plant.Q2 Set A(a) આપેલા Brayton plantની compressor અને turbine efficiency શોધો.3 marks3 ગુણ

Given: T1=15°C, pressure ratio rp=6, T2,a=242°C, T3=727°C, T4,a=366°C, γ=1.4. Use kelvin in temperature ratios.

  1. Convert: T1=288.15 K; T2,a=515.15 K; T3=1000.15 K; T4,a=639.15 K.
  2. Find isentropic compressor outlet: T2s=T1rp(γ−1)/γ=288.15×60.2857=480.78 K.
  3. Compressor efficiency: ηc=(T2s−T1)/(T2,a−T1)=(480.78−288.15)/(515.15−288.15)=0.8486.
  4. Find isentropic turbine outlet: T4s=T3/rp(γ−1)/γ=1000.15/60.2857=599.43 K.
  5. Turbine efficiency: ηt=(T3−T4,a)/(T3−T4s)=(1000.15−639.15)/(1000.15−599.43)=0.9009.

Compressor efficiency ≈ 84.9%; turbine efficiency ≈ 90.1%.

Exam tip: use actual-minus-inlet for a compressor, but turbine inlet-minus-actual outlet. Use absolute temperatures for the isentropic pressure relation.

  4. Isentropic turbine outlet: T4s=T3/rp(γ−1)/γ=1000.15/60.2857=599.43 K.

Q2 Set A(b) Describe parallel-flow and counter-flow heat exchangers with sketches.Q2 Set A(b) Parallel-flow અને counter-flow heat exchangers આકૃતિ સાથે સમજાવો.4 marks4 ગુણ

Parallel flow: hot and cold fluids enter at the same end and travel in the same direction. Their temperature difference is large near the inlet and falls along the exchanger; the cold stream outlet cannot exceed the hot-stream outlet in an ordinary exchanger.

Counter flow: the fluids enter from opposite ends and travel in opposite directions. The temperature difference is more uniform, so for the same area it generally transfers more heat; the cold outlet may exceed the hot outlet temperature (but cannot exceed the hot inlet temperature).

Both may be direct-contact or, more commonly, separated by a wall. Draw two passages and show arrows, inlet/outlet temperatures and hot/cold labels.

Parallel flow: ગરમ અને ઠંડા પ્રવાહી એક જ છેડેથી દાખલ થઈ એક જ દિશામાં ચાલે. Inlet પાસે temperature difference મોટો અને આગળ ઘટતો જાય; સામાન્ય exchangerમાં cold outlet, hot outlet કરતાં વધુ ન થાય.

Counter flow: બંને પ્રવાહી વિરુદ્ધ છેડેથી દાખલ થઈ સામસામે દિશામાં ચાલે. Temperature difference વધુ સમાન રહે એટલે એકસરખા areaમાં સામાન્ય રીતે વધુ heat transfer થાય; cold outlet hot outletથી વધુ ગરમ થઈ શકે, પણ hot inletથી વધુ નહીં.

બંને પ્રવાહ wallથી અલગ અથવા direct-contact હોઈ શકે; સામાન્ય રીતે wallથી અલગ હોય છે. બે passage, arrows, inlet/outlet temperature અને hot/cold labels સાથે sketch દોરો.

Q2 Set A(c) Find equivalent evaporation and factor of evaporation for the boiler.Q2 Set A(c) Boilerનું equivalent evaporation અને factor of evaporation શોધો.7 marks7 ગુણ

Given per 1 kg coal: ms=3.6 kg; hs=2776.2 kJ/kg; hfw=134 kJ/kg; latent heat at 100°C, L=2257 kJ/kg.

  1. Useful heat to each kg of steam: Δh=hs−hfw=2776.2−134=2642.2 kJ/kg.
  2. Factor of evaporation: F=Δh/L=2642.2/2257=1.1707. It is a ratio, so it has no unit.
  3. Equivalent evaporation: E=msF=3.6×1.1707=4.214 kg from and at 100°C per kg coal.

Factor of evaporation = 1.171; equivalent evaporation = 4.214 kg/kg coal.

Why this works: divide the actual heat absorbed by 1 kg steam by the heat needed to evaporate 1 kg water at 100°C, then multiply by actual steam per kg fuel.

1 kg coal માટે આપેલ: ms=3.6 kg; hs=2776.2 kJ/kg; hfw=134 kJ/kg; 100°C પર latent heat L=2257 kJ/kg.

Q2 Set B(a) Find ideal Brayton-cycle net power for 1 kg/s air.Q2 Set B(a) 1 kg/s હવા માટે ideal Brayton cycleની net power શોધો.3 marks3 ગુણ

Given: P1=1 bar, T1=15°C=288.15 K; pressure ratio rp=5; T3=800°C=1073.15 K; cp=1 kJ/(kg·K); γ=1.4; mass flow=1 kg/s. Ideal compressor and turbine are isentropic.

Q2 Set B(c) Explain natural and mechanical boiler draught with advantages and limitations.Q2 Set B(c) Boilerમાં natural અને mechanical draught લાભ-મર્યાદા સાથે સમજાવો.7 marks7 ગુણ

Draught is the pressure difference that supplies air to the furnace and carries flue gas through the boiler and chimney.

Natural draught: a chimney creates it because hot, lighter flue gas has lower density than outside air. Height and gas/ambient temperature difference control the draught.

  • Advantages: no fan power; simple, quiet, low maintenance.
  • Limitations: depends on weather and chimney height; response/control is limited; tall chimney costs more; not suitable for very high firing rates.

Mechanical draught: fans create/control flow. Forced draught (FD) fan pushes air into the furnace; induced draught (ID) fan draws flue gas out and keeps furnace slightly below atmospheric pressure; balanced draught uses both.

  • Advantages: compact, controllable, reliable high firing rate and better combustion control.
  • Limitations: fan power, capital/maintenance, noise; failure can upset combustion. FD systems need good sealing to avoid furnace gas leakage outward.

In an exam diagram, label air, furnace, boiler gas path, FD/ID fan and chimney.

Draught એ pressure difference છે જે furnaceમાં હવા લાવે અને boiler/chimneyમાંથી flue gas બહાર લઈ જાય.

Natural draught: chimneyથી બને. ગરમ flue gas હલકો હોવાથી તેની density બહારની હવા કરતાં ઓછી હોય; chimneyની ઊંચાઈ અને gas/બહારના તાપમાનનો તફાવત draught નક્કી કરે.

  • લાભ: fan power નહીં; સરળ, શાંત, ઓછું maintenance.
  • મર્યાદા: હવામાન અને chimney height પર આધારિત; control મર્યાદિત; ઊંચી chimney ખર્ચાળ; બહુ મોટા firing rate માટે યોગ્ય નહીં.

Mechanical draught: fanથી flow બને/control થાય. Forced draught (FD) fan હવાને furnaceમાં ધકેલે; induced draught (ID) fan flue gas ખેંચે અને furnaceને થોડું negative pressureમાં રાખે; બંને હોય તો balanced draught.

  • લાભ: compact, control સારું, ઊંચો firing rate અને combustion control.
  • મર્યાદા: fan power, ખર્ચ/maintenance, noise; fan fail થાય તો combustion બગડે. FDમાં leakage outward રોકવા સારા seals જોઈએ.

Q3 · Brayton cycle, radiation, boiler performance and insulationપ્ર.3 · Brayton cycle, radiation, boiler performance અને insulation

Q3 Set A(a) Explain the Brayton cycle briefly.Q3 Set A(a) Brayton cycle ટૂંકમાં સમજાવો.3 marks3 ગુણ

The ideal Brayton (Joule) cycle is the air-standard cycle for a gas-turbine plant. It has four internally reversible processes:

  1. 1–2: isentropic compression in compressor.
  2. 2–3: heat addition at constant pressure in combustion/heater.
  3. 3–4: isentropic expansion in turbine; turbine produces work.
  4. 4–1: heat rejection at constant pressure to close the ideal cycle.

Net work = turbine work − compressor work. A real open gas turbine has intake and exhaust rather than a literal closed loop, but the ideal air-standard model is drawn as a closed cycle.

Ideal Brayton (Joule) cycle gas-turbine plantનો air-standard cycle છે. તેમાં ચાર internally reversible process છે:

  1. 1–2: compressorમાં isentropic compression.
  2. 2–3: combustion/heaterમાં constant-pressure heat addition.
  3. 3–4: turbineમાં isentropic expansion; turbine work આપે.
  4. 4–1: ideal cycle બંધ કરવા constant-pressure heat rejection.

Net work = turbine work − compressor work. Real open gas turbineમાં intake/exhaust હોય છે; ideal air-standard modelને closed cycle તરીકે દોરીએ.

Q3 Set A(b) Explain the black-body concept in radiation heat transfer.Q3 Set A(b) Radiation heat transferમાં black-bodyની સંકલ્પના સમજાવો.4 marks4 ગુણ

A black body is an ideal surface that absorbs all incident radiation at every wavelength and direction: absorptivity α=1, reflectivity ρ=0 and transmissivity τ=0. At a given temperature it emits the maximum possible thermal radiation; emissivity ε=1.

  • It is an ideal standard, not necessarily a black-coloured real object.
  • A small hole in a large, isothermal cavity is a close practical approximation: radiation entering the hole reflects repeatedly and is almost completely absorbed.
  • For a black body, emissive power is Eb=σT⁴ (Stefan–Boltzmann law), with T in kelvin and σ=5.67×10⁻⁸ W/(m²·K⁴).
  • Real surfaces have ε<1; for a surface in large surroundings, net radiation is approximately εσA(Ts⁴−Tsur⁴).

At thermal equilibrium, Kirchhoff's law gives ε=α for the same wavelength and conditions.

Black body આદર્શ સપાટી છે જે દરેક wavelength અને દિશામાંથી પડતું radiation સંપૂર્ણ શોષે: absorptivity α=1, reflectivity ρ=0, transmissivity τ=0. આપેલા તાપમાને તે મહત્તમ thermal radiation છોડે; emissivity ε=1.

  • આ ideal standard છે; દરેક કાળી દેખાતી વસ્તુ black body નથી.
  • મોટા સમતાપી cavityમાં નાનું છિદ્ર તેની સારી નજીકની નકલ છે: અંદર આવેલ radiation વારંવાર reflect થઈ લગભગ શોષાઈ જાય.
  • Black body માટે Eb=σT⁴ (Stefan–Boltzmann law); T kelvinમાં અને σ=5.67×10⁻⁸ W/(m²·K⁴).
  • વાસ્તવિક સપાટી માટે ε<1; મોટા surroundingsમાં net radiation આશરે εσA(Ts⁴−Tsur⁴).

Thermal equilibriumમાં સમાન wavelength અને પરિસ્થિતિ માટે Kirchhoff law પ્રમાણે ε=α.

A jet condenser is a direct-contact condenser. Exhaust steam and cooling water mix in a chamber maintained under vacuum.

  1. Cooling water enters through a spray/nozzle and contacts exhaust steam directly.
  2. Steam condenses, rejecting latent heat to the water; the mixture temperature rises.

It is simple and compact but condensate is mixed with cooling water, so the water is normally not suitable as clean boiler feed without treatment.

Q4 Set A(c) Why is steam-turbine compounding needed? Explain one type with sketch.Q4 Set A(c) Steam turbineમાં compounding શા માટે? એક પ્રકાર આકૃતિ સાથે સમજાવો.7 marks7 ગુણ

Why: a large pressure drop in one impulse stage produces very high steam velocity and impractically high rotor speed. Compounding divides the energy/velocity change among stages so shaft speed and blade loading become manageable.

Velocity compounding (Curtis stage):

  1. Steam expands in one nozzle; pressure falls and velocity rises.
  2. It passes through the first moving-blade row; part of its kinetic energy turns the rotor and velocity falls.
  3. Fixed guide blades reverse/re-direct the flow with little pressure change.
  4. Further moving rows extract more kinetic energy; exit velocity is lower.

Pros: one major pressure drop/nozzle group, compact and lower rotor speed than a single un-compounded impulse stage. Cons: friction and windage in many blade rows reduce efficiency; pressure drop is concentrated in the nozzle.

  4. આગળની moving rows વધુ kinetic energy લે; exit velocity ઓછી થાય.

Q4 Set B(a) Define a steam condenser and state its functions.Q4 Set B(a) Steam condenserની વ્યાખ્યા અને કાર્યો લખો.3 marks3 ગુણ

A steam condenser is a heat exchanger that removes latent heat from turbine exhaust steam and converts it into water, usually at pressure below atmospheric pressure.

  • Creates/maintains vacuum at turbine exhaust, increasing expansion and turbine work.
  • Recovers condensate for reuse as boiler feedwater.
  • Reduces exhaust back pressure and improves plant thermal efficiency.
  • Removes air and other non-condensable gases to protect heat transfer and reduce corrosion.

Steam condenser એવો heat exchanger છે જે turbine exhaust steamમાંથી latent heat કાઢી તેને પાણીમાં ફેરવે; સામાન્ય રીતે દબાણ atmospheric કરતાં ઓછું રહે.

  • Turbine exhaust પર vacuum જાળવે, expansion અને turbine work વધારે.
  • Condensateને boiler feedwater તરીકે પાછું વાપરવા મળે.
  • Exhaust back pressure ઘટાડે અને plantની thermal efficiency સુધારે.
  • Air/non-condensable gas કાઢે જેથી heat transfer સારું રહે અને corrosion ઘટે.
Q4 Set B(b) Explain construction and working of a centrifugal air compressor.Q4 Set B(b) Centrifugal air compressorની રચના અને કાર્ય સમજાવો.4 marks4 ગુણ

Parts: inlet, rotating impeller with vanes, diffuser, volute casing, shaft/bearings and (for high pressure) multiple stages with intercoolers.

  1. Air enters axially at the impeller eye.
  2. Rotating vanes add energy and throw air radially outward; absolute velocity and pressure rise.
  3. In the diffuser, passage area increases and velocity falls; much of velocity energy converts to static pressure.
  4. Volute collects and delivers air to the receiver or next stage. Repeat in further stages if required.

It gives continuous, high-volume flow with few rubbing parts. Performance is sensitive to speed and surge/choke limits; do not call the diffuser a moving part.

  1. હવા impellerના eyeમાં axial રીતે પ્રવેશે.
  2. ફરતી vanes energy આપે અને હવાને radial બહાર ફેંકે; velocity તથા pressure વધે.
  3. Diffuserમાં passage મોટો થાય અને velocity ઘટે; velocity energyનો મોટો ભાગ static pressure બને.
  4. Volute હવા ભેગી કરી receiver અથવા આગળના stageમાં મોકલે. જરૂર મુજબ આગળના stagesમાં પુનરાવર્તન.

Q4 Set B(c) Describe types of steam nozzles, sketches and applications.Q4 Set B(c) Steam nozzlesના પ્રકાર, આકૃતિ અને ઉપયોગો સમજાવો.7 marks7 ગુણ

A steam nozzle converts pressure/enthalpy into kinetic energy and directs a high-velocity jet onto turbine blades.

  • Convergent: area decreases toward exit. It accelerates subsonic steam; at the throat flow may become critical/choked. Used where required pressure ratio is modest or where sonic throat flow is sufficient.
  • Divergent: area increases. For supersonic inlet flow it can further accelerate the flow; not a general subsonic-accelerating nozzle by itself.
  • Convergent-divergent (C-D): area first decreases to a throat, then increases. Subsonic steam accelerates to sonic at the throat and, with sufficient pressure ratio and correct back pressure, expands supersonically in the divergent part. Widely used for high-pressure-ratio steam-turbine nozzles.

Application: impulse turbine nozzles produce the high-speed jet; nozzle banks distribute steam to blade rows. Label inlet, throat, exit and flow direction on the profiles.

Steam nozzle pressure/enthalpyને kinetic energyમાં ફેરવે અને turbine blade તરફ high-velocity jet મોકલે.

  • Convergent: exit તરફ area ઘટે. Subsonic steam ઝડપે; throat પર flow critical/choked થઈ શકે. ઓછા જરૂરી pressure ratio અથવા sonic throat પૂરતું હોય ત્યાં વપરાય.
  • Divergent: area વધે. Supersonic inlet flowને વધુ ઝડપી કરી શકે; એકલું divergent nozzle દરેક subsonic પ્રવાહને ઝડપી કરતું નથી.
  • Convergent-divergent (C-D): પહેલાં area ઘટીને throat, પછી વધે. Subsonic steam throat પર sonic બને; પૂરતો pressure ratio અને યોગ્ય back pressure હોય તો divergent ભાગમાં supersonic expand થાય. High pressure-ratio steam turbineમાં સામાન્ય.

ઉપયોગ: impulse turbine nozzle high-speed jet બનાવે; nozzle bank steamને blade rowsમાં વહેંચે. Profilesમાં inlet, throat, exit અને flow direction label કરો.

Q5 Set B(b) What are boiler mountings? Explain one in detail.Q5 Set B(b) Boiler mountings શું છે? એકનું વિગતવાર વર્ણન કરો.4 marks4 ગુણ

Boiler mountings are essential fittings mounted on a boiler for safe operation, control and indication. Examples: safety valve, water-level indicator, pressure gauge, steam stop valve, feed check valve, blow-off cock and fusible plug.

Spring-loaded safety valve (one mounting):

  • Parts: valve and seat, spindle, spring, adjusting screw/cap and discharge outlet.
  • Normal pressure: spring force holds the valve on its seat.
  • Set pressure reached: steam force on the valve area exceeds the spring force; the valve lifts and steam escapes.
  • Pressure falls: spring pushes the valve back onto the seat.
  • Purpose: prevents boiler pressure from exceeding the safe working limit; it must be correctly set and never isolated during operation.

Q5 Set B(c) State effects of air leakage in steam condensers and explain one detection method.Q5 Set B(c) Steam condenserમાં air leakageની અસરો અને શોધવાની એક રીત સમજાવો.7 marks7 ગુણ

Effects of air leakage:

  • Non-condensable air occupies volume and raises condenser pressure/back pressure; turbine output and efficiency fall.
  • Air blanket on tubes adds thermal resistance, reducing heat-transfer rate and increasing cooling-water/exhaust temperature.
  • More air-extraction load is required; vacuum becomes difficult to maintain.
  • Oxygen/other gases promote corrosion; dissolved gases degrade condensate/feedwater quality.
  • High back pressure can raise turbine exhaust temperature and stress.

Detection — smoke/candle test on accessible joints while under vacuum: with equipment in a safe operating/test condition, move a small smoke source around flanges, inspection covers, valve stems and joints. At an inward leak the smoke is drawn toward the joint; a candle flame may deflect inward. Mark the location and repair only after isolating/depressurising according to site procedure. Do not use an open flame where flammable gas or unsafe conditions may exist. A controlled tracer-gas/vacuum leak test is a more sensitive alternative.

State the condenser must be under vacuum and describe the observed indication; safety qualification earns credit.

Air leakageની અસરો:

  • Non-condensable air જગ્યા રોકે અને condenser/back pressure વધારે; turbine output તથા efficiency ઘટે.
  • Tube પર air blanket thermal resistance વધારે; heat-transfer ઘટે અને cooling-water/exhaust temperature વધે.
  • વધુ air-extraction load જોઈએ; vacuum જાળવવું મુશ્કેલ.
  • Oxygen/અન્ય gas corrosion કરે; condensate/feedwaterની quality બગડે.
  • વધેલા back pressureથી turbine exhaust temperature/stress વધી શકે.

શોધવાની રીત — vacuum હેઠળ joint પાસે smoke/candle test: સલામત operating/test સ્થિતિમાં flange, inspection cover, valve stem અને jointની આસપાસ નાનો smoke source ફેરવો. અંદરની તરફ leak હોય તો smoke joint તરફ ખેંચાય; candle flame અંદર વળે. જગ્યા mark કરો; site procedure પ્રમાણે isolate/depressurise કર્યા પછી જ repair. જ્વલનશીલ gas/અસલામત સ્થિતિમાં ખુલ્લી આગ ન વાપરો. Controlled tracer-gas/vacuum leak test વધુ sensitive વિકલ્પ છે.
