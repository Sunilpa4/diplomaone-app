Q1 · Principles, ranging and WCB angles

Q.1 (a) Explain Principles of surveying. (03 marks)Q.1 (a) Explain Principles of surveying. (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Whole to part: Establish major control triangle / traverse first, minor detail inside. Errors stay local, never accumulate.

Fix by two independent references: Every new point located from two known points — intersection of distance or direction — so check possible.

Other corollaries: work from firm datum, provide independent checks, economy of accuracy.

આખા થી ભાગ: મોટું control પહેલા, નાની વિગત અંદર.

બે સંદર્ભ થી નવો બિંદુ: દરેક નવો point બે જાણીતા થી નક્કી.

Q.1 (b) Explain Types of ranging in detail. (04 marks)Q.1 (b) Explain Types of ranging in detail. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Direct ranging: End stations intervisible — ranging rod by eye or line ranger, intermediate rods in line.

Indirect / reciprocal: Obstacle / rising ground — two intermediates, reciprocal sight until both align.

Random line ranging for forest.

Direct: બન્ને છેડા દેખાય ત્યાં eye થી line માં rod.

Indirect: ટેકરી વચ્ચે બે rod વારાફરતી align.

Q.3 (c) Explain errors in the compass survey and describe the closing error. (07 marks)Q.3 (c) Explain errors in the compass survey and describe the closing error. (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ

Instrumental: Pivot, needle, graduation. Personal: Centering levelling reading. Natural: Magnetic storm, local attraction iron, declination.

Closing error: Traverse fails to close on itself due to angular/linear errors. Vector e = √ (ΣLat²+ΣDep²). Adjusted by Bowditch or transit rule.
