Q1 · Theodolite Traverse — repetition, bearing, vernier & closing error

Q.1 (a) What are the advantages of using repetition method in theodolite traverse survey? (03 marks)Q.1 (a) What are the advantages of using repetition method in theodolite traverse survey? (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Repetition method improves accuracy for traverse where many angles are measured.

Advantages: 1) Least count effectively divided by number of repetitions, so finer reading. 2) Graduation errors of circle averaged out. 3) Both faces left/right readings cancel collimation and eccentricity. 4) Ideal for long traverses. 5) Faster than reiteration for same precision.

રિપીટિશન પદ્ધતિ ટ્રાવર્સમાં ઘણા ખૂણા માપવા ચોકસાઈ વધારે.

ફાયદા: 1 લીસ્ટ કાઉન્ટ n થી ભાગાય ઝીણું વાંચન. 2 ગ્રેજ્યુએશન ભૂલ સરેરાશ. 3 બંને ફેસથી કોલિમેશન દૂર. 4 લાંબા ટ્રાવર્સ માટે ઉત્તમ. 5 સમાન ચોકસાઈ માટે રિઇટરેશન કરતાં ઝડપી.

Q.1 (b) Explain method for measuring magnetic bearing of a line with theodolite. (04 marks)Q.1 (b) Explain method for measuring magnetic bearing of a line with theodolite. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Attach trough compass or use theodolite with compass.

1) Set up at A, centre and level. 2) Set vernier A to 0° and clamp. 3) Release lower clamp, rotate to magnetic north indicated by compass and clamp lower. 4) Release upper, sight B, read bearing on vernier — this is magnetic bearing AB. 5) Repeat face left/right and average, check FB − BB ≈ 180° to detect local attraction.

ટ્રફ કોમ્પાસ સાથે થિયોડોલાઇટથી મેગ્નેટિક બેરિંગ.

1 A પર સેટ સેન્ટર લેવલ. 2 વર્નિયર A 0° સેટ ક્લેમ્પ. 3 લોઅર છોડી કોમ્પાસથી મેગ્નેટિક ઉત્તર તરફ ફેરવી લોઅર ક્લેમ્પ. 4 અપર છોડી B તરફ જુઓ બેરિંગ વાંચો — મેગ્નેટિક બેરિંગ AB. 5 બંને ફેસ સરેરાશ, FB−BB ≈ 180° ચેક.

Q.1 (c) Draw parts of main and Vernier scale of theodolite you have used. Also calculate least count of it. (07 marks)Q.1 (c) Draw parts of main and Vernier scale of theodolite you have used. Also calculate least count of it. (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ

Main scale is graduated 0°–360° with smallest division 20′ or 30′. Vernier has 60 divisions covering 59 main divisions in vernier theodolite.

Least count = smallest main division ÷ number of vernier divisions. If main is 20′ and vernier 60 → LC = 20′/60 = 20″. If main 30′ → LC = 30″. Example: 20″ theodolite can read 20″, 10″ electronic reads 10″ by digital encoder. Parts shown are main graduation, vernier arrow, magnifier and vernier scale.

મેઇન સ્કેલ 0°–360° દર 20′ કે 30′, વર્નિયર 60 ભાગ 59 મેઇન પર.

લીસ્ટ કાઉન્ટ = નાનું મેઇન ÷ વર્નિયર સંખ્યા. 20′ હોય તો 20″, 30′ હોય તો 30″. દા. 20″ થિયોડોલાઇટ 20″ વાંચે. ભાગો: મેઇન ગ્રેજ્યુએશન, વર્નિયર તીર, મેગ્નિફાયર.

Method: Compute latitude = L·cos θ and departure = L·sin θ where θ is WCB, sum them, closing error e = √(ΣLat² + ΣDep²), relative error = e/perimeter.

AB 105.8 m 319°15′ → Lat +80.17 m Dep −69.12 m; BC 142.5 m 51°30′ → +88.64, +111.44; CD 188.8 m 131°45′ → −125.67, +140.90; DA 188.9 m 256°45′ → −43.29, −183.88. ΣLat = −0.15 m, ΣDep = −0.66 m, e = √(0.15²+0.66²) = 0.68 m, perimeter 626 m, relative 1:925, bearing of error about 257°. This is Bowditch check.

AB +80.17 −69.12, BC +88.64 +111.44, CD −125.67 +140.90, DA −43.29 −183.88, ΣLat −0.15 ΣDep −0.66, e = 0.68 m, પરિમિતિ 626 m, 1:925, એરર બેરિંગ 257°.

Q2 · Trigonometric & Tacheometric — short notes, derivations & chimney/tacheometer

Q.2 (a) Write short note on “Trigonometrical levelling” (03 marks)Q.2 (a) Write short note on “Trigonometrical levelling” (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Trigonometrical levelling finds elevation of high inaccessible objects using vertical angle and distance: height = D·tan α. RL = RL of instrument axis + height − staff. Types: base accessible with tape, base inaccessible same plane or not same plane, and reciprocal to cancel refraction over water. Used for towers, chimneys, hills.

ટ્રિગ લેવલિંગ વર્ટિકલ એંગલ અને અંતરથી ઊંચા અગમ્ય ઓબ્જેક્ટની ઊંચાઈ D·tan α થી શોધે. RL = સાધન અક્ષ + ઊંચાઈ − સ્ટાફ. પ્રકાર: બેઝ સુલભ, અગમ્ય સમાન/અસમાન પ્લેન, રેસિપ્રોકલ. ટાવર, ચીમની, ટેકરી માટે.

Q.2 (a) OR Calculate the tacheometric constant from the following observations. (03 marks)Q.2 (a) OR Calculate the tacheometric constant from the following observations. (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

ડેટા: 50 m પર 0.495 m, 150 m પર 1.485 m, D = K·S + C.

50 = 0.495K + C, 150 = 1.485K + C → 100 = 0.99K → K = 101 ≈ 100, C ≈ 0–0.5 m. એનાલેટિકમાં K = 100 C = 0.

Q.2 (b) In trigonometrical levelling base of object is inaccessible, instrument stations in the same vertical plane as object and height of instrument axis at the same level. (04 marks)Q.2 (b) In trigonometrical levelling base of object is inaccessible, instrument stations in the same vertical plane as object and height of instrument axis at the same level. Let AB = d, nearer station distance to object D, heights h = D·tan α1 at nearer and h = (D+d)·tan α2 at farther if farther sees smaller angle. If height of instrument same, RL = RL of BM + BS + h. Derivation uses two similar right triangles in same plane.

AB = d, નજીક D, h = D·tan α1 અને h = D+d·tan α2, સરખાવતાં D = d·tan α2/tan α1 − tan α2, h = D·tan α1, RL = HI + h − ઓબ્જેક્ટ ઊંચાઈ. સમાન પ્લેનમાં બે કાટકોણ ત્રિકોણથી સૂત્ર.

Q.2 (b) OR What is the anallatic lens? Give disadvantages of it. (04 marks)Q.2 (b) OR What is the anallatic lens? Give disadvantages of it. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Anallatic lens is an extra convex lens placed between objective and diaphragm to make additive constant C = 0 and multiplying constant K = 100 exactly, simplifying D = K·S.

Disadvantages: Reduces brightness about 10%, extra cost, more delicate, harder to clean, needs precise positioning. Modern tacheometers often use internal focusing without separate anallatic.

એનાલેટિક લેન્સ ઓબ્જેક્ટિવ અને ડાયાફ્રેમ વચ્ચે વધારાનો બહિર્ગોળ લેન્સ C = 0 અને K = 100 ચોક્કસ કરવા, D = K·S સરળ.

ગેરફાયદા: પ્રકાશ 10% ઓછો, વધુ ખર્ચ, નાજુક, સફાઈ મુશ્કેલ, ચોક્કસ ગોઠવણ જોઈએ.

Q3 · Tacheometry & Curves Basics — fixed hair, principle & circular curve

Q.3 (a) Explain fix hair method of tacheometry. (03 marks)Q.3 (a) Explain fix hair method of tacheometry. (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Fixed hair method uses diaphragm with three fixed horizontal hairs. Staff intercept S = top − bottom varies with distance. For horizontal sight D = K·S + C, for inclined D = K·S·cos²θ and V = K·S·sin2θ/2. Simplest and most used, needs vertical staff and anallatic lens for C=0. Accuracy 1:1000.

ફિક્સ્ડ હેરમાં ત્રણ સ્થિર આડા વાળ, સ્ટાફ ઇન્ટરસેપ્ટ S બદલાય. આડા સાઇટ D = K·S + C, ત્રાંસા D = K·S·cos²θ. સૌથી સરળ વધુ વપરાય, વર્ટિકલ સ્ટાફ જોઈએ.

Q.3 (a) OR What are stadia diaphragm? Draw different shape of it used in tacheometric surveying. (03 marks)Q.3 (a) OR What are stadia diaphragm? Draw different shape of it used in tacheometric surveying. (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Stadia diaphragm is glass with stadia hairs etched. Shapes: three horizontal hairs with central vertical, inclined cross hairs, ladder or comb pattern. Different shapes help in inclined staff work and reduce confusion. All give same intercept principle.

સ્ટેડિયા ડાયાફ્રેમ કાચ પર વાળ કોતરેલા. આકાર: ત્રણ આડા + ઊભો, ત્રાંસા ક્રોસ, સીડી. ત્રાંસા સ્ટાફમાં ઉપયોગી.

Q.3 (b) Explain principle of tacheometry. (04 marks)Q.3 (b) Explain principle of tacheometry. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Principle: Horizontal distance proportional to staff intercept: D = (f/i)·S + (f+d) = K·S + C. Derived from similar triangles formed by objective. f is focal length, i stadia interval. For inclined line, horizontal and vertical components by trigonometry. Enables rapid distance and elevation without taping.

સિદ્ધાંત: હોરિઝોન્ટલ અંતર સ્ટાફ ઇન્ટરસેપ્ટ પ્રમાણમાં: D = f/i·S + f+d = K·S + C. ઓબ્જેક્ટિવથી સમદ્વિબાહુ ત્રિકોણથી. ત્રાંસા માટે ત્રિકોણમિતિ.

Q.3 (b) OR Explain method of finding multiplying constant and additive constant in the field. (04 marks)Q.3 (b) OR Explain method of finding multiplying constant and additive constant in the field. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Select level ground, measure two known distances D1,D2, take intercepts S1,S2, solve D1 = K·S1 + C and D2 = K·S2 + C → K = (D2−D1)/(S2−S1), C = D1 − K·S1. Check with third distance or plot D vs S best fit line slope K intercept C.

સમતલ જમીન પર બે જાણીતા અંતર D1,D2 પર ઇન્ટરસેપ્ટ S1,S2 માપી D1 = K·S1 + C અને D2 = K·S2 + C ઉકેલો → K = D2−D1/S2−S1, C = D1 − K·S1. ત્રીજા અંતરથી ચકાસો અથવા ગ્રાફ.

Q.3 (c) Draw neat sketch of simple circular curve. Show its all components and define any three of them. (07 marks)Q.3 (c) Draw neat sketch of simple circular curve. Show its all components and define any three of them. (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ

Components: PI intersection of tangents, PC/T1 start, PT/T2 end, R radius, Δ deflection angle, Tangent length T = R·tan(Δ/2), Curve length L = R·Δ·π/180, Long chord Lc = 2R·sin(Δ/2), External E = R·sec(Δ/2)−R, Mid ordinate M = R−R·cos(Δ/2), Apex distance, Versed sine. Three defined: T is distance PI to PC, Lc is straight chord PC−PT, M is distance from chord mid to curve.

ઘટકો: PI ટેન્જન્ટ આંતરછેદ, PC T1 શરૂ, PT T2 અંત, R ત્રિજ્યા, Δ વિચલન, T = R·tan Δ/2, L = R·Δ·π/180, Lc = 2R·sin Δ/2, E = R·sec Δ/2−R, M = R−R·cos Δ/2, એપેક્સ, વર્સ્ડ સાઇન. ત્રણ: T PI−PC, Lc સીધો કોર્ડ, M મધ્ય ઓર્ડિનેટ.

Q.4 (c) Calculate the ordinate at 10 m. interval for simple circular curve having a long chord of 150 m and versed sine of 2.50 m. (07 marks)Q.4 (c) Calculate the ordinate at 10 m. interval for simple circular curve having a long chord of 150 m and versed sine of 2.50 m. (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ

Q5 · Modern Instruments — electronic theodolite, EDM, GPS, ATR & total station

Q.5 (a) Explain the following. (1) Electronic theodolite. (2) EDM instrument. (03 marks)Q.5 (a) Explain the following. (1) Electronic theodolite. (2) EDM instrument. (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Electronic theodolite: Digital angle encoder and LCD replace vernier, auto compensator for vertical, 1″ accuracy, no reading error, data can be stored. Functions: measure angle, slope, set out.

EDM: Electronic Distance Measurement by infrared phase comparison with prism reflector, accuracy ±2 mm + 2 ppm, auto reduces to horizontal/vertical.

ઇલેક્ટ્રોનિક થિયોડોલાઇટ: ડિજિટલ એન્કોડર LCD વર્નિયરને બદલે, ઓટો કોમ્પેન્સેટર, 1″ ચોકસાઈ, ડેટા સ્ટોર.

EDM: ઇન્ફ્રારેડ ફેઝ તુલના પ્રિઝમ સાથે ±2 mm, હોરિઝોન્ટલ વર્ટિકલ ઓટો.

Q.5 (a) OR What is ATR? Explain in detail. (03 marks)Q.5 (a) OR What is ATR? Explain in detail. (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

ATR = Automatic Target Recognition. CCD and laser auto-detect prism, lock and track without manual bisection. Steps: coarse search, fine adjust, lock. Accuracy 1″ range 1000 m, enables robotic one-man and monitoring.

ATR = ઓટોમેટિક ટાર્ગેટ રેકગ્નિશન. CCD અને લેસર પ્રિઝમ ઓટો શોધી લોક અને ટ્રેક કરે. તબક્કા: સ્થૂળ શોધ, સૂક્ષ્મ ગોઠવણ, લોક. 1″ 1000 m રોબોટિક.

Q.5 (b) Write short note on “GPS receivers” (04 marks)Q.5 (b) Write short note on “GPS receivers” (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

GPS receiver has antenna, RF front end, processor, memory. Types: handheld ±3 m, differential ±cm, RTK real-time. Uses 4+ satellites trilateration. Applications: control, navigation, staking, GIS. Needs open sky, not for indoor or under tree.

GPS રીસીવર એન્ટેના, RF, પ્રોસેસર, મેમરી. પ્રકાર: હેન્ડહેલ્ડ ±3 m, ડિફરન્શિયલ ±cm, RTK. 4+ સેટેલાઇટ ટ્રાઇલેટરેશન. ઉપયોગ: કંટ્રોલ, નેવિગેશન, GIS. ખુલ્લું આકાશ જોઈએ.

Q.5 (b) OR Explain various segment of a GPS. (04 marks)Q.5 (b) OR Explain various segment of a GPS. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Three segments: Space — 24 satellites, Control — master and monitor stations, User — receiver. Space transmits signal, control corrects orbit, user computes position.

ત્રણ ભાગ: અવકાશ — 24 સેટેલાઇટ, કંટ્રોલ — માસ્ટર મોનિટર, યુઝર — રીસીવર. અવકાશ સિગ્નલ, કંટ્રોલ ભ્રમણ સુધારે, યુઝર સ્થાન ગણે.
