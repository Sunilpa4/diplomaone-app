Q1(a) Define force, work and pressure.Q1(a) Force, work અને pressureની વ્યાખ્યા આપો.3 marks3 ગુણ

Question: Q1(a) Define force, work and pressure.

  • Force (F): a push or pull that can change motion or deform a body. It is a vector. SI unit: newton (N); for constant mass, net force F = ma.
  • Work (W): energy transferred when a force causes displacement. For a constant force, W = Fs cos θ; when force and displacement are parallel, W = Fs. SI unit: joule (J) = N·m. If there is no displacement, mechanical work on that body is zero.
  • Pressure (p): normal force per unit area, p = F/A for a uniformly loaded area. SI unit: pascal (Pa) = N/m². Greater force raises pressure; for the same force, smaller area raises pressure.

પ્રશ્ન: Q1(a) Force, work અને pressureની વ્યાખ્યા આપો.

  • Force (બળ): ધક્કો અથવા ખેંચાણ, જે ગતિ બદલે અથવા પદાર્થને વિકૃત કરે. તે vector છે. SI unit newton (N); સ્થિર mass માટે net force F = ma.
  • Work (કાર્ય): બળથી displacement થાય ત્યારે energyનું transfer. સ્થિર બળ માટે W = Fs cos θ; બળ અને displacement એક દિશામાં હોય તો W = Fs. SI unit joule (J) = N·m. displacement ન હોય તો પદાર્થ પર mechanical work શૂન્ય.
  • Pressure (દબાણ): એકમ ક્ષેત્રફળ પર normal force, p = F/A. SI unit pascal (Pa) = N/m². બળ વધે તો pressure વધે; એ જ બળ ઓછા ક્ષેત્રફળ પર હોય તો pressure વધે.

Question: Q1(c) Classify the air compressor and give its application.

An air compressor raises air pressure using mechanical work. It is classified by operating principle:

ClassTypes / principleTypical application
Positive-displacementReciprocating piston traps a volume and reduces it; rotary screw, vane and lobe machines trap/transport air in rotating elements.Workshops, pneumatic tools, tyre service, instrument/control air and general plant supply; type/pressure selected for duty.

Q1(c) OR · Explain working of single stage reciprocating air compressor.Q1(c) OR · Single-stage reciprocating air compressorનું કાર્ય સમજાવો.7 marks7 ગુણ

Question: Q1(c) OR · Explain working of single stage reciprocating air compressor.

Construction: cylinder, piston, connecting rod and crank, suction (inlet) valve, delivery valve, clearance space, air filter, receiver, motor/drive and cooling arrangement. Both valves are automatic pressure-operated non-return valves.

  1. Suction stroke: the crank moves the piston away from the cylinder head. Cylinder pressure falls below inlet pressure, the suction valve opens and filtered air enters. The delivery valve remains shut.
  2. Compression stroke: the piston returns; both valves are initially shut and trapped air is compressed. Its pressure and temperature rise.
  3. Delivery: when cylinder pressure exceeds receiver/delivery pressure plus valve resistance, the delivery valve opens and compressed air flows to the receiver. Near the end, a small clearance volume remains; its trapped air re-expands on the next suction stroke.

A single-acting machine compresses on one side of the piston; one complete cycle takes one crank revolution. Cooling, lubrication, receiver safety valve and pressure controls are required as designed.

પ્રશ્ન: Q1(c) OR · Single-stage reciprocating air compressorનું કાર્ય સમજાવો.

રચના: cylinder, piston, connecting rod અને crank, suction (inlet) valve, delivery valve, clearance space, air filter, receiver, motor/drive અને cooling વ્યવસ્થા. બંને valve દબાણથી ચાલતા automatic non-return valve છે.

  1. Suction stroke: crank pistonને cylinder headથી દૂર ખસેડે. Cylinder pressure inlet કરતાં ઘટે; suction valve ખૂલે અને filter કરેલી air આવે. Delivery valve બંધ રહે.
  2. Compression stroke: piston પાછો આવે; શરૂઆતમાં બંને valve બંધ રહે અને અંદરની air compress થાય. તેનું pressure અને temperature વધે.
  3. Delivery: cylinder pressure receiver/delivery pressure અને valve resistance કરતાં વધુ થાય ત્યારે delivery valve ખૂલે અને compressed air receiverમાં જાય. અંતે થોડું clearance volume રહે; તેની air આગલા suctionમાં ફરી ફેલાય.

Single-acting machine pistonની એક બાજુએ compression કરે; એક cycleમાં crankનો એક ફેરો. રચના મુજબ cooling, lubrication, receiver safety valve અને pressure control જરૂરી છે.

Q2(b) Explain boyle’s law with neat sketch.Q2(b) Boyleનો નિયમ સ્વચ્છ આકૃતિ સાથે સમજાવો.4 marks4 ગુણ

Question: Q2(b) Explain boyle’s law with neat sketch.

For a fixed mass of gas at constant temperature, pressure is inversely proportional to volume:

p ∝ 1/V   therefore   pV = constant
p₁V₁ = p₂V₂

When the gas is compressed isothermally, volume decreases and absolute pressure increases; when it expands, pressure falls. The p–V plot is a rectangular hyperbola. Use absolute pressure and keep temperature and gas mass constant. This relation is an ideal-gas model approximation over the applicable range.

પ્રશ્ન: Q2(b) Boyleનો નિયમ સ્વચ્છ આકૃતિ સાથે સમજાવો.

નિશ્ચિત gas mass અને સ્થિર temperature પર pressure, volumeના વ્યસ્ત પ્રમાણમાં હોય છે:

p ∝ 1/V   એટલે   pV = સ્થિર
p₁V₁ = p₂V₂

Gasને isothermal રીતે compress કરીએ તો volume ઘટે અને absolute pressure વધે; ફેલાય તો pressure ઘટે. p–V graph rectangular hyperbola છે. Absolute pressure લો અને temperature તથા gas mass સ્થિર રાખો. આ લાગુ પડતી મર્યાદામાં ideal-gasનું અનુમાન છે.

Q2(c) Explain Cochran boiler with neat sketch.Q2(c) Cochran boiler સ્વચ્છ આકૃતિ સાથે સમજાવો.7 marks7 ગુણ

Question: Q2(c) Explain Cochran boiler with neat sketch.

Type and construction: Cochran is a vertical, multi-tubular, fire-tube, internally fired, natural-circulation boiler. The vertical cylindrical shell has a hemispherical top. Main parts include furnace and grate, combustion chamber, fire tubes, smoke box/chimney, water space, steam space and standard mountings.

Working: feed water fills the shell to the working level and surrounds the furnace and tubes. Fuel burns on the grate. Hot gases pass through the furnace/combustion chamber and fire tubes, transfer heat to surrounding water, then leave through the smoke box and chimney. Water circulates naturally as heated water rises and cooler water descends. Steam collects in the upper steam space and is supplied through the stop valve. The safety valve, pressure gauge, water-level indicator, feed check and blow-off cock provide protection/control.

Use: compact, small-capacity steam supply. Draw and label shell, furnace, grate, combustion chamber, fire tubes, chimney, water level, steam space and mountings.

પ્રશ્ન: Q2(c) Cochran boiler સ્વચ્છ આકૃતિ સાથે સમજાવો.

પ્રકાર અને રચના: Cochran એક vertical, multi-tubular, fire-tube, internally fired અને natural-circulation boiler છે. Vertical cylindrical shellની ઉપર hemispherical top હોય છે. મુખ્ય ભાગ: furnace/grate, combustion chamber, fire tube, smoke box/chimney, water space, steam space અને જરૂરી mountings.

કાર્ય: shellમાં working level સુધી પાણી furnace અને tubeની આસપાસ ભરાય. Grate પર fuel બળે. ગરમ gas furnace/combustion chamber અને fire tubeમાંથી પસાર થઈ પાણીમાં heat આપે; પછી smoke box/chimneyથી બહાર જાય. ગરમ પાણી ઉપર અને ઠંડું નીચે જાય એટલે કુદરતી circulation થાય. ઉપરના steam spaceમાં steam ભેગી થઈ stop valveથી મળે. ઉપયોગ: નાની ક્ષમતાના compact steam supply માટે. આકૃતિમાં shell, furnace, grate, combustion chamber, fire tube, chimney, water level, steam space અને mountings label કરો.

Q2(b) OR · Explain charle’s law with neat sketch.Q2(b) OR · Charlesનો નિયમ સ્વચ્છ આકૃતિ સાથે સમજાવો.4 marks4 ગુણ

Question: Q2(b) OR · Explain charle’s law with neat sketch.

For a fixed mass of gas at constant pressure, volume is directly proportional to absolute temperature:

V ∝ T   therefore   V/T = constant
V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂

Temperature must be in kelvin (K), not directly in Celsius. At fixed pressure, heating expands the gas; the V–T graph is a straight line through the origin when plotted against absolute temperature. Keep the gas mass and pressure constant.

પ્રશ્ન: Q2(b) OR · Charlesનો નિયમ સ્વચ્છ આકૃતિ સાથે સમજાવો.

નિશ્ચિત gas mass અને સ્થિર pressure પર volume, absolute temperatureના સીધા પ્રમાણમાં છે:

V ∝ T   એટલે   V/T = સ્થિર
V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂

Temperature kelvin (K)માં જ લો, સીધું Celsiusમાં નહીં. સ્થિર pressure પર ગરમ કરતાં gas ફેલાય; absolute temperature સામે V–T graph originમાંથી પસાર થતી સીધી રેખા છે. Gas mass અને pressure સ્થિર રાખો.

Q2(c) OR · Explain Babcock and wilcox boiler with neat sketch.Q2(c) OR · Babcock and Wilcox boiler સ્વચ્છ આકૃતિ સાથે સમજાવો.7 marks7 ગુણ

Question: Q2(c) OR · Explain Babcock and wilcox boiler with neat sketch.

Type and construction: Babcock and Wilcox is a horizontal, externally fired, water-tube boiler with natural circulation. A steam-and-water drum connects to headers and a bank of inclined water tubes. Furnace, grate, baffles, uptake/chimney and boiler mountings complete the unit.

Working: feed water enters the drum. Water circulates through the headers and inclined tubes; furnace gases sweep across the tube bank in a guided zigzag path. Heat turns part of the water into a steam-water mixture that returns to the drum. Steam separates and collects in the drum; it is delivered through the stop valve. Baffles improve contact with hot gas; the gas finally leaves through the chimney. State the standard safety and level-control mountings in the sketch.

It suits larger steam-generating duties than a small vertical fire-tube unit. Label drum, headers, inclined water tubes, furnace/grate, baffles, gas path and chimney.

Q3(a) Give the classification of boiler.Q3(a) Boilerનું વર્ગીકરણ આપો.3 marks3 ગુણ

Question: Q3(a) Give the classification of boiler.

Boilers can be classified on several independent bases (one boiler belongs to one category on each basis):

  • Fluid in tubes: fire-tube (hot gas in tubes, water outside) or water-tube (water in tubes, hot gas outside).
  • Axis: horizontal, vertical or inclined.
  • Firing: internally fired or externally fired.
  • Water circulation: natural or forced circulation.
  • Use / mobility: stationary, portable/mobile or marine.
  • Pressure: low, medium or high pressure (limits depend on the adopted standard).

Examples: Cochran—vertical fire-tube, internally fired; Babcock and Wilcox—horizontal water-tube, externally fired.

પ્રશ્ન: Q3(a) Boilerનું વર્ગીકરણ આપો.

Boilerને અનેક સ્વતંત્ર આધાર પર વર્ગીકૃત કરી શકાય (દરેક આધાર પર boiler એક categoryમાં આવે):

  • Tubeમાં fluid: fire-tube (tubeમાં ગરમ gas, બહાર પાણી) અથવા water-tube (tubeમાં પાણી, બહાર ગરમ gas).
  • Axis: horizontal, vertical અથવા inclined.
  • Firing: internally fired અથવા externally fired.
  • Water circulation: natural અથવા forced circulation.
  • ઉપયોગ/ચાલન: stationary, portable/mobile અથવા marine.
  • Pressure: low, medium અથવા high (મર્યાદા લાગુ standard પર આધારિત).

Q3(b) Explain steam formation process.Q3(b) Steam બનવાની પ્રક્રિયા સમજાવો.4 marks4 ગુણ

Question: Q3(b) Explain steam formation process.

Consider water heated at a fixed pressure:

  1. Subcooled/compressed liquid: water temperature rises until it reaches the saturation temperature for that pressure. This is sensible heating.
  2. Evaporation: further heat changes liquid into vapour at approximately constant saturation temperature and pressure. Liquid and vapour coexist; supplied energy is latent heat.
  3. Wet steam: before all liquid evaporates, the mixture contains water droplets. Dryness fraction x = mass of dry vapour / total mass (0 < x < 1).
  4. Dry saturated steam: the last liquid has just evaporated; x = 1 and the steam is at saturation temperature.
  5. Superheated steam: adding heat at the same pressure raises vapour temperature above saturation temperature.

Draw temperature versus heat supplied at constant pressure with a rising liquid segment, a vaporisation plateau and a rising superheat segment.

પ્રશ્ન: Q3(b) Steam બનવાની પ્રક્રિયા સમજાવો.

નિશ્ચિત pressure પર પાણી ગરમ કરીએ:

  1. Subcooled/compressed liquid: તે pressureનું saturation temperature આવે ત્યાં સુધી પાણી ગરમ થાય; આ sensible heating.
  2. Evaporation: વધુ heatથી liquid લગભગ સ્થિર saturation temperature/pressure પર vapour બને. Liquid અને vapour સાથે રહે; ઊર્જા latent heat છે.
  3. Wet steam: બધું પાણી વરાળ બને તે પહેલાં ટીપાંવાળું મિશ્રણ. Dryness fraction x = dry vapour mass / કુલ mass (0 < x < 1).
  4. Dry saturated steam: છેલ્લું liquid હમણાં જ વરાળ બને; x = 1 અને steam saturation temperature પર હોય.
  5. Superheated steam: એ જ pressure પર વધુ heat આપતાં vapour temperature saturationથી ઉપર જાય.

નિશ્ચિત pressure પર temperature સામે heatનો graph દોરો: liquidનો વધતો ભાગ, vaporisation plateau અને superheatનો વધતો ભાગ.

Q3(c) Explain working of four stroke petrol engine.Q3(c) Four-stroke petrol engineનું કાર્ય સમજાવો.7 marks7 ગુણ

Question: Q3(c) Explain working of four stroke petrol engine.

A petrol engine is a spark-ignition (SI), internal-combustion engine. The piston moves between top and bottom dead centres; inlet and exhaust valves are timed by the valve mechanism. One cycle requires four strokes (two crankshaft revolutions, 720°) and gives one power stroke.

  1. Suction/intake: inlet valve open, exhaust closed; the descending piston draws an air-fuel mixture into the cylinder.
  2. Compression: both valves closed; the rising piston compresses the mixture. A spark plug ignites it near the end of the stroke.
  3. Power/expansion: burning gases raise pressure and push the piston down with both valves closed. Connecting rod and crankshaft deliver useful work.
  4. Exhaust: exhaust valve opens; the rising piston pushes burnt gases out. The cycle repeats.

In a neat section show cylinder, piston, connecting rod, crankshaft, inlet/exhaust valves, spark plug and flow arrows. Actual valve timing overlaps the ideal dead-centre events.

પ્રશ્ન: Q3(c) Four-stroke petrol engineનું કાર્ય સમજાવો.

Petrol engine spark-ignition (SI), internal-combustion engine છે. Piston top અને bottom dead centre વચ્ચે ફરે; valve mechanism inlet/exhaust valveનો સમય નિયંત્રે. એક cycle માટે ચાર stroke (crankshaftના બે ફેરા, 720°) અને એક power stroke.

  1. Suction/intake: inlet valve ખુલ્લો, exhaust બંધ; નીચે જતો piston air-fuel mixture cylinderમાં ખેંચે.
  2. Compression: બંને valve બંધ; ઉપર જતો piston mixture compress કરે. Strokeના અંતે spark plug તેને સળગાવે.
  3. Power/expansion: સળગેલા gasનું pressure વધે અને બંને valve બંધ હોય ત્યારે pistonને નીચે ધકેલે. Connecting rod/crankshaft કામ આપે.
  4. Exhaust: exhaust valve ખૂલે; ઉપર જતો piston burnt gas બહાર કાઢે. Cycle ફરી શરૂ થાય.

સ્વચ્છ sectionમાં cylinder, piston, connecting rod, crankshaft, inlet/exhaust valve, spark plug અને flow arrow દોરો. વાસ્તવિક valve timing ideal dead-centreથી થોડું overlap કરે છે.

Question: Q3(a) OR · Define boiler mountings. List the mountings of the boiler.

Boiler mountings are essential fittings mounted on a boiler for safe operation, control and indication. Common mountings are:

  1. Safety valve — releases excess pressure.
  2. Water-level indicator / gauge glass — shows water level.
  3. Pressure gauge — indicates steam pressure.
  4. Steam stop valve — controls steam leaving the boiler.
  5. Feed check valve — admits feed water and prevents reverse flow.
  6. Blow-off cock / blow-down valve — drains water and sediment.

પ્રશ્ન: Q3(a) OR · Boiler mountingsની વ્યાખ્યા આપો અને તેમની યાદી બનાવો.

Boiler mounting એટલે boiler પર લગાડેલું સલામત operation, control અને indication માટેનું આવશ્યક fitting. સામાન્ય mountings:

  1. Safety valve — વધારે pressure છોડે.
  2. Water-level indicator / gauge glass — પાણીનું સ્તર બતાવે.

Q3(b) OR · Explain types of steam.Q3(b) OR · Steamના પ્રકારો સમજાવો.4 marks4 ગુણ

Question: Q3(b) OR · Explain types of steam.

  • Wet steam: saturated vapour mixed with suspended liquid-water droplets. Dryness fraction x is below 1; wetness fraction = 1 − x.
  • Dry saturated steam: all liquid has just evaporated; it is at the saturation temperature for its pressure and has x = 1.
  • Superheated steam: steam heated above saturation temperature at the same pressure. Degree of superheat = actual steam temperature − saturation temperature.

For a wet mixture, x = mass of dry saturated vapour / total mass of the mixture. Steam quality matters in turbines and pipes because entrained droplets can cause erosion and reduce performance.

પ્રશ્ન: Q3(b) OR · Steamના પ્રકારો સમજાવો.

  • Wet steam: saturated vapour સાથે પાણીનાં ટીપાંનું મિશ્રણ. Dryness fraction x, 1થી ઓછી; wetness fraction = 1 − x.
  • Dry saturated steam: બધું liquid હમણાં જ વરાળ બને; તેના pressureનું saturation temperature હોય અને x = 1.
  • Superheated steam: એ જ pressure પર saturation temperatureથી વધુ ગરમ કરેલી steam. Degree of superheat = steamનું actual temperature − saturation temperature.

Wet mixture માટે x = dry saturated vapourનું mass / મિશ્રણનું total mass. Turbine અને pipeમાં steam quality જરૂરી છે; ટીપાં erosion અને performance ઘટાડે.

Q4(a) Write advantages of Centrifugal pump.Q4(a) Centrifugal pumpના ફાયદા લખો.3 marks3 ગુણ

Question: Q4(a) Write advantages of Centrifugal pump.

Q4(b) Discuss the importance of priming in pump.Q4(b) Pumpમાં primingનું મહત્વ સમજાવો.4 marks4 ગુણ

Question: Q4(b) Discuss the importance of priming in pump.

Priming is filling the centrifugal-pump casing and suction pipe completely with the liquid to be pumped and expelling air before start-up. A conventional centrifugal impeller develops only a small pressure rise if it spins in air; it cannot reliably evacuate air from a dry suction line.

  1. Fill casing and suction line using the priming arrangement; keep the delivery valve in the prescribed starting position.
  2. Remove trapped air; check foot valve/non-return valve and suction joints for leakage.
  3. Start only when primed, then open/control delivery as instructed and check for steady flow.

Priming lets the impeller transfer energy to liquid and create the suction pressure difference needed to draw more liquid. It prevents loss of suction/air-lock and dry running, which can overheat or damage seals. Follow the pump maker's safe procedure.

પ્રશ્ન: Q4(b) Pumpમાં primingનું મહત્વ સમજાવો.

Priming એટલે ચાલુ કરતાં પહેલાં centrifugal pumpનું casing અને suction pipe પંપ કરવાના પ્રવાહીથી સંપૂર્ણ ભરવું અને હવા કાઢવી. સામાન્ય centrifugal impeller હવામાં ફરે તો બહુ ઓછું pressure બનાવે; ખાલી suction lineની હવા વિશ્વસનીય રીતે ખેંચી શકતું નથી.

  1. Priming વ્યવસ્થા વડે casing/suction line ભરો; શરૂઆતમાં delivery valve સૂચના મુજબ રાખો.
  2. અટવાયેલી હવા કાઢો; foot valve/non-return valve અને suction jointમાં leak નથી તે તપાસો.
  3. Prime થયા પછી જ ચાલુ કરો; સૂચના મુજબ delivery ખોલો/નિયંત્રો અને સ્થિર flow તપાસો.

Primingથી impeller liquidને energy આપે અને વધુ liquid ખેંચવા જરૂરી suction pressure difference બને. Suction ખોવાવું/air-lock અને dry running અટકે; dry runningથી seal ગરમ/નુકસાન પામે. Manufacturerની સલામત રીત પાળો.

Q4(c) Enlist types of belt drive and explain compounding belt drive.Q4(c) Belt driveના પ્રકારોની યાદી આપો અને compound belt drive સમજાવો.7 marks7 ગુણ

Question: Q4(c) Enlist types of belt drive and explain compounding belt drive.

Common types: open belt, crossed belt, quarter-turn belt, compound belt, stepped/cone-pulley drive, fast-and-loose pulley drive and idler/jockey-pulley drive.

Compound belt drive: used when a large overall speed change is needed in stages. The first belt connects the driver to a pulley on an intermediate shaft. A second pulley is fixed to that same intermediate shaft, and a second belt connects it to the final driven pulley. The two intermediate pulleys rotate at the same speed because they are keyed/fixed to one shaft. With negligible slip, the overall speed ratio is the product of the two stage ratios. Belt tension, alignment, guards and permissible speed must be considered.

In the sketch label driver, first belt, intermediate shaft with two fixed pulleys, second belt and final driven pulley. If both stages are open belts, the driver and final driven pulley rotate in the same direction.

પ્રશ્ન: Q4(c) Belt driveના પ્રકારોની યાદી આપો અને compound belt drive સમજાવો.

Question: Q4(a) OR · Write advantages of Reciprocating pump.

  • Positive-displacement action can deliver liquid against comparatively high head at a low/moderate flow rate, subject to design.

Question: Q4(b) OR · Explain centrifugal pump with sketch.

Main parts: impeller with curved vanes, volute/diffuser casing, suction pipe with strainer and foot valve (as fitted), delivery pipe and valve, shaft and driver. The pump is primed before starting.

  1. Liquid enters axially at the impeller eye.
  2. The rotating impeller gives the liquid velocity and throws it radially outward by centrifugal action.

Question: Q4(c) OR · Enlist types of gear train. Explain any two.

Types: simple, compound, reverted and epicyclic (planetary) gear trains.

1. Simple gear train: each shaft carries one gear. The driver meshes with the driven gear; one or more idler gears may be inserted to change direction or bridge centre distance. 2. Compound gear train: at least one intermediate shaft carries two gears rigidly fixed together. One gear meshes with the input gear; the second drives the next gear. Stage speed ratios multiply, enabling a large overall ratio in compact space.

Other types: a reverted train has input and output shafts coaxial (parallel axes in the usual arrangement); an epicyclic train has planet gears whose axes move around a central sun gear. Use an arrow to show direction and clearly mark gears fixed on the same shaft.

Question: Q5(a) OR · Write short note on hydraulic lift.

A hydraulic lift raises a platform/load using pressurised liquid and Pascal’s law: pressure applied to a confined fluid is transmitted throughout it. A small pump piston supplies pressure to a larger lifting cylinder; the larger piston produces a larger lifting force for the same pressure (force multiplication is related to piston areas). A reservoir, pump, check/control valves, cylinder, platform and safety devices form the system.

To lower, a controlled valve returns fluid to the reservoir. Uses include vehicle service lifts and goods platforms designed for the rated load. Benefits are smooth force multiplication and convenient lifting; limits include leakage, fluid/temperature sensitivity and maintenance needs. Use rated supports and locks; never work under a lift held only by hydraulic pressure.

Q5(b) OR · Explain vapour compression refrigeration system.Q5(b) OR · Vapour compression refrigeration system સમજાવો.4 marks4 ગુણ

Question: Q5(b) OR · Explain vapour compression refrigeration system.

The system circulates a refrigerant through four main components:

  1. Compressor: draws low-pressure vapour from the evaporator and raises its pressure and temperature using work.
  2. Condenser: rejects heat to surroundings; high-pressure refrigerant condenses to liquid (often after desuperheating).
  3. Expansion device: throttles liquid to lower pressure; some flashes to vapour and the mixture becomes cold.
  4. Evaporator: the low-pressure refrigerant absorbs heat from the refrigerated space and evaporates; vapour returns to the compressor.

Thus heat is moved from a cold space to warmer surroundings with work input. The loop is compressor → condenser → expansion device → evaporator → compressor. Show high/low-pressure sides and the direction of refrigerant flow. COP of refrigeration = cooling effect / work input; no cycle property calculation is needed here.

પ્રશ્ન: Q5(b) OR · Vapour compression refrigeration system સમજાવો.

Refrigerant ચાર મુખ્ય ભાગમાં ફરે છે:

  1. Compressor: evaporatorની low-pressure vapour ખેંચે અને workથી pressure/temperature વધારે.
  2. Condenser: બહાર heat છોડે; high-pressure refrigerant liquid બને (પહેલાં superheat દૂર થઈ શકે).
  3. Expansion device: liquidનું throttling કરી pressure ઘટાડે; થોડું vapour flash થાય અને મિશ્રણ ઠંડું બને.
  4. Evaporator: low-pressure refrigerant ઠંડી જગ્યા/હવામાંથી heat શોષી વરાળ બને; પાછી compressorમાં જાય.

આ રીતે work inputથી ઠંડી જગ્યાની heat ગરમ બહારના વાતાવરણમાં ખસે. Loop: compressor → condenser → expansion device → evaporator → compressor. High/low-pressure side અને refrigerantનો flow બતાવો. Refrigeration COP = cooling effect / work input; અહીં cycle propertyની ગણતરી જરૂરી નથી.

પ્રશ્ન: Q5(c) OR · Brakeનું વર્ગીકરણ કરો અને shoe brake સ્વચ્છ આકૃતિ સાથે સમજાવો.

વર્ગીકરણ: ચલાવવાની રીત પ્રમાણે mechanical, hydraulic, pneumatic અને electric/electromagnetic; friction element પ્રમાણે block/shoe, band, disc અને internal-expanding drum brake. Vehicle service/parking brakeમાં અલગ રીતો જોડાય શકે.

Shoe (block) brake: liningવાળું shoe lever, cam અથવા actuatorથી ફરતા drum/wheelની સપાટી પર દબાય. Normal forceથી બનેલું friction rotationનો વિરોધ કરે, braking torque આપે અને kinetic energyને મુખ્યત્વે heatમાં ફેરવે. બળ છોડતાં shoe દૂર જાય; રચના મુજબ return spring હોય. Friction coefficient, contact area, drumની સ્થિતિ અને heatનું વિસર્જન performanceને અસર કરે.

ફરતો drum, lined shoe, pivot/lever અથવા cam, applied-force arrow અને rotation/braking દિશા દોરો. યોગ્ય dutyનું rated brake અને guard વાપરો; ગરમ drumને અડશો નહીં.
