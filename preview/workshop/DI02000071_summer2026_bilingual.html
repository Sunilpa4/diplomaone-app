Q2 · Lami, parallelogram and square resultant

Q.2 (a) Define Lami's theorem with formula. (03 marks)Q.2 (a) Define Lami's theorem with formula. (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

If three concurrent coplanar forces keep body in equilibrium, each force proportional to sine of angle between other two.

F1/sinα = F2/sinβ = F3/sinγ where α opposite F1 etc.

Used for hanging loads, sphere between walls, jib.

ત્રણ concurrent બળ સંતુલન હોય તો દરેક બળ સામેના ખૂણા ના sin ના પ્રમાણમાં.

F1/sinα =F2/sinβ =F3/sinγ
Q.2 (b) Describe Law of parallelogram of forces. (04 marks)Q.2 (b) Describe Law of parallelogram of forces. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

If two concurrent forces P and Q at angle θ represented as adjacent sides, resultant is diagonal both magnitude and direction.

R² = P²+Q²+2PQ cosθ   tanα = Q sinθ/(P+Q cosθ) where α angle of R with P

For θ=90° → R=√(P²+Q²) tanα=Q/P. Base for triangle and polygon laws.

બે concurrent P Q θ ખૂણે બાજુ દર્શાવો તો diagonal પરિણામી.

R²=P²+Q²+2PQcosθ tanα=Qsinθ/(P+Qcosθ)
