Q.1 (c) Classify the Defects in Timber and Explain any three with figure. (7 marks)Q.1 (c) Classify the Defects in Timber and Explain any three with figure. (7 marks)7 marks૦૭ ગુણ

Types: Natural — knots, twisted grain. Conversion — warp, wane. Fungal — dry rot, sap stain. Insect — beetle holes.

Three explained:

  1. Knot: Base of branch embedded creates hard dark circle, reduces tensile and causes crack at knot. Avoid for beams.
  2. Shake: Crack along grain — star shake from frost, ring shake along annual ring, weakens shear.
  3. Warp: Board bends bow cup twist due to uneven shrinkage during seasoning, makes joints open.

Select straight clear grain, season properly, treat.

પ્રકાર: Natural — knot twisted, Conversion — warp wane, ફૂગ સડો, જીવ કાણાં.

  1. Knot: ડાળી ગાંઠ સખત કાળું વર્તુળ tensile ઘટાડે.
  2. Shake: અનાજ સાથે ચિરો — star frost થી ring annual ring પર.
  3. Warp: પાટિયું વળે uneven сукવણી થી સાંધા ખૂલે.

સીધું ગાંઠ વગર પસંદ કરો, seasoning બરાબર.

Q2 · Lime, building materials and properties

Q.2 (a) Define the Term for Lime : (1) Calcination (2) Slaking (3) Setting (3 marks)Q.2 (a) Define the Term for Lime : (1) Calcination (2) Slaking (3) Setting (3 marks)3 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Calcination: Heating limestone CaCO3 at 900°C to produce quick lime CaO and CO2 gas.

Slaking: Adding water to CaO with hissing and swelling gives Ca(OH)2 slaked lime plus heat, volume doubles, needs maturing.

Setting: Hardening of lime mortar — fat lime by carbonation Ca(OH)2 + CO2 → CaCO3 in air, hydraulic by hydration of silicates underwater.

Calcination: ચૂના પથ્થર CaCO3 900°C એ CaO + CO2.

Slaking: CaO માં પાણી ફૂંફાડા ફૂલે Ca(OH)2 + ગરમી.

Setting: સખત થવું — fat lime હવા માં CO2 થી CaCO3 hydraulic પાણી માં silicate થી.

Artificial is fast controlled chamber, not sun.

Kiln: Stack planks stickered in insulated chamber, hot air 40-90°C circulated with humidity from 90% to 40% gradually, 10-20 days depending thickness. Probes measure moisture stop at 12%.

Other: Chemical — soak in urea salt displaces water, electrical — high frequency, water boiling. Kiln gives uniform low warp, natural slow but cheap for large beams.

Artificial ઝડપી chamber:

Kiln: પાટિયા sticker સાથે chamber 40-90°C ભેજ 90% થી 40% 10-20 દિવસ 12% થાય ત્યાં સુધી.
