Q.1 (a) Define soil, sand and water content. (03 marks)Q.1 (a) Define soil, sand and water content. (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Soil: Natural aggregate of mineral particles with voids filled by water/air, formed by weathering of rocks.

Water content w: પાણીનું વજન / ઘન કણોનું વજન ·100%, oven drying થી મળે. English keywords: soil, sand, water content, void.

Q.1 (b) Draw three phase diagram of soil; give full name of all parts. (04 marks)Q.1 (b) Draw three phase diagram of soil; give full name of all parts. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Three-phase system: Soil has solids + water + air. Diagram shows volumes Va air, Vw water, Vs solids and weights Wa≈0, Ww, Ws.

Full names: Va=Volume of air, Vw=Volume of water, Vs=Volume of solids, Vv=Va+Vw voids, Ws=Weight of solids. Relations: n=Vv/V, e=Vv/Vs, Sr=Vw/Vv·100%.

Three-phase diagram: માટીમાં solids + water + air. Volumes V_a, V_w, V_s અને weights W_s, W_w.

V_v = V_a+V_w voids, n= V_v/V, e=V_v/V_s, Sr=V_w/V_v. English keywords: three phase, void ratio e, porosity n, degree of saturation Sr.

Q.1 (c) Explain core cutter method for determination of density of soil. (07 marks)Q.1 (c) Explain core cutter method for determination of density of soil. (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ

Objective: Find field bulk density γ = W/V and dry density γd=γ/[1+w] for cohesive soils using core cutter.

Apparatus [IS 2720 Part 29]: Cylindrical cutter 10 cm dia 12.7 cm height V≈1000 cc, dolly 2.5 cm, rammer, balance, palette, oven.

Step 1 — Calibration: Measure cutter inner dia D and height H, volume V=πD²H/4. Weigh empty cutter W1.

Step 2 — Field driving: Clean ground, place cutter with dolly, drive with rammer till dolly sinks, excavate, trim top/bottom flush with straight edge.

Step 3 — Weighing: Weigh cutter+soil W2, soil weight W=W2−W1, bulk density γ=W/V g/cm³.

Step 4 — Water content: Take representative sample, oven dry to get w.

Step 5 — Calculations: γd=γ/[1+w], void ratio e=Gγw/γd−1.

Precautions: Suitable only for soft cohesive soils, not gravel, drive vertical, avoid shattering, trim without voids.

Limitation & Use: Quick field γ for compaction control vs sand replacement for granular. Report γ, γd, w.

Core cutter method: Field માં bulk density γ=W/V કાઢવા, cohesive soil માટે.

Apparatus: Cutter 10 cm dia, dolly, rammer, balance, oven.

Steps: 1] V=πD²H/4 અને ખાલી વજન W1, 2] જમીન પર dolly સાથે rammer થી drive કરો, trim કરો, 3] W2 વજન કરી W=W2−W1 ⇒ γ=W/V, 4] નમૂનામાંથી w, 5] γd=γ/[1+w].

Care: Gravel માં નહીં, vertical drive. English keywords: bulk density, dry density, core cutter, water content.

Q.2 (c) Explain the method to find liquid limit of soil. (07 marks)Q.2 (c) Explain the method to find liquid limit of soil. (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ

Objective [IS 2720 Part 5]: Water content at which soil flows to close 12 mm groove after 25 blows in Casagrande apparatus.

Apparatus: Casagrande cup, grooving tools Type A/B, sieved soil <425 µm, spatula, 6–8 containers, oven.

Step 1 — Preparation: Take 120 g air-dried 425 µ soil, add distilled water, mix to paste, cure 24 h.

Step 2 — First trial: Place paste in cup max 10 mm thick, cut 12 mm groove along centre, rotate crank 2 rps, count blows N to close 12 mm at bottom. Take water content w.

Step 3 — Repeat: Change water to get N≈10–40, 4–5 readings. For each N measure w by oven drying.

Step 4 — Flow curve: Plot w [Y linear] vs log N [X]. Best-fit straight line. Liquid limit LL is w at N=25 from curve.

Step 5 — Alternative cone: 80 g cone 30°, penetration 20 mm in 5 s gives LL [IS].

Uses: Classification, PI=LL−PL, toughness index. Reproducible within 2%.

Liquid limit: 25 blows પર groove 12 mm બંધ થાય તે w.

Q.4 (b) Define cohesion, internal friction, shear strength and angle of repose. (04 marks)Q.4 (b) Define cohesion, internal friction, shear strength and angle of repose. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Cohesion c: Intermolecular bond shear resistance at zero normal stress, due to electrostatic forces in clay.

Angle internal friction φ: Resistance due to interlocking, τ=σ·tanφ at zero cohesion.

Shear strength τf: Max shear before failure, τf=c+σ·tanφ [Coulomb].

Angle of repose θ: Max heap slope without sliding, for granular θ≈φ.

Cohesion c: zero normal પર shear resistance clay માં.

φ: interlocking resistance.

τf=c+σ·tanφ: shear strength.

Angle of repose: heap નો max slope ≈φ. English keywords: cohesion, friction, shear strength.

Q.4 (c) Explain factors affecting Permeability in soil. (07 marks)Q.4 (c) Explain factors affecting Permeability in soil. (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ

Darcy's law: v=k·i, k coefficient of permeability.

1] Grain size: Coarse → high k ∝ D10² [Hazen k= C·D10²].

2] Void ratio e: k ∝ e³/[1+e] [Kozeny-Carman], compaction reduces k.

3] Composition: Clay mineral montmorillonite low k due to adsorbed water.

4] Structure: Flocculated higher k than dispersed.

5] Fabric/stratification: Horizontal k >> vertical k [kh≈kv·5].

6] Degree saturation: Partial saturation blocks voids with air, k falls.

7] Fluid viscosity/temperature: k ∝ γw/µ, warm water higher k.

Uses: Filter design needs controlled k, dam core low k.

k પર અસર: 1] Grain size D10², 2] Void ratio e³/[1+e], 3] Clay mineral, 4] Structure flocculated/dispersed, 5] k_h>k_v, 6] Saturation, 7] Viscosity/temperature.

English keywords: permeability, Darcy, void ratio, grain size.
