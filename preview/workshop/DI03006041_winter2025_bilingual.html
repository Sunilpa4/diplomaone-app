Q1 · Direct stress, strain, stress-strain curve & composite bar

Q.1 (a) Define following: (1) Direct stress (2) Linear strain (3) Poisson’s ratio (03 marks)Q.1 (a) Define following: (1) Direct stress (2) Linear strain (3) Poisson’s ratio (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

1) Direct stress: Internal resistance per unit area when load acts along axis of member. Tensile if pulling, compressive if pushing. Formula σ = P/A, unit N/mm². For 80 kN on 18 mm dia, σ = 80000 / 254 = 315 N/mm².

2) Linear strain: Change in length per original length along loading direction. ε = δL / L, no unit. For 4 m bar elongation 6.3 mm, ε = 0.00157.

3) Poisson’s ratio: Ratio of lateral strain to linear strain with negative sign. μ = −lateral / linear, about 0.25–0.30 for steel, 0.15 for concrete. Governs volumetric change.

1] ડાયરેક્ટ સ્ટ્રેસ: અક્ષ પર બળ લાગે ત્યારે એકમ ક્ષેત્રફળ દીઠ આંતરિક પ્રતિકાર. σ = P/A, N/mm². 80 kN 18 mm પર 315 N/mm².

2] લિનિયર સ્ટ્રેઈન: લંબાઈમાં ફેરફાર / મૂળ લંબાઈ. ε = δL/L. 4 m પર 6.3 mm તો 0.00157.

3] પોઈસન ગુણોત્તર: બાજુની સ્ટ્રેઈન / લિનિયર સ્ટ્રેઈન. μ લગભગ 0.25–0.30 સ્ટીલ.

Q.1 (b) Draw stress-strain curve for mild steel bar giving salient points on it. (04 marks)Q.1 (b) Draw stress-strain curve for mild steel bar giving salient points on it. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Mild steel curve has Proportional limit Hooke’s law up to 250 N/mm², Elastic limit just beyond, Upper and lower yield drop at 250, Strain hardening rising to Ultimate 400–500, then Necking to Fracture. Elastic region slope is E = 2×10⁵. Yield plateau shows large strain without load increase. Used to know safe stress with factor of safety.

Q2 · Moment of Inertia — theorems, ratios & T-section & SFD/BMD

Q.2 (a) (1) Write statement of parallel axis theorem with equation. (2) What will be ratio of Ixx and ymax for circular section? (03 marks)Q.2 (a) (1) Write statement of parallel axis theorem with equation. (2) What will be ratio of Ixx and ymax for circular section? (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

1) Parallel axis: MOI about any axis parallel to centroidal axis = I_G + A·d² where I_G is centroidal MOI, A area, d distance between axes. Used to shift I from centroid to base.

2) Circle: For diameter D, I_xx = πD⁴/64, y_max = D/2, ratio I_xx / y_max = πD³/32 = (πD⁴/64)/(D/2). Also section modulus Z = I/y = πD³/32.

1] સમાંતર અક્ષ: I = I_G + A·d², I_G સેન્ટ્રોઇડલ, A ક્ષેત્ર, d અંતર.

2] વર્તુળ: I_xx = πD⁴/64, y_max = D/2, ગુણોત્તર = πD³/32 = Z.

Q3 · Beams — types, contra-flexure, bending stress & shear stress

Q.3 (a) Give three examples each of statically determinate beams and statically indeterminate beams. (03 marks)Q.3 (a) Give three examples each of statically determinate beams and statically indeterminate beams. (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Determinate: Simply supported, cantilever, overhang with one hinge — equilibrium alone gives reactions.

Indeterminate: Propped cantilever, fixed both ends, continuous 2-span — need compatibility beyond equilibrium. Degree = extra unknowns.

ડિટરમિનેટ: સાદો ટેકો, કેન્ટીલીવર, ઓવરહેંગ — સમતુલાથી પ્રતિક્રિયા.

ઇન્ડિટરમિનેટ: પ્રોપ્ડ કેન્ટીલીવર, બંને છેડે ફિક્સ, સતત — વધારાની સુસંગતતા જોઈએ.

Q.3 (b) Explain importance of point of contra flexure. (04 marks)Q.3 (b) Explain importance of point of contra flexure. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Point of contra-flexure is where bending moment is zero and changes sign — sagging to hogging. At this point, bending stress zero, shear may be max. Important: reinforcement curtailed, hinge can be provided, deflection inflection, design needs to know where tension shifts from bottom to top.

કોન્ટ્રા-ફ્લેક્સર જ્યાં બેન્ડિંગ મોમેન્ટ શૂન્ય અને નિશાની બદલે — સેગિંગ થી હોગિંગ. ત્યાં તાણ શૂન્ય, સ્ટીલ ઘટાડાય, હિન્જ મૂકાય.

Q.3 (a) OR Define shear force and bending moment. (03 marks)Q.3 (a) OR Define shear force and bending moment. (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Shear force: Algebraic sum of vertical forces to one side of section, tries to shear beam.

Bending moment: Sum of moments about section due to forces, causes bending — sagging positive concave up, hogging negative.

શીયર ફોર્સ: એક બાજુના ઊભા બળોનો સરવાળો, કાપવાનો પ્રયાસ.
