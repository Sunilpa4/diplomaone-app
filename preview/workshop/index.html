What to look for

  • Coverage: every syllabus line of the unit has its own card.
  • Depth: definitions are one clear sentence; explanations add the “why”, not just labels.
  • Numericals: Given → Formula → Steps → bold answer with unit → check line.
  • Derivations: symbols defined, assumptions stated, ≤6 ordered steps, final relation + validity.
  • Diagrams: no clipped label, arrows not crossing text, one idea per diagram (tap to zoom).
  • Language: English-only units must have no Gujarati text.
  • Exam practice: marks are suggested (3/4/7) and every question is answered in the last notes section.
