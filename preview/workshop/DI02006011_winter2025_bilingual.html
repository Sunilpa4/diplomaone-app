Q1 · RF, offsets and principles

Q.1 (a) Define: (1) Representative fraction (2) Plan (3) Map (03 marks)Q.1 (a) Define: (1) Representative fraction (2) Plan (3) Map (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

(1) Representative Fraction (RF): The ratio of a distance on the map/plan to the corresponding ground distance, both in same units. RF = 1 : (Ground / Map). Example: RF 1:50000 means 1 cm on sheet = 50000 cm = 500 m on ground. RF is unitless. If RF >1:5000 it is large scale (building), if <1:50000 small scale (state).

(2) Plan: Large-scale (1:500 to 1:5000) representation of a small area where earth's curvature is ignored and details like walls, pipes, RLs are shown to scale for execution.

(3) Map: Small-scale (1:50,000 upwards) representation of a large area where curvature and projection are considered, showing topography, rivers, roads for planning.

RF: નકશા અંતર અને જમીન અંતરનો ગુણોત્તર — 1:50000 =1cm =500m. Unitless.

Plan: મોટો scale 1:500-5000 નાનો વિસ્તાર curvature ignore — execution માટે.

Map: Small scale 1:50000+ મોટો વિસ્તાર curvature consider — planning માટે.

Q.1 (b) Define: (1) Offset (2) Tie line (3) Base line (4) Check line (04 marks)Q.1 (b) Define: (1) Offset (2) Tie line (3) Base line (4) Check line (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

(1) Offset: Short lateral distance measured perpendicular (or sometimes oblique) from the chain line to a detail (corner of building, tree). Kept <15m to avoid sag error. Measured with tape after setting right angle with cross-staff.

(2) Tie line: A subsidiary line joining two tie stations on the main lines. It ties down interior details far from main lines and provides a check. Example: Across a large field, a tie line between two chain lines locates a pond centrally.

(3) Base line: The longest main line of the framework, on which all other lines depend. Chosen along centre of area, on firm level ground, with clear intervisibility. Its length is measured twice precisely.

(4) Check line (proof line): A line joining the apex of a triangle to a point on the opposite side, measured to verify field + plotting accuracy. If measured = plotted length, work is correct.

Offset: chain line થી detail નું ટૂંકું લંબ અંતર — 15m નીચે.

Tie line: બે chain lines ને જોડતી subsidiary — interior detail locate, check.

Base line: સૌથી લાંબી main line — framework તેના પર, સપાટ મજબૂત જમીન.

Check line: triangle apex થી સામે બાજુ પર — accuracy proof.

Q.1 (c) State the principles of Surveying and explain with help of sketch (07 marks)Q.1 (c) State the principles of Surveying and explain with help of sketch (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ

Principle-1 Whole to Part: As in Q1a Summer — establish primary control (A-B-C triangle) with highest accuracy, then fill minors (d,e,f inside). Error of a minor stays local, control remains undistorted. Procedure: Reconnaissance → select base line → triangulate → then chain offsets. Opposite (part to whole) would accumulate error and rotate entire survey.

Principle-2 Fix by two measures: Every new point needs at least two independent measurements — linear (chain distances from two known points = arc-arc intersection) or angular (bearing + distance). This gives uniqueness + check.

Application: In the sketch, main triangle ABC is established, then point D is fixed by distances from A and B, point E from B and C. Check lines AD, BE prove accuracy.

આખા થી ભાગ: મોટું triangle ABC પહેલા, પછી D,E બે અંતરથી — ભૂલ સ્થાનિક.

બે માપથી fix: દરેક નવો point બે અંતર/દિશાથી — check મળે.

Q.2 (b) List out different methods used for computation of area by taking offsets and explain any one in detail. (04 marks)Q.2 (b) List out different methods used for computation of area by taking offsets and explain any one in detail. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Methods: Mid-ordinate, Average ordinate, Trapezoidal, Simpson's 1/3rd.

Simpson's 1/3rd in detail (most accurate for parabolic boundaries): Requires even number of intervals (odd number of ordinates). Area = (d/3)[O₁ + Oₙ + 4(O₂+O₄+…) + 2(O₃+O₅+…)] where d = common interval between offsets, Oᵢ = ordinate length. Derivation: Three consecutive points define a parabola; integrate. Example: d=10m, O=2,3,5,4,6 → Area =10/3[2+6+4(3+4)+2(5)]=10/3[8+28+10]=153.33 m². Limitation: d constant, even intervals, last ordinate if even number of intervals not handled (add trapezoid for last).

Mid, Average, Trapezoidal, Simpson — Simpson: d/3[O1+On+4 odd+2 even], even intervals, parabola, સૌથી ચોક્કસ.

Q.2 (c) OR Write Advantages and Disadvantages of Plain Table Survey. (07 marks)Q.2 (c) OR Write Advantages and Disadvantages of Plain Table Survey. (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ

Advantages (3.5 marks — 6 points): 1) Field & office combined — no booking error. 2) Missing details noticed on ground immediately. 3) No chain/tape transcription — direct plot to scale. 4) Cheapest for small surveys, contour interpolation on sheet. 5) No magnetic effect if oriented by backsight. 6) Map ready immediately.

Disadvantages: 1) Not for wet/windy weather — paper expands. 2) Not precise — alidade accuracy 1:1000 vs theodolite 1:5000. 3) Heavy board + tripod to carry. 4) Replotting difficult if sheet lost. 5) Needs skilled operator for orientation; small error magnifies.

ફાયદા: field જ office — ભૂલ ઓછી, છૂટી વિગત દેખાય, સસ્તું. ગેરફાયદા: પવન/વરસાદમાં નહીં, precise નહીં, ભારે, skilled.

Q3 · Bearings, adjustment and conversions

Q.3 (a) Define: i) Magnetic bearing ii) Fore bearing iii) Isogonic lines. (03 marks)Q.3 (a) Define: i) Magnetic bearing ii) Fore bearing iii) Isogonic lines. (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

i) Magnetic bearing: Horizontal angle between the line and magnetic north (direction of magnetic needle), measured clockwise 0–360° WCB or 0–90° QB. Changes slowly due to secular variation; needs declination correction to true bearing (True = Magnetic ± Declination).

ii) Fore bearing (FB): Bearing of a line measured in the direction of survey progress (A→B). The opposite B→A is Back bearing (BB = FB±180°). FB and BB differ by 180° if no local attraction.

iii) Isogonic lines: Imaginary lines on earth's surface joining points of equal magnetic declination at a given epoch. Agonic line joins zero declination. Used on navigation charts to correct bearings.

Magnetic bearing: magnetic north થી ખૂણો. Fore bearing: survey આગળ direction A→B, BB opposite ±180°. Isogonic: સરખા declination ને જોડતી રેખા, agonic zero.

Q.3 (b) Explain Temporary adjustment of Prismatic compass. (04 marks)Q.3 (b) Explain Temporary adjustment of Prismatic compass. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ
  1. Fixing & centering: Screw box to tripod, spread legs, plumb bob exactly over peg. For precise work shift tripod bodily.
  2. Levelling: Release ball-socket, tilt box until circular bubble is central. This frees the ring to swing without friction.
  3. Focussing prism: Slide prism jacket until graduations are sharp. Adjust for eye.
  4. Observing bearing: Release brake, sight object through eye slit bisecting vane wire, read WCB under prism simultaneously. Record, then move to next station; BB should be FB±180°.

Check all adjustments at every station; poor levelling is the commonest 1–2° error.

1) Plumb થી centering, 2) bubble centre levelling, 3) prism focus, 4) eye slit થી sight કરી prism નીચે WCB વાંચો — BB = FB±180° check.

Q.3 (b) OR Enlist different methods of Plane table surveying and explain any one in detail. (04 marks)Q.3 (b) OR Enlist different methods of Plane table surveying and explain any one in detail. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Four methods: Radiation, Intersection, Traversing, Resection.

Q4 · Levelling, HI and contour terms

Q.4 (a) Define: (1) Levelling (2) Reduced level (3) Bench Mark (03 marks)Q.4 (a) Define: (1) Levelling (2) Reduced level (3) Bench Mark (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

(1) Levelling: Branch of surveying to determine elevation of points with respect to a common datum (mean sea level). Done with level + staff.

(2) Reduced Level (RL): Vertical distance of a point above/below datum (GTS datum 0 at Karachi). Example: BM RL 100.00 means 100m above datum.

(3) Bench Mark (BM): Fixed permanent reference point of known RL. Types: GTS (Survey of India precise), Permanent (PWD building plinth), Arbitrary (site assumed 100m), Temporary (site peg).

Levelling: datum થી ઊંચાઈ માપન. RL: datum થી vertical અંતર. BM: જાણીતો RL નો કાયમી point — GTS, permanent, arbitrary, temporary.

Q.4 (b) Explain Contour Interval and Horizontal equivalent. (04 marks)Q.4 (b) Explain Contour Interval and Horizontal equivalent. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Contour Interval (CI): The constant vertical distance between successive contours. Chosen based on scale, purpose and relief. Example: CI 1m for 1:1000 building, 20m for 1:50,000 hilly survey. CI constant on a sheet.

Horizontal Equivalent (HE): The horizontal distance between two successive contours measured perpendicular to them. Not constant — small HE where slope steep (close contours), large where flat (wide contours). Relation: Gradient = CI / HE. Example: CI 1m, HE 10m → 1:10 slope.

CI: બે contours વચ્ચે vertical અંતર fixed — 1m building, 20m hill. HE: horizontal અંતર variable — steep નજીક, flat દૂર, gradient = CI/HE.

Q.4 (a) OR Define: (1) Line of collimation (2) Back Sight (3) Fore Sight (03 marks)Q.4 (a) OR Define: (1) Line of collimation (2) Back Sight (3) Fore Sight (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

(1) Line of collimation: The line joining optical centre of objective and intersection of cross-hairs. When levelled, it is horizontal and gives RL via HI. Error checked by two-peg test.

Collimation: objective કેન્દ્ર અને cross-hair જોડતી રેખા. BS: setup પછી પ્રથમ વાંચન known RL પર. FS: shift પહેલા છેલ્લું વાંચન.

Q5 · Levelling errors, theodolite and contour

Q.5 (a) Enlist Errors in levelling and explain anyone. (03 marks)Q.5 (a) Enlist Errors in levelling and explain anyone. (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Types: Instrumental (collimation, bubble, staff zero), Personal (parallax, reading), Natural (curvature, refraction, wind, settlement).

Detail — Collimation error: Line of collimation not horizontal when bubble centre. Effect ∝ distance; on long sight gives 2–3cm error. Eliminated by keeping BS and FS distances equal (balancing) or by two-peg test correction (adjust diaphragm).

યંત્ર (collimation, bubble), વ્યક્તિ (parallax), natural (curvature). Collimation: bubble centre છતાં line horizontal નહીં — BS/FS અંતર સરખા રાખો અથવા two-peg test થી સુધારો.

Q.5 (b) Explain Temporary adjustment of theodolite. (04 marks)Q.5 (b) Explain Temporary adjustment of theodolite. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

1) Setting & centering: Tripod over peg, plumb bob exact.

2) Levelling: Plate bubble parallel to two screws — turn opposite equally till centre, rotate 90° use third screw. Repeat till centre in all positions → vertical axis truly vertical.

Q.5 (b) OR Explain Following terms with respect to Theodolite (1) Transiting (2) Face left observation (3) Line of Collimation (4) Trunnion axis (04 marks)Q.5 (b) OR Explain Following terms with respect to Theodolite (1) Transiting (2) Face left observation (3) Line of Collimation (4) Trunnion axis (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

(1) Transiting (Plunging): Rotating telescope 180° about horizontal axis to point opposite direction — reverses line of collimation for extending line or face left/right.

(2) Face left observation: When vertical circle is on observer's left while sighting. Mean of face left + face right eliminates collimation and index errors.

(3) Line of Collimation: Optical axis — objective centre to cross-hair intersection. Must be perpendicular to horizontal axis (collimation adjustment).

(4) Trunnion (Horizontal) axis: Axis about which telescope tilts vertically, supported by A-frames. Must be perpendicular to vertical axis.

Transiting: telescope 180° vertical flip. Face left: circle ડાબે — mean થી error cancel. Collimation: optical axis. Trunnion: vertical tilt નો આડો અક્ષ.
