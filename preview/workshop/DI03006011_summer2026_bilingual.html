4. Drainage correction + dewateringWater table rise, scourFench drain, sump pumps, apron, fix leaking pipesPrevent softening
5. Load reduction / re-levellingOverloading added floorsRemove extra load, lift slab with jacks, re-pack grout, monitor with levels 1 mmRestore level

Part-B Prevention by proper setting out — 7 steps before concrete:

Given: Long concrete pavement/slab expands in summer and contracts in winter, length large → thermal stress causes random cracks, blow-up, faulting.
Concept: Construction joint is deliberate weak plane to control crack location and allow movement with load transfer devices.
Type of joint needed for long pavementSpacingConstruction detailPurpose why provided
1. Transverse Expansion joint — primaryEvery 30–45 m full depth20–25 mm wide gap with compressible filler board + dowel bars 20 mm dia @300 mm c/c half bonded for load transfer + sealant on topAllows expansion in heat prevents blow-up/compression failure, dowels keep levels equal
2. Transverse Contraction / Control jointEvery 4–5 m, depth 1/3 slabShallow groove saw cut 3–4 mm wide, no gap, aggregate interlock transfers loadControls shrinkage cracking to straight line not random, creates deliberate crack
3. Longitudinal joint at lane centreContinuous along centre of 7 m wide pavementButt joint with tie bars 10 mm @500 mmHolds lanes together, prevents longitudinal crack from warping
4. Construction joint at day's endAt pour stopKeyed or dowelled vertical faceBinds old to new concrete

What happens without joints: Random map cracks 2–3 m apart, faulting steps, blow-up heave in summer, water ingress corrodes sub-base, riding comfort lost, early failure. Joints guide cracks to planned lines, sealed against water.

Final Answer: Long pavement needs Expansion joints 30–45 m with filler + dowels, Contraction joints 4–5 m groove, Longitudinal tie-bar joint at centre, Construction keyed joint at pour end — to control thermal crack, allow movement and maintain load transfer.

Excavating: Dig, cut, move earth — excavator, backhoe, dozer, dragline, clam shell, scraper. Works horizontally/vertically on ground.

Hoisting: Lift and place vertically — tower/mobile crane, derrick, hoist, winch. Moves material height, no digging. Some machines like excavator with hook do both but primary classes differ by function and site need.

Excavating: ખોદે કાપે માટી ખસેડે — excavator, backhoe, dozer, dragline, clam shell. જમીન પર.

Hoisting: ઊંચકે મૂકે — tower crane, derrick, hoist. ઊભું હલન, ખોદે નહીં.
