Q.1 (c) A site has black cotton soil with high swelling and shrinkage properties. As a site engineer, which type of foundation would you recommend for a single-storey residential building? Justify your selection with reasons. (07 marks)Q.1 (c) A site has black cotton soil with high swelling and shrinkage properties. As a site engineer, which type of foundation would you recommend for a single-storey residential building? Justify your selection with reasons. (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ

Problem: Black cotton swells on wetting heaves floor, shrinks on drying cracks walls. Active zone 2–3.5 m seasonal movement.

Recommended: Under-reamed pile foundation with plinth beam + granular cushion.

1. Bulbs 2–3 nos at 3–3.5 m depth anchor below active zone, uplift resisted. 2. Pile cap + plinth beam ties building acts as one. 3. 100 mm sand/murrum blanket breaks capillary and swelling pressure. 4. Economical for single storey vs raft — less concrete. Alternative low-cost: Strip raft with CNS cohesive non-swelling soil replacement 1 m + sand.

Not allowed: Shallow strip directly on black cotton — severe cracks.

સમસ્યા: કાળી માટી ભીની ફૂલે સૂકી તિરાડે active zone 2–3.5 m.

ભલામણ: Under-reamed pile + plinth beam + granular blanket.

1. 3–3.5 m ઊંડે bulbs active zone નીચે anchor. 2. Plinth beam બાંધે. 3. 100 mm રેતી પથારી swelling કાપે. 4. એક માળ માટે કરકસર. વિકલ્પ: CNS માટી બદલી + raft. છીછરો strip સીધો નહીં — તિરાડ.

Q.1 (c) OR During the construction of a commercial building, differential settlement is observed in the foundation. Suggest possible remedial measures and explain how proper setting out of foundation layout could have prevented this failure. (07 marks)Q.1 (c) OR During the construction of a commercial building, differential settlement is observed in the foundation. Suggest possible remedial measures and explain how proper setting out of foundation layout could have prevented this failure. (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ

Differential settlement is unequal settlement causing cracks, tilt.

Remedial: 1. Grouting cement grout under footing, 2. Underpinning extend to hard strata with piles, 3. Jacketing enlarge footing, 4. Drainage correction, 5. Load reduction. Re-level and monitor.

Prevention by setting out: Establish base line, 3-4-5 right angle, peg every corner, string lines, 1 m offset for trench, diagonal check equal, levels to avoid eccentric loading, check against drawing before concrete.

અસમાન બેસવું તિરાડ નમવું.

ઉપાય: Grouting, Underpinning ઊંડા hard પર, Jacketing મોટું, Drainage સુધારો, ભાર ઘટાડો.

રોકવા setting out: Base line, 3-4-5 કાટખૂણો, દરેક ખૂણે peg, 1 m offset, diagonal સરખું, level થી eccentric નહીં.

Q.2 (c) OR A long concrete pavement is being constructed. Which type of construction joint should be provided, and why? (07 marks)Q.2 (c) OR A long concrete pavement is being constructed. Which type of construction joint should be provided, and why? (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ

Long concrete expands/contracts with temperature. Provide Transverse expansion joints 20 mm wide every 30–45 m with filler and dowels for load transfer; Contraction joints every 4–5 m shallow groove to control random cracks; Longitudinal joint at lane centre with tie bars. Without them slab cracks uncontrolled, faulting, blow-up.

લાંબો pavement તાપમાને વધે ઘટે. Expansion 30–45 m 20 mm filler + dowels, Contraction 4–5 m છીછરી ખાંચ તિરાડ કાબુ, Longitudinal lane મધ્યે tie bars. નહીં તો random તિરાડ faulting.

Q.3 (a) Explain the difference between excavating machinery and hoisting machinery. (03 marks)Q.3 (a) Explain the difference between excavating machinery and hoisting machinery. (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Excavating: Dig, cut, move earth — excavator, backhoe, dozer, dragline, clam shell, scraper. Works horizontally/vertically on ground.

Q.3 (c) OR A water retaining structure has developed leakage cracks. Which method (grouting or guniting) would you suggest and why? (07 marks)Q.3 (c) OR A water retaining structure has developed leakage cracks. Which method (grouting or guniting) would you suggest and why? (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ

For deep leakage cracks: Grouting first. Cement/epoxy grout injected under pressure fills internal voids, restores water tightness. Need packers, pressure 2–5 kg/cm2. Guniting is sprayed mortar 25–50 mm for surface repair, protective layer after grouting. For surface honeycomb only — guniting alone. Water retaining must be pressure sealed internally — grouting primary, guniting secondary.

ઊંડી લીક તિરાડ: Grouting પહેલાં. સિમેન્ટ/epoxy grout દબાણથી અંદર ખાલી પૂરે પાણી રોકે 2–5 kg/cm2. Guniting 25–50 mm spray સપાટી રિપેર, grouting પછી રક્ષણ. માત્ર સપાટી honeycomb હોય તો guniting એકલું. પાણી રોકવા અંદરથી સીલ — grouting મુખ્ય.

Q4 · Wiring, dampness, bonds, joints, concrete, w/c ratio

Q.4 (a) Explain the difference between series wiring and parallel wiring in electrification. (03 marks)Q.4 (a) Explain the difference between series wiring and parallel wiring in electrification. (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Series: Single path — current same, voltage divides, one bulb fails all off, dimmer, not used for buildings.

Parallel: Each load gets full voltage independently, failure of one does not affect others, brighter, used for houses. Wiring more but reliable.

Series: એક માર્ગ — એક બગડે બધા બંધ, voltage વહેંચાય, ઝાંખું. Parallel: દરેકને પૂરું voltage independent, એક બગડે બીજું ચાલુ, ઘરમાં વપરાય.

Q.4 (b) List sources of dampness in buildings (Any four). (04 marks)Q.4 (b) List sources of dampness in buildings (Any four). (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

1. Rain penetration through wall/roof cracks. 2. Rising capillary moisture from ground. 3. Condensation from temperature difference. 4. Leaking pipes and drains. 5. Poor drainage around plinth. 6. Groundwater table high.

1. વરસાદ દિવાલ તિરાડથી. 2. જમીનથી capillary ઊંચે. 3. ઠંડી ગરમી condensation. 4. પાઇપ લીક. 5. plinth પાસે drainage નબળું. 6. ભૂગર્ભ પાણી ઊંચું.

Q.4 (c) Explain the difference between English bond and Flemish bond. (07 marks)Q.4 (c) Explain the difference between English bond and Flemish bond. (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ

English bond: Alternate courses all headers then all stretchers, vertical joints broken with queen closer next to quoin header. Strongest, 1/4 brick lap, best for load bearing.

Flemish bond: Each course alternates header and stretcher within same course. Better appearance, uses more bats, slightly weaker, requires skilled mason. Variant Double/Single Flemish.

અંગ્રેજી: એક કોર્સ બધા હેડર, બીજો બધા સ્ટ્રેચર, queen closer થી સાંધા તૂટે. સૌથી મજબૂત ભારવાહી માટે.

ફ્લેમિશ: દરેક કોર્સમાં હેડર સ્ટ્રેચર બદલે. દેખાવ સુંદર, થોડી નબળી, કુશળ કારીગર જોઈએ.

Q.4 (a) OR Explain the difference between expansion joints and contraction joints. (03 marks)Q.4 (a) OR Explain the difference between expansion joints and contraction joints. (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Expansion joint: Full gap 12–25 mm with filler and dowels to allow expansion in heat, prevents blow-up. Every 30 m.

Contraction joint: Weakened plane 1/3 depth groove to control shrinkage cracking, no gap, every 4–5 m. Load transfer by aggregate interlock.

Expansion: 12–25 mm ખાલી filler + dowels ગરમીમાં વધવા દે, blow-up રોકે 30 m.

Contraction: 1/3 ઊંડાઈ ખાંચ shrinkage તિરાડ કાબુ 4–5 m, gap નહીં.

Q.4 (b) OR List the main ingredients of concrete. (04 marks)Q.4 (b) OR List the main ingredients of concrete. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Cement OPC/PPC, Fine aggregate river sand Zone II, Coarse aggregate 10–20 mm, Clean potable water, Admixtures plasticizer/retarder/air entraining as needed. Ratio forms M20, M30 etc.

સિમેન્ટ, રેતી Zone II, કપચી 10–20 mm, સ્વચ્છ પાણી, admixture જરૂર મુજબ. પ્રમાણ M20 M30.

Q.4 (c) OR Explain why water-cement ratio is important in concrete mix design. (07 marks)Q.4 (c) OR Explain why water-cement ratio is important in concrete mix design. (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ

Water-cement ratio controls strength, workability, durability, permeability. Abrams law: strength inversely proportional to w/c. High w/c >0.6 gives weak porous permeable, low <0.35 incomplete hydration harsh mix. Optimum 0.40–0.50 for RCC balances workability and strength. Lower w/c with plasticizer gives high strength durable concrete.

પાણી-સિમેન્ટ ગુણોત્તર strength, workability, durability, permeability નક્કી. Abrams નિયમ: w/c વધુ strength ઓછી. >0.6 નબળું છિદ્રાળું, <0.35 અધૂરું hydration. RCC માટે 0.40–0.50 ઉત્તમ, plasticizer થી વધુ strength.

Q.5 (b) OR A worker is assigned to work on scaffolding at 10 m height. Which safety equipment will you provide, and why? (04 marks)Q.5 (b) OR A worker is assigned to work on scaffolding at 10 m height. Which safety equipment will you provide, and why? (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

At 10 m fall fatal: Full body harness with shock absorber anchored to independent lifeline, Helmet, Non-slip safety shoes, Guard rails 1 m + mid rail + toe board on platform, Safety net below, Safe ladder. Reason: arrest fall, protect head/feet, prevent object fall.
