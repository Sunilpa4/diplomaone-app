Q1 · Principles, ranging and WCB angles

Q.1 (a) Explain Principles of surveying. (03 marks)Q.1 (a) Explain Principles of surveying. (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

1) Work from whole to part: First establish a master control framework with high accuracy — primary traverse or triangulation covering the whole area. Then fill interior details with lower accuracy. If error occurs in a detail, it stays local and does not distort the master. Opposite (part to whole) accumulates errors and can shift the entire survey.

2) Fix every point by at least two independent measurements: A new station is located by two distances, or a distance + direction, or two angles from two known points. This gives a check — the two arcs/rays must intersect at one point. A single linear measurement leaves ambiguity.

Corollary: Provide independent checks (closing traverse, tie lines), work systematically, and choose scale/accuracy suitable for purpose.

1) આખા થી ભાગ તરફ: પહેલા આખા વિસ્તાર માટે મોટું કંટ્રોલ નેટવર્ક ચોકસાઈથી બનાવો, પછી અંદરના નાના પોઇન્ટ ઓછી ચોકસાઈથી ભરો. ભૂલ સ્થાનિક રહે, આખો નકશો બગડે નહીં.

2) દરેક નવો પોઇન્ટ બે સ્વતંત્ર માપથી નક્કી: બે અંતર અથવા અંતર+દિશા અથવા બે ખૂણા — બે કમાન એક બિંદુએ મળે તો જ પોઇન્ટ સાચો. એક માપથી ચેક ન થાય.

Q.1 (b) Explain Types of ranging in detail. (04 marks)Q.1 (b) Explain Types of ranging in detail. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Direct ranging — when ends intervisible: Observer behind one end rod aligns intermediate rods by eye or with a line ranger. One rod at a time is moved until its flag covers the far rod. Fastest, used on flat open ground, accuracy ~1:1000.

Indirect / reciprocal ranging — when rising ground or hill blocks view: Two surveyors stand at intermediate points M and N. Each sees the other and the far end. They alternately direct each other into line until both are simultaneously on AB. Needs 4-6 iterations.

Random line ranging: When a forest or building completely blocks view, run a random traverse close to the true line, offset to the blocked point and back-calculate. Slowest.

Choice depends on visibility and urgency.

Direct રેન્જિંગ: બંને છેડા દેખાય ત્યારે eye અથવા line ranger થી વચ્ચેના rod લાઇનમાં ગોઠવો. સપાટ ખુલ્લી જમીનમાં ઝડપી.

Indirect / reciprocal: વચ્ચે ટેકરી હોય ત્યારે બે વ્યક્તિ M અને N પર ઊભા રહી વારાફરતી એકબીજાને direction આપી લાઇનમાં લાવે — 4-6 પ્રયાસે બંને AB પર આવે.

Random line: જંગલ/મકાન આડે હોય ત્યારે નજીકની random line ચલાવી offset થી સાચી line કાઢો.

Whole Circle Bearing (WCB): Angle measured clockwise from magnetic or true north, 0° to 360°. North 0°/360°, East 90°, South 180°, West 270°. Advantage: Single value, no quadrant confusion, ideal for traverse calculation. Back bearing = FB ±180°.

Given FB and BB differ by exactly 180° for every line → no local attraction, survey is consistent.

Rule for interior: At each vertex, interior = Back bearing of previous line − Fore bearing of next line. If result >180°, interior = 360° − result (exterior to interior). For closed polygon sum must be (n−2)×180°.

If sum differed, error is distributed equally to all angles.

Q.2 (b) State methods of Plane Tabling and explain any one in detail. (04 marks)Q.2 (b) State methods of Plane Tabling and explain any one in detail. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Four methods: (i) Radiation (ii) Intersection (iii) Traversing (iv) Resection.

Radiation — detailed steps for 4 marks: 1) Choose a central station O that sees all details. 2) Set plane table over O, centre with plumb fork, level with spirit level, orient with trough compass to north. 3) Pin paper, mark O, sight each detail point A,B,C with alidade touching O, draw rays OA, OB... 4) Measure ground distance OA with chain/tape, scale it on the ray (e.g., 1cm=2m), prick point a. Repeat for all. 5) Join detail points for outline. Ideal for small open plot, one setup covers all, quick but error radiates from one point so orientation must be precise.

Intersection needs two stations, traversing links many stations, resection locates the table itself.

ચાર પદ્ધતિ: Radiation, Intersection, Traversing, Resection.

Radiation વિગત: મધ્યમાં O પસંદ કરો, table centre-level-orient, alidade થી દરેક detail તરફ કિરણ દોરો, chain થી અંતર માપી scale થી point મૂકો — નાના પ્લોટ માટે એક setup માં ઝડપી, પણ orientation ચોક્કસ.

Q.2 (c) Explain Temporary adjustment of Prismatic compass. (07 marks)Q.2 (c) Explain Temporary adjustment of Prismatic compass. (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ
  1. Fixing to tripod: Screw compass box to tripod head firmly. Tripod legs spread ~60°, shoes firmly pressed. Height chest level.
  2. Centering over station: Drop plumb bob from box centre to peg. Shift tripod bodily until bob is exact. For precise work use plumb fork.
  3. Levelling — bring bubble central: Ball-and-socket joint is loosened, box tilted until circular bubble is centre. This lets graduated ring swing freely without touching glass — otherwise friction causes 1°–2° error.
  4. Focussing the prism: Slide prism up/down until graduations appear sharp and vertical. Clean lens, adjust for eye.
  5. Checking freedom of needle: Lift brake pin, needle should swing freely and settle. If sluggish, magnetize or clean pivot.
  6. Taking bearing: Release brake, sight through eye slit to object vane wire, read graduation under prism at the same instant. Record FB from A→B, shift to B read BB (should be FB±180°). If not, local attraction present.

All four adjustments must be repeated at every station; levelling is most critical.

Temporary adjustment: 1) Tripod પર fix. 2) Plumb bob થી centering. 3) Bubble centre કરી levelling — ring free ફરે. 4) Prism focus સાફ દેખાય. 5) Brake છોડી સોય free ફરે છે કે check. પછી eye slit થી sight કરી prism નીચે bearing વાંચો — FB અને BB 180° તફાવત check.

Q.2 (a) OR Define (i) Contour (ii) Fore sight (iii) Axis of telescope (03 marks)Q.2 (a) OR Define (i) Contour (ii) Fore sight (iii) Axis of telescope (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

(i) Contour: An imaginary line on ground joining points of equal elevation above datum (mean sea level). In plan it is horizontal section of terrain at a fixed RL — e.g., 102.0m contour. Spacing indicates steepness.

(ii) Fore Sight (FS): The staff reading taken on the last point observed from a level setup, just before the instrument is shifted. It indicates the point being left. In calculations FS is subtracted from Height of Instrument.

(iii) Axis of telescope (Line of collimation): The optical axis — line joining the optical centre of objective lens and the intersection of horizontal + vertical cross-hairs. It must be horizontal when bubble is centre; its collimation error is checked by two-peg test.

Contour: સરખા RL ને જોડતી કાલ્પનિક રેખા — datum થી સમાન ઊંચાઈ.

Fore Sight: setup બદલતા પહેલા છેલ્લું staff વાંચન — શિફ્ટ પહેલા point.

Telescope axis: objective કેન્દ્ર અને cross-hair intersection ને જોડતી રેખા — collimation line.

Q.2 (b) OR Explain characteristics of contours. (04 marks)Q.2 (b) OR Explain characteristics of contours. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ
  • Never cross, except overhanging cliff / cave where two elevations occupy same plan position. Otherwise contours are nested.
  • Closeness = steepness: Closely spaced → steep slope (e.g., 1m interval at 2mm plan = 1:50 slope). Widely spaced → flat/gentle.
  • Perpendicular to steepest slope: Water flows at right angle to contour. Ridge lines are convex outward, valley lines concave.
  • Closed loops: Hill — values increase inwards; pond — values decrease inwards. A loop never ends inside the sheet.
  • Equal interval in vertical, not horizontal: Contour interval (e.g., 1m) is constant; horizontal equivalent varies with slope.

1) cliff સિવાય ક્યારેય cross નહીં. 2) નજીક = steep, દૂર = flat. 3) steepest slope ને લંબ, પાણી contour ને કાટખૂણે વહે. 4) બંધ loop — ટેકરી અંદર વધે, તળાવમાં ઘટે. 5) vertical interval fixed, horizontal બદલે.

Q.3 (c) Explain errors in the compass survey and describe the closing error. (07 marks)Q.3 (c) Explain errors in the compass survey and describe the closing error. (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ

A) Instrumental errors: Pivot friction, bent needle, worn agate, eccentricity of graduations, prism misalignment. Remedy: Service, test on known bearing.

B) Personal errors: Inaccurate centering (1cm shift =0.5° in 10m), poor levelling (graduation touches glass), parallax in prism, reading without steady needle. Use plumb, level carefully.

C) Natural errors: Local attraction from iron pipes, fences, DC lines; diurnal magnetic variation (~10' per day), magnetic storms, declination not corrected. Avoid by stepping away from steel, apply declination.

Closing error: For closed traverse, sum of latitudes ΣL·cosθ and departures ΣL·sinθ should be zero. Practical sum is e = √(ΣLat²+ΣDep²) — the gap between start and end. Expressed as 1 in (Perimeter/e). Bowditch rule distributes correction ∝ length: CorrLat = −e_lat·(l/Σl); Transit rule ∝ |Lat| for angular dominance. If e within 1:500–1:1000 acceptable.

યંત્ર ભૂલ: pivot ઘર્ષણ, scale eccentricity. વ્યક્તિ: centering/levelling ખોટું. Natural: local attraction લોખંડ, diurnal variation. Closing error: બંધ traverse માં ΣLat ΣDep શૂન્ય હોવા જોઈએ, અંતર e=√(ΣLat²+ΣDep²), Bowditch થી length પ્રમાણે સુધારો.

Q.3 (b) OR Enlist instruments used for setting out right angles. explain each in details. (04 marks)Q.3 (b) OR Enlist instruments used for setting out right angles. explain each in details. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

1) Open cross-staff: Octagonal box with 4 slits at 90°, sight through one, second line perpendicular. Simple, ~0.5° accuracy.

2) French cross-staff: Adds 45° slits for 45° offset.

3) Optical square: Two mirrors at 45° reflect ray by 90° — observer sees direct and reflected images coincide when perpendicular.

4) Prism square: Uses pentaprism, more accurate, no parallax.

5) Site square / theodolite: Most accurate for long offsets, set 90° on horizontal circle.

Open cross-staff 4 slit 90°, optical square 45° mirror થી 90°, prism square, theodolite સૌથી ચોક્કસ.

Q.4 (b) List the fundamental axis of theodolite & give relation between them. (04 marks)Q.4 (b) List the fundamental axis of theodolite & give relation between them. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Five axes: 1) Vertical (azimuth) axis 2) Horizontal (trunnion) axis 3) Line of collimation (sight) axis 4) Plate level axis 5) Altitude bubble/stadia axis.

Q.4 (a) OR Define (i) Face right (ii) Vertical axis (iii) Swinging (03 marks)Q.4 (a) OR Define (i) Face right (ii) Vertical axis (iii) Swinging (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

(i) Face right: When vertical circle is on observer's right while sighting. Face right and face left readings differ by 180°; mean eliminates collimation error.

(ii) Vertical (azimuth) axis: The axis about which the whole instrument rotates in horizontal plane. Must be truly vertical after levelling.

(iii) Swinging (or swivelling): Rotating telescope about vertical axis in horizontal plane — e.g., swinging from backsight to foresight to measure angle.

Q.4 (c) OR Write Advantages and Disadvantages of Plain Table Survey. (07 marks)Q.4 (c) OR Write Advantages and Disadvantages of Plain Table Survey. (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ

Advantages (4–5 points for 3.5 marks): 1) Field is also office — no transcription error. 2) Omitted details seen on ground immediately. 3) No need for skilled chain/compass party — one table operator. 4) Cheapest for small fill-in, contour interpolation directly. 5) Suitable for magnetically disturbed areas (no compass needed if oriented by backsight).

Disadvantages: 1) Not usable in rain/wind — paper spoils. 2) Not for precise large surveys — alidade accuracy ~1:1000. 3) Replotting difficult if sheet damaged. 4) Heavy board + tripod to carry. 5) Requires calm, skilled orienting each setup.

ફાયદા: field જ office — ભૂલ ઓછી, છૂટી વિગત દેખાય, સસ્તું, magnetic disturbance માં ચાલે.

ગેરફાયદા: વરસાદ/પવનમાં નહીં, precise નહીં, sheet બગડે તો replotting અઘરું, ભારે.

Q5 · Orientation, adjustment and contouring

Q.5 (a) Explain the Orientation in Plain Table Survey (03 marks)Q.5 (a) Explain the Orientation in Plain Table Survey (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Orientation = keeping table at every station parallel to its position at first station. Then plotted rays remain parallel to ground lines. Without it, traversing will not close.

Three ways: (i) Magnetic compass — align trough compass north to sheet north. Quick but affected by local attraction. (ii) Backsighting — alidade on previous line drawn on sheet, sight back to previous station and rotate table till exact. Most accurate. (iii) Resection — after two known points plotted, table oriented by intersecting. Always orient before taking new rays.

Orientation: દરેક station પર ટેબલ પહેલાની જેમ સમાંતર રાખવું — નહિ તો traverse બંધ ન થાય. Compass થી અથવા back-sight થી (પાછળની રેખા પર alidade મૂકી પાછળ sight કરી table ફેરવો) — back-sight સૌથી ચોક્કસ.

Q.5 (b) Explain temporary adjustment of Theodolite. (04 marks)Q.5 (b) Explain temporary adjustment of Theodolite. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

1) Setting up & centering: Tripod over peg, plumb bob exact, legs firm.

2) Levelling: Turn plate until plate bubble parallel to two foot screws — turn them opposite equally until bubble centre; turn 90° and use third screw. Repeat until bubble centre in all positions → vertical axis vertical.

3) Focussing eyepiece: Turn eyepiece until cross-hairs sharp (no parallax).

4) Focussing objective: Direct to staff, turn focusing screw until image sharp and cross-hairs do not move when eye moves. Ready for angle measurement.

1) Plumb થી centering. 2) Plate bubble બે screw વિરુદ્ધ ફેરવી centre, 90° ફેરવી ત્રીજો screw. 3) Eyepiece focus cross-hair sharp. 4) Objective focus — parallax નહીં.

Q.5 (c) Explain the methods of locating contour lines in surveying. (07 marks)Q.5 (c) Explain the methods of locating contour lines in surveying. (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ

Direct method: Find and peg points of a required RL with level — radiate from a setup, staff-man moves until reading = HI − required RL. Trace closed loop. Accurate but slow, used for small areas, highways.

Indirect methods:

  • Grid (squares) method: Divide area into 10×10m squares, take RL of each grid corner with level, interpolate contour between corners. Best for flat civil sites.
  • Cross-section method: Run centre line, take levels at regular intervals and perpendicular offsets — ideal for roads/canals.
  • Tacheometric method: Use tacheometer to get distance + RL rapidly for hilly large areas.

Interpolation: Arithmetic (proportional calculation), graphical (trace paper), or estimation between spot heights to draw contour.

Direct: જરૂરી RL ના points level થી શોધી trace — slow.

Indirect: Grid 10×10m દરેક corner RL લઈ interpolate; Cross-section રસ્તા માટે; Tacheometric ડુંગરમાં ઝડપી. Arithmetic/graphical થી contour દોરો.

Q.5 (a) OR What are the objectives and uses of surveying? (03 marks)Q.5 (a) OR What are the objectives and uses of surveying? (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Objectives: (i) Determine relative positions of points on/above/below earth surface (distance, direction, elevation). (ii) Prepare plans/maps to scale. (iii) Compute areas, volumes, set out works.

Uses: Design roads, railways, dams, tunnels; fix property boundaries; estimate earthwork; prepare GIS, town planning, navigation charts.

ઉદ્દેશ: બિંદુઓની સાપેક્ષ સ્થિતિ નક્કી, નકશો બનાવવો, area/volume કાઢવા.

ઉપયોગ: રસ્તા ડેમ ટનલ design, હદ નક્કી, earthwork estimate, town planning.

Q.5 (b) OR Explain: Bowditch rule and Transit rule. (04 marks)Q.5 (b) OR Explain: Bowditch rule and Transit rule. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Bowditch (compass) rule — assumes errors proportional to length: Correction in latitude = −(ΣLat error) × (length of line / perimeter). Same for departure. Used when linear and angular errors both present (most compass traverses). Also called compass rule.

Transit rule — assumes angular error dominates: Correction in latitude = −(ΣLat error) × (|Latitude| / Σ|Latitude|). Gives more correction to lines with larger latitude. Used for theodolite traverses where angles are precise. Bowditch is more popular.

After correction, ΣLat =0 and ΣDep =0, traverse closes.

Bowditch: સુધારો length પ્રમાણે — Corr = −error × l/Σl. Compass traverse માટે.

Transit: સુધારો |Lat| પ્રમાણે — angular error વધારે હોય ત્યારે theodolite માટે.
