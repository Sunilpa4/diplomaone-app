Q.1 (b) Explain vertical curves on road. (04 marks)Q.1 (b) Explain vertical curves on road. (04 marks)04 marks૦૪ ગુણ
Concept: Vertical curves smooth the grade changes in road profile. Without them vehicle experiences jerk, sight is blocked on summit, headlight sight lost in valley. Provided as simple parabola y = ax².
TypeWhere grades meetShape & Sight governingIRC Length
Summit / Crest+ve to −ve, + to + steep, − to −Convex upward, visibility over crest = SSD limited. Length L = N·S² / 2·H where N = grade differenceGoverned by SSD. At 80 km/h SSD 120m → L ≈ 0.5·N·SSD approx. Example N=4% → L 200+ m
Valley / Sag−ve to +ve, dipConcave, headlight sight at night governs + comfort centrifugal 0.3 m/s² limit. L = N·V³ / C where C comfort factorGoverned by comfort + night sight + drainage. Need sag curve to prevent water ponding at low point

Provision: Summit on hill tops and over crest level crossings, sag in valley floor, underpasses, bridge approaches. Gradient compensation on curves applies to vertical too. Parabola chosen because rate of change of grade constant → smooth driving.

ખ્યાલ: ઢાળ બદલાય ત્યાં વાહન આંચકા વગર જાય, દૃષ્ટિ અવરોધે નહીં એ માટે પેરાબોલા y=ax² નો વળાંક.
પ્રકારક્યાંનિયંત્રણ
Summit શિખર convex+ થી − ઢાળSSD થી લંબાઈ — ૮૦ km/h SSD ૧૨૦m તો L ≈ N·S²/2H
Valley ખીણ concave− થી + ઢાળરાત્રે headlight દૃષ્ટિ + comfort 0.3 m/s²

પેરાબોલા કારણ કે grade બદલાવ સમાન. શિખર પર headlight નહીં પણ દિવસ દૃષ્ટિ, ખીણમાં રાત્રી દૃષ્ટિ.

Q.1 (c) Explain various mode of transportation. (07 marks)Q.1 (c) Explain various mode of transportation. (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ
Given: Need to move people/goods. Question asks for four modes — Road, Rail, Water, Air. Each has infrastructure, vehicle, operation.
Concept: Choice depends on distance, bulk, time, cost, geography. No single mode is best — they chain ship→rail→truck.
ModeInfrastructure + VehicleAdvantagesLimitations / Best for

Q.3 (a) What is flash point and fire point of bitumen? Which instrument use to find it. (03 marks)Q.3 (a) What is flash point and fire point of bitumen? Which instrument use to find it. (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ
TermDefinitionTypical value
Flash pointLowest temperature at which bitumen vapours momentarily flash when small flame passed, vapours ignite but not sustain≥175°C for paving bitumen VG-30. Safety limit heating must stay 20°C below flash
Fire pointTemperature 5–10°C above flash where vapour burns continuously for 5 s. Fire point = flash + 5 to 10°C≈180–200°C. Indicates fire hazard during heating

Instrument: Pensky-Martens Closed Cup or Cleveland Open Cup open cup more common for bitumen as per IS 1209. Closed cup for cutback/emulsion. Heating rate 5–6°C/min, stir, apply flame at 5°C interval.

Q.4 (a) Explain importance of highway drainage. (03 marks)Q.4 (a) Explain importance of highway drainage. (03 marks)03 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Water is road's first enemy: Standing water hydroplanes, strips bitumen, softens subgrade CBR to 30%, pumps fines, erodes slopes. Q.5 (c) OR Enlist and explain elements covered in terminal building. (07 marks)Q.5 (c) OR Enlist and explain elements covered in terminal building. (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ
Concept: Terminal is landside-airside interface — public meets aircraft only inside it. Two flows: passengers & baggage never block each other, segregated departures/arrivals.
ZoneElementsFunction
Public landsideForecourt, parking, portico, enquiry, ticket countersArrival by road, vehicle circulation

Airside concourseDeparture lounges, boarding gates, aerobridges, bus lounges, VIP loungesHold passengers by flight, board via bridge/bus
ArrivalsBaggage reclaim belts, customs green/red for international, immigration countersDeliver pax/bags, border control

Design driven by peak-hour passengers — area norms 14–20 m² per peak hr pax international.

Answer: Terminal = forecourt → check-in/security → departure lounges/gates → aerobridge → baggage reclaim/customs → cargo/APRON services. Landside and airside meet only inside.
