Q1 · Basics, force and resultant

Q.1 (a) Define Engineering Mechanics and give its classification. (3 marks)Q.1 (a) Define Engineering Mechanics and give its classification. (3 marks)3 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Engineering Mechanics is science of forces on bodies at rest or motion — predicts stability and motion for design.

  • Statics: Bodies at rest — equilibrium ΣF=0 ΣM=0 — beams, trusses.
  • Dynamics: Bodies in motion — Kinematics motion without forces and Kinetics forces cause motion — vehicles, engines.
  • Further: Rigid vs deformable, fluid, strength of materials — mechanics is base for all design.

એન્જિનિયરિંગ મિકેનિક્સ એટલે બળો ની અસર નું વિજ્ઞાન — સ્થિર કે ગતિ માટે ડિઝાઇન આગાહી.

  • Statics: સ્થિર શરીર ΣF=0 ΣM=0 — beam truss.
  • Dynamics: ગતિ — Kinematics ગતિ માત્ર Kinetics બળ થી ગતિ — વાહન એન્જિન.
  • આગળ rigid deformable fluid — base.
Q.1 (b) Define force and give characteristics of force. (4 marks)Q.1 (b) Define force and give characteristics of force. (4 marks)4 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Force is push or pull that changes or tends to change state of rest or motion — vector.

  • Magnitude: value in N or kN, 1kN=1000N.
  • Direction: angle with reference axis.
  • Point of application: where force touches body.
  • Line of action: infinite line along force.
  • Sense and nature: tensile away, compressive towards.

Transmissibility: force can slide on its line without effect on rigid body.

બળ એટલે ધક્કો ખેંચાણ જે સ્થિતિ બદલે — સદિશ.

  • મૂલ્ય: N kN 1kN=1000N.
  • દિશા: axis સાથે ખૂણો.
  • લાગુ બિંદુ: જ્યાં સ્પર્શે.
  • ક્રિયા રેખા: બળ ની સીધી રેખા.
  • ખેંચાણ દાબ: દૂર તરફ tensile નજીક compressive.

Q2 · Resolution, Varignon and equilibrium

Q.2 (a) Explain resolution of a force. (3 marks)Q.2 (a) Explain resolution of a force. (3 marks)3 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Resolution is splitting F into components along axes — opposite of resultant. For rectangular: Fx = F cosθ Fy = F sinθ where θ with X. For non perpendicular parallelogram law: F1 = F sinα / sin(θ) etc.

Use to find ΣFx ΣFy then R. For space 3D cos α β γ.

Resolution એટલે F ને axis પર છૂટા પાડવા — result નું ઊલટું. કાટખૂણે Fx = F cosθ Fy = F sinθ. ત્રાંસા માટે parallelogram.

ΣFx ΣFy થી R મળે.

Q.2 (b) State and explain Varignon’s Principal. (4 marks)Q.2 (b) State and explain Varignon’s Principal. (4 marks)4 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Varignon's theorem: Moment of resultant about any point equals sum of moments of its components about that point.

Proof: R = F1+F2 vector → r×R = r×F1 + r×F2. So MoR = Mo1+Mo2.

Use: Find moment of inclined load by resolving to horizontal vertical then sum — easier than perpendicular distance for slanted force. For distributed load moment = area moment.

Varignon: કોઈ બિંદુ એ પરિણામી નો moment = ઘટક ના moments નો સરવાળો.

R = F1+F2 → r×R = r×F1+r×F2. ત્રાંસા બળ નો moment ઘટક પાડી શોધવો સહેલો.

Q.2 (a) OR State and explain Principal of Transmissibility of forces. (3 marks)Q.2 (a) OR State and explain Principal of Transmissibility of forces. (3 marks)3 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Principle: Force acting at one point on rigid body can be moved to any point on its line of action without changing external effects — translations and rotations same. Internal deformation may change but rigid motion same. Proof via adding equal opposite forces on line.

Use: Move force along line to convenient point for moment calculation.

નિયમ: Rigid શરીર પર બિંદુ એ લાગતું બળ તેની ક્રિયા રેખા પર ક્યાંય ખસેડો બાહ્ય અસર એ જ રહે. internal બદલાય પણ ગતિ એ જ.

Q.3 (b) Differentiate between axis of reference and axis of symmetry. (4 marks)Q.3 (b) Differentiate between axis of reference and axis of symmetry. (4 marks)4 marks૦૪ ગુણ

Axis of reference: Any chosen coordinate X or Y for measurements — moments taken about it, not necessarily centroidal.

Axis of symmetry: Line that divides shape into mirror halves — area above equals below mirrored. All symmetry axes are centroidal, CG lies on them. Reference may be outside shape.

Example: rectangle symmetry both central axes, reference at bottom for ȳ measurement.

Reference axis: માપ માટે ગમે તે X Y — moment તેના પર.

Symmetry axis: આકૃતિ ને અરીસા બે ભાગ — બધા symmetry centroidal હોય CG તેના પર. reference બહાર પણ હોઈ શકે.

Law of machine relates effort P and load W linearly: P = mW + C. m =tanθ slope reciprocal of efficiency at large load, C = friction intercept effort at no load. Determined by testing machine at different W measure P plot straight line.

From law ideal effort Pi = mW, friction Pf = C, mechanical advantage changes with load.

યંત્ર નો નિયમ P = mW + C સીધી રેખા — m ઢાળ, C નો લોડે friction. જુદા W પર P માપી plot કરી મળે.

Q.5 (a) OR Define: (1) Velocity Ratio, (2) Mechanical Advantage, (3) Ideal Machine (3 marks)Q.5 (a) OR Define: (1) Velocity Ratio, (2) Mechanical Advantage, (3) Ideal Machine (3 marks)3 marks૦૩ ગુણ

Velocity ratio VR: Ratio distance moved by effort to distance moved by load per unit time — Dp/Dw — constant for machine geometry.

Mechanical advantage MA: Load lifted divided by effort applied — W/P — varies with friction.

Ideal machine: Frictionless η=100% so MA = VR — effort only overcomes load, no losses.

VR: effort અંતર / load અંતર — geometry સ્થિર.

MA: W/P.

Ideal: friction શૂન્ય η=100% MA=VR.
