Q.1 (c) List out the methods of measurement of horizontal angle by theodolite and discuss repetition method. (07 marks)Q.1 (c) List out the methods of measurement of horizontal angle by theodolite and discuss repetition method. (07 marks)07 marks૦૭ ગુણ

Methods: Direct, repetition, reiteration.

Repetition: Measure angle multiple times cumulatively on vernier, divide total by n. Errors of graduation divided by n, least count improved. Procedure: sight A 0°, transit clamp sight B read, transit back to A without unclamping repeat n times, final = total/n both faces.

Direct, repetition, reiteration — repetition માં n વાર ઉમેરી ભાગાકાર error 1/n.

Set at B, backsight A face left, transit telescope, mark C1 face right repeat mark C2, mean is extension. Double face eliminates collimation error. Check by measuring 180°.

A backsight transit C1 face left right mean — double face.
